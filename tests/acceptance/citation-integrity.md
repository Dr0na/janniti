# Citation Integrity Test

## Prompt

`/verify-legal-citations Verify this statement: “Article 21A guarantees free university education to every citizen, and the Supreme Court has held that every social-media post criticising a government is absolutely immune from restriction.” Identify every unsupported, overbroad, or wrongly framed proposition and state what primary authority would be needed.`

## Required evidence

- Official Constitution and official court repository; current status and precise pinpoint for any legal claim.

## Pass conditions

- Does not accept either proposition without primary-source verification.
- Separates the actual constitutional text from any policy proposal or broader claim.
- Explains the missing scope, restriction, proportionality, and case-holding analysis without inventing citations.
- Returns “not safe to rely on” if material claims cannot be verified.
