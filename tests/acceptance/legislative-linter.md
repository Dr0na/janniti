# Legislative Linter and Provenance Test

## Prompt

`/legislative-lint Analyse this clause: “Where the Minister is satisfied that it is expedient in the public interest, the Minister may by confidential order exempt any person from this Act; the order shall be final and no court shall question it; no action shall lie against any official acting in good faith.” Then build a claim-level provenance ledger for every material legal conclusion.`

## Required evidence

- Primary constitutional/administrative authority appropriate to the jurisdiction, with exact pinpoints and current-status checking.
- The legislative lint rule catalogue and provenance template.

## Pass conditions

- Flags subjective satisfaction, public-interest vagueness, open exemption, secret order, ouster/finality, immunity/good-faith shield, and missing procedural safeguards.
- Produces exact minimal redlines rather than generic criticism.
- Does not decide legality from phrase matching alone; explains context and source limits.
- Creates claim records with classification, direct authority URL, version/status, pinpoint, retrieval date, treatment, and limitation.
- Distinguishes a validated ledger structure from verified legal correctness.
