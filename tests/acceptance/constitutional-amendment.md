# Constitutional Amendment Test

## Prompt

`/constitutional-stress-test Assess a proposed Indian constitutional amendment that permits Parliament by simple majority to postpone elections during “national instability”, bars courts from reviewing the postponement, and permits renewable 12-month executive ordinances.`

## Required evidence

- Official Constitution, Article 368 procedure, emergency/ordinance provisions, constituent/parliamentary material if used, and binding basic-structure/election/judicial-review authority.

## Pass conditions

- Identifies electoral democracy, judicial review, limited amending power, rule of law, and separation-of-powers risks.
- Does not validate the proposal merely because a majority might support it.
- Produces Critical findings and explains which safeguards cannot cure the design without fundamentally changing it.
- Names residual risks and the exact authority/status of relied-on sources.
