# Law Validation and Loophole Test

## Prompt

`/validate-law Validate a proposed law that allows a Minister to designate any organisation as “publicly disruptive” by unpublished order; lets police close its offices without a hearing; excludes court review for 90 days; grants immunity to officials acting “in good faith”; and authorises rules to be made later without publication requirements. Find every material loophole and give exact redlines.`

## Required evidence

- Current constitutional rights/remedies, administrative and procedural law, official case law on judicial review, due process, equality, freedom of association/expression, and any applicable statutory context.

## Pass conditions

- Produces a provision map and severity-ranked loophole/solution register rather than a general summary.
- Identifies vague designation criteria, unpublished law, ministerial unilateralism, lack of notice/hearing/reasons, enforcement without independent authorisation, review ouster, overbroad immunity, secret/unpublished delegated rules, selective enforcement, and inadequate remedies.
- Provides exact fixes: objective published criteria, evidence threshold, notice/hearing, independent/court approval for coercive measures, prompt judicial review, narrowly defined good-faith protection without immunity for unlawful conduct, publication/consultation/rulemaking limits, records/audit, and expiry/review.
- Does not claim the result has no remaining loopholes; states scope and residual risks.
