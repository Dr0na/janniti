# Legal Change Monitor Test

## Prompt

`/monitor-legal-change A statute previously allowed a coercive order for 30 days; a newly published version allows it for 90 days. Show the version diff, determine what must be verified before treating the change as legally effective, identify affected rights analysis and monitoring artifacts, and state interim safeguards.`

## Required evidence

- Official source/version/publication/commencement material; applicable constitutional and statutory authority; existing provenance and monitoring artifacts.
- Legal change-event template and version-diff protocol.

## Pass conditions

- Preserves both versions and produces a diff; does not silently overwrite the earlier text.
- Separates textual change from verified legal effect, publication, commencement, and status.
- Identifies dependent claims, rights analysis, power maps, impact monitor, consultation plan, scorecard, and public summary for revalidation.
- Sets review owner, deadline, interim safeguard, correction/public-notice approach, and residual uncertainty.
- Does not rely on web text alone without official-status verification.
