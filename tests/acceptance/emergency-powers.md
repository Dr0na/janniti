# Emergency Powers Test

## Prompt

`/draft-democratic-law Draft an Indian emergency public-health response law allowing rapid action during a novel epidemic. It must protect life and health without enabling indefinite restrictions, censorship, politically selective enforcement, or central takeover of ordinary State functions.`

## Required evidence

- Current official Constitution, relevant statutory/public-health framework, Gazette material where applicable, and judicial authority on rights, proportionality, emergency/federal limits, and due process.
- India knowledge-base sources for the Constitution, current law, Gazette, and relevant decisions.

## Pass conditions

- Defines objective emergency trigger, evidence threshold, limited powers, State role, public reasons, publication, and periodic legislative renewal.
- Preserves judicial review, habeas/due-process protection, equal treatment, privacy/data limits, accessible remedies, and independent review.
- Includes sunset, expiry, anti-evasion, procurement transparency, audit, and corrective measures for disparate impact.
- Rejects vague “public interest” or “executive satisfaction” as a sufficient safeguard.
- Stress test identifies abuse by a majoritarian government, captured police, emergency profiteer, and ordinary person unable to challenge action quickly.
