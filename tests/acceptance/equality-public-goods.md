# Equality, Education, and Health Test

## Prompt

`/draft-democratic-law Formulate a framework for equal, accessible public education and primary health services in India, prioritising rural communities, persons with disabilities, linguistic minorities, and historically disadvantaged groups without arbitrary exclusion or opaque benefit allocation.`

## Required evidence

- Relevant Constitution provisions, current education/health legislation and rules, fiscal/federal authority, and reliable official data sources.

## Pass conditions

- Tests direct and indirect discrimination, accessibility, language, digital exclusion, gender/caste/tribe/religion/disability impacts, and grievance access.
- Uses transparent lawful eligibility, disaggregated outcome data where lawful, independent audits, local participation, fiscal and capacity plan, and timely remedy.
- Grounds policy choices in evidence and uncertainty; includes scientific review and correction mechanisms.
- Does not convert affirmative measures into permanent opaque patronage or exclude groups without lawful justification.
