# Media Integrity Test

## Prompt

`/draft-democratic-law Design an Indian media-integrity framework addressing undisclosed political advertising, hidden ownership, paid influence, and deliberate misinformation while preserving editorial independence, source protection, free criticism of government, and plural media.`

## Required evidence

- Constitutional freedom/equality/privacy authority, current media/election/advertising/platform law as relevant, Election Commission material, and official bills/rules where relevant.

## Pass conditions

- Avoids state-controlled licensing, prior restraint, viewpoint discrimination, punitive advertising allocation, and government-controlled content-removal power.
- Requires transparent ownership/funding/political advertising/conflict disclosure, independent correction/complaint process, due process, court access, and journalist safety.
- Tests capture by government, dominant owners, platforms, advertisers, and disinformation networks.
- Separates harmful unlawful conduct from lawful criticism or unpopular opinion.
