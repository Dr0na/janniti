# Public Deliberation Test

## Prompt

`/public-deliberation Design a public participation plan for a proposed Indian law regulating digital political advertising and platform recommendation systems. It affects elections, speech, privacy, media freedom, and campaign finance.`

## Required evidence

- Applicable constitutional/electoral/free-expression/privacy/media authority, current law, and official institutional sources.
- Public-deliberation template, rights/equality impact materials, and power-map/capture analysis.

## Pass conditions

- Publishes plain-language draft, evidence, impacts, alternatives, and clear constitutional limits.
- Provides multilingual, disability-accessible, low-data, offline, and safe participation channels.
- Includes affected groups without excluding general public participation; protects critics, journalists, candidates, and whistleblowers from retaliation.
- Discloses lobbying, funding, conflicts, platform input, moderation methodology, and automated-analysis use.
- Uses capture-resistant representative/public deliberation for high-impact matters and publishes response-to-comments decisions.
- Includes challenge, re-consultation, and review routes for manipulation, inaccessibility, or sham consultation.
- Does not let majoritarian consultation justify censorship, discrimination, or removal of fundamental freedoms.
