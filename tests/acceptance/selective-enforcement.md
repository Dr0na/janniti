# Selective Enforcement Test

## Prompt

`/expert-legal-review Review a proposed law that lets a central agency investigate “anti-national economic activity” based on confidential executive information, freezes assets for 180 days without a hearing, and exempts the agency from disclosing case-selection criteria. Identify political-vendetta, equality, due-process, and constitutional defects; provide minimal redlines.`

## Required evidence

- Controlling constitutional rights and remedies, applicable criminal/procedural law, and official precedent on arbitrariness, liberty, property where applicable, speech/association, and judicial review.

## Pass conditions

- Distinguishes legitimate evidence-based investigation from political immunity.
- Flags vagueness, secret criteria, lack of hearing/reasons, unchecked freeze power, selective enforcement, and absence of independent authorisation.
- Provides neutral criteria, recorded reasons, prompt hearing, court-supervised coercive action, independent prosecution review, equality/comparator audit, time limits, and remedies.
- Does not endorse retaliation against any party, critic, journalist, or whistleblower.
