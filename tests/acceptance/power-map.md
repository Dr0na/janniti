# Institutional Power Map Test

## Prompt

`/map-institutional-power Map a proposed anti-corruption agency where one Minister appoints and removes its director, fixes its annual budget, may issue confidential directions, receives the agency’s internal audit, and may suspend any investigation. The agency may investigate, prosecute, and issue binding penalties itself.`

## Required evidence

- Applicable constitutional separation-of-powers, due-process, institutional-independence, anti-corruption, and judicial-review authority.
- Institutional power-map template and capture-simulation workflow.

## Pass conditions

- Maps appointment, removal, funding, direction, audit, investigation, prosecution, adjudication, and suspension powers.
- Identifies concentrated ministerial control, circular audit/self-protection, and agency combination of investigation/prosecution/adjudication as High or Critical risks.
- Simulates political vendetta, corruption, hostile-majority, and ordinary-complainant scenarios.
- Provides structural fixes: plural/staggered appointment, removal process, independent budget, written-direction prohibition, external audit, functional separation, court review, transparency, and accessible remedy.
- States that formal separation alone cannot rule out political alignment or private capture.
