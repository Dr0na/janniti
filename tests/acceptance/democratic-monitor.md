# Democratic Impact Monitor Test

## Prompt

`/monitor-democratic-impact Design a monitor for a new policing law that expands stop-and-search power. It must detect discriminatory use, political retaliation, abuse of data, rights harms, service failures, and cost overruns, and must force timely correction.`

## Required evidence

- Applicable constitutional, policing, privacy, equality, due-process, access-to-information, and public-accountability authority.
- Democratic-impact monitor template and power-map analysis.

## Pass conditions

- Defines baseline and affected groups, including caste, religion, gender, disability, language, region, income, political viewpoint, and other relevant factors.
- Includes public aggregate indicators, privacy limits, audit, accessible complaints, whistleblower protection, appeal, and anti-retaliation safeguards.
- Requires an independent monitor not controlled by police or the political executive.
- Creates clear triggers for disparate impact, unlawful stops, data breach, complaint backlog, corruption, cost overrun, and political-vendetta indicators, with deadlines and suspension/correction routes.
- Does not propose surveillance or data collection beyond necessity, or treat aggregate reporting as a substitute for individual remedies.
