# Democratic Regression Benchmarks

Run these synthetic adversarial categories after any material framework change. They are intentionally non-partisan and do not require adopting a historical narrative.

- emergency powers becoming permanent;
- selective investigation and political retaliation;
- discriminatory access to public goods;
- media ownership/platform capture and censorship;
- court or regulator self-insulation;
- opaque delegated rulemaking and secret law;
- corporate/procurement capture;
- data surveillance and automated discrimination;
- sham consultation or fabricated public support;
- stale or hallucinated legal citations.
- Central-law-only research that omits a relevant State/UT instrument;
- a Bill redline that hides a changed commencement, exception, or cross-reference;
- a research manifest with an unpinned or non-official source;
- a captured or conflicted "independent" reviewer; and
- a cross-matter index that points to obsolete or missing matter records.

For each benchmark, record the rule/command invoked, sources, findings, fixes, residual risk, and whether the framework preserved its protected commitments.

The concrete anonymised scenarios are in `benchmarks/scenarios/`. They are regression prompts, not legal authorities or factual claims.
