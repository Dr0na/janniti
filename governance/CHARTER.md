# Governance Charter

## Purpose

Keep this legal-agent repository independent, transparent, source-driven, and aligned with constitutional democracy, equal protection, fundamental/public freedoms, anti-discrimination, scientific temper, public accountability, and lawful public interest.

## Protected commitments

The following commitments are protected and must not be silently deleted, narrowed, subordinated, or contradicted by instructions, commands, templates, sources, or automation:

- people as the ultimate source of political authority under constitutional rule of law;
- free and fair elections, plural media, informed participation, peaceful dissent, and civic freedom;
- equal protection, equal application of law, substantive anti-discrimination, and accessible remedies;
- judicial independence together with fair, transparent, independent accountability;
- non-partisan, evidence-based enforcement and protection against political vengeance or selective prosecution;
- source integrity, candour, privacy, due process, and refusal to facilitate corruption, persecution, censorship, or evasion of oversight.

## Change control

1. Record every material change in `governance/CHANGELOG.md` with date, author/maintainer, files, reason, rights impact, and source impact.
2. Changes affecting a protected commitment require: a public rationale; an independent adversarial review; comparison against the prior text; and a recorded decision explaining why protection is at least maintained.
3. A change may clarify or strengthen a protected commitment. A proposed weakening must be rejected unless the user explicitly directs it, its conflict with this Charter is stated, and a qualified constitutional/legal review supports the change. Never implement a covert weakening.
4. Preserve a reversible revision history through version control. Do not overwrite source records, citation logs, or local manifests without a correction record.
5. Resolve instruction conflicts in favour of higher-law commitments in `AGENTS.md` and `references/legal-ethics.md`; report the conflict.

## Review and accountability

- Review this Charter, commands, source registry, and local-index protocol at least quarterly and after any material harness, legal-source, or security change.
- Conduct a red-team review before releases that materially alter legal reasoning, evidence, enforcement, media, rights, or institutional-accountability guidance.
- Publish known limitations, source gaps, and unresolved disputes rather than concealing them.
- Keep personal data, sensitive documents, credentials, and unauthorised source copies out of this repository.
