# /validate-law

Validate the supplied law, bill, rule, regulation, policy, judgment framework, or institutional design. Find legal, constitutional, procedural, enforcement, accountability, equality, rights, implementation, and anti-circumvention loopholes; provide a precise solution for every material confirmed gap. If the text or jurisdiction is missing, request it before concluding.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `references/legal-ethics.md`, `references/review-checklist.md`, `commands/constitutional-stress-test.md`, and `commands/verify-legal-citations.md`. For India, also read `references/india-constitutional-research.md` and the applicable primary-source collections in `knowledge-base/sources.yaml`.

## Validation method

1. **Establish the legal baseline:** jurisdiction, date/version, legal status, competent authority, related laws, delegated rules, case law, and transition/commencement.
2. **Model the instrument:** objective, definitions, beneficiaries/burdened persons, powers, duties, exceptions, discretion, decision-makers, evidence, records, enforcement, appeal, remedies, funding, rulemaking, and expiry.
3. **Test every provision against the loophole catalogue:**
   - vague terms, undefined thresholds, subjective satisfaction, and conflicting provisions;
   - overbroad exceptions, exemptions, immunity, ouster/finality clauses, and unreviewable discretion;
   - indirect evasion through delegation, outsourcing, technology, secrecy, funding, emergency powers, or serial temporary action;
   - gaps in competence, constitutional rights, equality, due process, privacy, federalism, jurisdiction, standing, limitation periods, and remedies;
   - selective enforcement, political vengeance, discrimination, retaliatory audit/investigation, and unequal access;
   - captured appointments, conflicts, self-investigation, opaque allocation, budget dependence, corruption, and weak audit;
   - missing evidence threshold, notice, hearing, written reasons, disclosure, data safeguards, independent authorisation, appeal, and effective correction;
   - implementation failure: impossible timelines, unfunded duties, inaccessible procedure, language/disability/digital barriers, missing metrics, and no review/sunset;
   - media, information, and public-participation failures; and
   - obsolete cross-references, repeal/amendment conflict, unintended criminalisation, or perverse incentives.
4. **Stress test:** apply hostile-majority, captured-agency, corrupt-official, emergency, powerful-private-actor, platform/media, and ordinary-person scenarios.
5. **Fix by design:** pair every confirmed loophole with the smallest effective redline or operational safeguard. Do not solve a gap by giving an unchecked new power to another body.
6. **Verify citations and current status** before finalising.

## Required deliverable

1. **Validation conclusion:** legally/constitutionally robust, robust with conditions, materially vulnerable, critically vulnerable, or insufficient material to validate.
2. **Scope and source record:** text/version reviewed, jurisdiction/date, primary sources, material gaps in evidence, and authorities not checked.
3. **Provision map:** provision, purpose, power holder, affected persons, checker, record, challenge, remedy, and expiry.
4. **Loophole and solution register:**

| ID | Severity | Provision/issue | Exploitation path | Harm | Legal basis | Exact fix/redline | Owner and verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

5. **Rights, equality, anti-vendetta, and public-freedom assessment.**
6. **Stress-test matrix** and single-point-of-failure register.
7. **Implementation fixes:** resources, training, data, audit, public reporting, transition, and review/sunset.
8. **Residual risks and limits:** explicitly state what has not been validated and why.

Use these severity levels: **Critical** (durable authoritarian/unreviewable power or irreparable rights loss); **High** (serious selective repression, corruption, or rights deprivation); **Medium** (plausible abuse, accountability, or implementation failure); **Low** (correctable clarity or operational gap). A lack of discovered defects is not proof that no loophole exists.
