# /public-legal-accessibility

Create an accessible public legal communication pack for the supplied proposal. Read `accessibility/README.md`, the consultation plan, and the rights/equality assessment. Store the pack in the matter's `11-public-legal-accessibility/` folder.

Deliver: plain-language summary; purpose/non-purpose; practical rights and remedies; power/check map; alternatives and risks; consultation questions; implementation timeline; relevant language plan; screen-reader/easy-read/print/low-data formats; and user-comprehension test plan. Preserve material uncertainty and dissent; do not turn accessibility into propaganda.
