# Expert Escalation Brief: Constitutional & Technical Legal Issues

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/007-national-universal-free-healthcare-and-medical-integrity-bill`  

---

## High-Risk Legal Issues Submitted for Senior Constitutional Counsel Review

1. **Constitutional Entrenchment Mechanism (Article 21B Amendment)**:
   - *Issue*: Can Parliament bind future Parliaments under ordinary statutory law, or is insertion of Article 21B under Article 368 mandatory to render the right to free health care un-repealable by a simple majority?
   - *Recommendation*: Propose both the framework statute and the companion Constitutional (139th Amendment) Bill to achieve immutable Part III status.
2. **Private Price Regulation & Fundamental Freedom (Article 19(1)(g))**:
   - *Issue*: Does price-capping private healthcare establishments violate the right to carry on trade/profession?
   - *Recommendation*: Grounded in *Modern Dental College* (2016) 7 SCC 353, price regulation constitutes a reasonable restriction under Article 19(6) in the public interest of healthcare access.
3. **AI Clinical Audit & Medical Professional Negligence**:
   - *Issue*: How to balance automated AI prescription flags with clinical discretion and natural justice?
   - *Recommendation*: Establish double-blind AI processing where AI acts strictly as an advisory flagging tool for human Independent Medical Review Boards (IMRC) with mandatory hearing rights before disciplinary or penal action.
