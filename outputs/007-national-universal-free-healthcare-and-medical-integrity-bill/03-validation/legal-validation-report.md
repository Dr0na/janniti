## 1. Statutory Loophole & Risk Audit

| Tested Risk / Loophole Scenario | Vulnerability Assessment | Statutory Fix / Mitigation in Bill |
|---|---|---|
| **Off-Label Private Overcharging** | Private facilities might bundle unlisted consumables to circumvent tariff caps. | Clause 4(4) and Clause 6 prohibit balance billing; mandatory transparent cost-plus auditing by National Healthcare Tariff Authority (NHTA). |
| **Coercive Pharma Inducements** | Drug companies might bypass kickback bans using indirect shell entity consulting fees. | Clause 9 broadly defines inducements to cover direct/indirect financial benefits, sponsorships, and research grants; punishable under Clause 17. |
| **AI Prescription False Positives** | AI audit system might penalize doctors for rare, legitimate clinical choices. | Clause 11(3) and Clause 12 mandate double-blind referral to human Independent Medical Review Boards with mandatory right of hearing. |
| **Unauthorized Data Access** | EHR records could be commercialized or accessed by health insurers to deny claims. | Clause 13(4) strictly prohibits insurance and employer access; criminalizes data breaches under DPDP Act 2023 and Information Technology Act. |
| **Public Facility Budget Starvation** | State governments might underfund public healthcare facilities after enactment. | Clause 14 & 15 establish the National Universal Health Trust Fund financed by a non-lapsable health cess and mandatory 5% budget earmark. |
