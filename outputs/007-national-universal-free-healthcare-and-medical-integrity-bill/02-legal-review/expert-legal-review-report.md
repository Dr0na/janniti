## 1. Summary of Findings

The *National Universal Free Healthcare and Medical Integrity Bill, 2026* is legally sound, constitutionally compliant, and structurally robust. The Bill successfully operationalizes the fundamental right to health under Article 21 of the Constitution of India and advances the Directive Principles of State Policy under Article 47.

## 2. Specific Clause Analysis & Recommendations

1. **Clause 3 (Right to Free Care)**: Completely removes financial barriers to public care. Recommended ensuring non-resident emergency care coverage to prevent arbitrary exclusion.
2. **Clause 4 & 6 (Private Tariff Caps)**: Valid under reasonable restriction jurisprudence (Article 19(6)), satisfying public interest objectives (*Modern Dental College v. State of M.P.* (2016) 7 SCC 353).
3. **Clause 11 & 12 (AI Clinical Audit)**: Ensures algorithmic transparency and human-in-the-loop review to prevent automated medical malpractice liability errors.
4. **Clause 16 (Anti-Repeal Entrenchment)**: Companion Constitutional 139th Amendment (Article 21B) provides effective entrenchment against ordinary statutory repeal.
