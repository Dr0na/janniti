## Statutory Source & Version Monitoring

1. **Constitutional Amendment Tracking**: Monitor progress of the draft *Constitution (One Hundred and Thirty-Ninth Amendment) Bill* (Article 21B) across Parliament and State Assembly ratifications.
2. **Supreme Court Precedent Tracking**: Track Supreme Court and High Court jurisprudence on Article 21, private hospital price regulation, and clinical audit due process.
3. **Statutory & Regulatory Snapshotting**: Run `scripts/snapshot_legal_source.py` on official gazette notifications issued by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, National Medical Commission, and CDSCO to ensure hash integrity and prevent unauthorized dilution.
