## Power Distribution Matrix

| Institutional Body | Core Function | Checks & Balances | Appellate / Judicial Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| **National Universal Health Authority (NUHA)** | National healthcare administration, facility density compliance, medicine supply chain. | Multi-stakeholder Board; Parliamentary audit; central fund disbursements. | High Courts / Supreme Court judicial review. |
| **National Healthcare Tariff Authority (NHTA)** | Cost-plus pricing determination for private healthcare establishments. | Annual transparent public cost hearings; judicial oversight panel. | Appellate Tribunal & High Court writ jurisdiction. |
| **AI Clinical Audit & IMRC Board** | Algorithmic audit of prescriptions, diagnostic orders, and operative suggestions. | Human-in-the-loop clinical review; double-blind processing; mandatory hearing rights. | National Medical Commission & High Court judicial review. |
| **National Medical Records Protection Authority (NMRPA)** | Data security, zero-trust architecture, and DPDP 2023 compliance for National EHR. | Independent Data Protection Board; audit logging; court-warrant requirement. | High Courts / Supreme Court writ remedies. |
| **National Universal Health Trust Fund Board** | Non-lapsable public healthcare fund management. | Autonomous judicial/expert Board of Trustees; CAG annual statutory audit. | Parliamentary Public Accounts Committee review. |
