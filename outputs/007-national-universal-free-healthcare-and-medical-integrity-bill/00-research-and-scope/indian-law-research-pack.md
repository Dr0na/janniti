# Research Pack: Universal Free Healthcare & Medical Integrity Framework in India

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/007-national-universal-free-healthcare-and-medical-integrity-bill`  
**Jurisdiction:** India (Union and States/UTs)  
**As-of Date:** 2026-07-29  

---

## 1. Statutory & Constitutional Landscape

### A. Constitutional Framework & Legislative Competence
1. **Article 21 (Right to Life)**: Interpreted by the Supreme Court of India in *Paschim Banga Khet Mazdoor Samity v. State of West Bengal* (1996) 4 SCC 37 and *Consumer Education and Research Centre v. Union of India* (1995) 3 SCC 42 as encompassing the fundamental right to health, emergency medical care, and medical facilities.
2. **Article 47 (Directive Principles)**: Imposes a primary duty on the State to raise the level of nutrition and the standard of living of its people and to improve public health.
3. **Seventh Schedule Distribution of Powers**:
   - **List II (State List), Item 6**: *"Public health and sanitation; hospitals and dispensaries."*
   - **List III (Concurrent List), Item 20**: *"Economic and social planning."*
   - **List III (Concurrent List), Item 23**: *"Social security and social insurance; employment and unemployment."*
   - **List III (Concurrent List), Item 26**: *"Legal, medical and other professions."*
   - **List III (Concurrent List), Item 1**: *"Criminal law."*
   - **Article 246 & Article 252/253**: Union Parliament has competence to enact national framework legislation for universal health entitlements, professional standards, price caps, criminal prohibitions on kickbacks/antimicrobial abuse, and inter-state health planning, bolstered by resolutions under Article 252 or treaty/international commitments (WHO IHR, Alma-Ata Declaration, Astana Declaration) under Article 253.

### B. Existing Enactments & Deficiencies
1. **Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010**: Voluntary adoption by States; lacks universal free care guarantees and fails to prescribe radius-based infrastructure density.
2. **National Medical Commission Act, 2019**: Regulates medical education and professional conduct, but NMC Ethics Regulations (2023) lack criminal teeth to penalize pharma-doctor brand kickbacks.
3. **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 & Pharmacy Act, 1948**: Regulates drug manufacture and sale, but fails to prevent irrational antibiotic over-the-counter dispensing and lacks AI clinical audit mechanisms.
4. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023**: Provides general data privacy rules, but lacks specialized medical record encryption and patient-centric EHR governance.

---

## 2. Baseline Health Indicators & Evidence Gaps

1. **Out-of-Pocket Expenditure (OOPE)**: High household expenditure (~47% of total health expenditure) drives millions into poverty annually due to catastrophic health shocks, private diagnostic costs, and branded medicine markups.
2. **Infrastructure Deficit**: Severe rural-urban disparity in hospital beds, specialized ICU facilities, and diagnostic labs. Lack of statutory radius mandates (15 km urban / 30 km rural) leaves rural populations underserved.
3. **Prescribing & Operative Misconduct**:
   - Widespread prescribing of expensive branded medicines driven by pharmaceutical company incentives, gifts, sponsorships, and kickbacks rather than generic chemical names.
   - Unnecessary surgeries (e.g., unwarranted Caesarean sections, hysterectomies, cardiac stent implants) and irrational diagnostic testing.
   - Rampant, unchecked use of broad-spectrum antibiotics causing severe Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR).

---

## 3. Reform Imperatives & Policy Architecture

1. **Universal Free Health Services**: Free outpatient care, inpatient care, diagnostic tests, surgeries, OTC medicines, essential life-saving drugs, and medical equipment across all public health facilities.
2. **Private Facility Price Ceiling**: National Healthcare Tariff Authority (NHTA) to enforce mandatory nominal price caps on private hospitals and diagnostic centres.
3. **Statutory Radius Density**: Mandatory state-of-the-art public healthcare facility within 15 km in urban areas and 30 km in rural areas.
4. **AI Clinical Auditing**: Double-blind AI-driven prescription and operative procedure auditing under the National AI Policy, supervised by Independent Medical Review Committees (IMRCs).
5. **Secure EHR Architecture**: End-to-end encrypted National Health Record System compliant with DPDP 2023.
6. **Criminalization of Brand Kickbacks & Antimicrobial Abuse**: Penal provisions under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) / statutory penal clauses prohibiting pharmaceutical brand favoritism and unapproved antibiotic sales.
7. **Entrenchment & Anti-Repeal**: Proposed Constitutional (139th Amendment) Bill inserting Article 21B, combined with statutory non-derogation clauses and a National Universal Health Trust Fund.
