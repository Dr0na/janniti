## 1. Adversarial Scenario & Abuse Matrix

| Adversarial Attack Vector | Operational Risk | Statutory Countermeasure in Bill | Residual Risk Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subsequent Government Repeal / Dilution** | Future executive or legislative coalition attempts to repeal free care or re-introduce user fees. | Clause 16 non-derogation clause + Schedule I draft Constitutional 139th Amendment (Article 21B) entrenching right in Part III. | **Low** (Constitutional barrier) |
| **Pharma Regulatory Capture** | Pharmaceutical lobby captures prescription guidelines or dilutes anti-kickback enforcement. | Clause 7, 9 & 12 enforce independent multi-stakeholder governance, transparent generic INN rules, and criminal prosecution. | **Low** (Independent oversight) |
| **AI Algorithmic Overreach / Bias** | Automated AI audit system denies legitimate off-label clinical treatments or misidentifies doctors. | Clause 11(3) & 12 require double-blind AI processing, mandatory Independent Medical Review Board human evaluation, and hearing rights. | **Low** (Human-in-the-loop) |
| **Mass EHR Patient Privacy Breach** | Unauthorized access to patient health data by state intelligence, insurers, or cyber-attackers. | Clause 13 mandates zero-trust architecture, end-to-end encryption, strict DPDP Act compliance, and mandatory judicial warrants. | **Low** (Cryptographic security) |
| **Inter-State Funding Starvation** | Central Government restricts health funding to opposition-ruled States. | Clause 14 & 15 establish the autonomous National Universal Health Trust Fund backed by a statutory non-lapsable health cess. | **Low** (Autonomous fund) |

2. **Federal Balance**: Union framework legislation respects State administrative autonomy under List III (Concurrent List), providing non-lapsable central funds while enabling State rule-making under Clause 22.
