# Public Consultation & Deliberation Plan: National Universal Free Healthcare and Medical Integrity Bill, 2026

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/007-national-universal-free-healthcare-and-medical-integrity-bill`  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  

---

## Consultation Architecture

1. **60-Day Nationwide Public Window**: Multi-channel submission via digital portal, district drop-boxes, and physical town-halls.
2. **22 Official Languages Translation**: Full draft and plain-language summaries published in all Schedule VIII languages.
3. **Lobbying Transparency Register**: Compulsory registration of all meetings and written submissions from pharmaceutical companies, private hospital lobbies, and diagnostic device manufacturers.
4. **Public Outcome Matrix**: Mandatory publication of a itemized matrix explaining accepted and rejected public suggestions prior to parliamentary introduction.
