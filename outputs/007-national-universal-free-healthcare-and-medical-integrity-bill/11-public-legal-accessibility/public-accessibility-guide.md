# Public Legal Accessibility Guide: Universal Free Healthcare & Medical Integrity

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/007-national-universal-free-healthcare-and-medical-integrity-bill`  

---

## What This Bill Means for Every Citizen

### 1. Completely Free Healthcare at Public Facilities
- **Zero Cost**: Every person in India has the right to free treatment, surgeries, ICU care, diagnostics, OTC medicines, and prescription generic drugs at all government hospitals and health centres.
- **No Hidden Charges**: Public facilities cannot ask for any registration fee, user charge, or payment for blood or medicines.

### 2. Fair & Capped Prices at Private Hospitals
- **Price Ceilings**: Private hospitals and diagnostic labs cannot charge more than the maximum nominal prices set by the government.
- **No Advance Denial**: Private hospitals cannot demand cash deposits before giving emergency life-saving care.

### 3. Healthcare Facilities Nearby
- **Urban Mandate**: A modern public health facility must be available within **15 km** in cities.
- **Rural Mandate**: A modern public health facility must be available within **30 km** in villages.

### 4. Stopping Brand Corruption & Unnecessary Surgeries
- **Generic Medicines**: Doctors must prescribe generic chemical names instead of expensive brand names.
- **AI Prescription Audit**: AI tools will check prescriptions to catch doctors or hospitals doing unnecessary surgeries or taking brand kickbacks.
- **Stopping Antibiotic Misuse**: Antibiotics will be controlled to stop dangerous antibiotic resistance.

### 5. Protected Patient Records & Anti-Repeal Guarantee
- **Private EHR**: Your medical records are encrypted and protected under strict data privacy laws.
- **Constitutional Guarantee**: The right to free healthcare is grounded in the Constitution under Article 21 and proposed Article 21B so no future government can take it away.
