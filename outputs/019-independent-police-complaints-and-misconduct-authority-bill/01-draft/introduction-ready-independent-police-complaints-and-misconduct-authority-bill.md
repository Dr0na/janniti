# THE INDEPENDENT POLICE COMPLAINTS AND MISCONDUCT AUTHORITY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to establish independent District and State Police Complaints Authorities headed by retired judicial officers; to confer direct investigative powers, search powers, and First Information Report registration powers on the Authorities in cases of police misconduct; to mandate automatic inquiries into custodial deaths, police firings, and severe police brutality; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Independent Police Complaints and Misconduct Authority Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "District Complaints Authority" means the District Police Complaints Authority established under section 3;

(b) "Police Misconduct" means any act or omission by a police officer amounting to custodial violence, extortion, illegal detention, sexual harassment, false implication, or abuse of power; and

(c) "State Complaints Authority" means the State Police Complaints Authority established under section 3.

### 3. Establishment of Independent Police Complaints Authorities

(1) Every State Government shall establish an independent State Police Complaints Authority at the state level and a District Police Complaints Authority in every district.

(2) The State Complaints Authority shall be headed by a retired Judge of the High Court, and the District Complaints Authority shall be headed by a retired District Judge.

(3) The Authorities established under sub-section (1) shall operate independently of the police department and shall have their own dedicated cadre of forensic investigators.

### 4. Direct Investigative and FIR Registration Powers

(1) The Complaints Authority shall have power to receive complaints directly from any aggrieved person, register First Information Reports, and conduct independent criminal investigations into Police Misconduct.

(2) The directions and recommendations of the Complaints Authority regarding disciplinary proceedings or criminal prosecution shall be binding on the State Government and Police Department.

### 5. Mandatory Automatic Inquiries

(1) The Complaints Authority shall initiate a mandatory automatic inquiry into every incident involving—

  (a) death or severe bodily injury in police custody;

  (b) police firing resulting in injury or death; and

  (c) allegation of rape or sexual assault committed by a police officer.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Internal police inquiry mechanisms suffer from institutional bias, leading to impunity for police misconduct (*Prakash Singh* judgment).

2. The Independent Police Complaints and Misconduct Authority Bill, 2026 establishes independent complaints authorities headed by retired judges with binding investigative and prosecution powers.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3 establishes District and State Police Complaints Authorities.

2. Recurring annual operational expenditure is estimated at rupees five hundred crores.

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION
