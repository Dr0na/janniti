# Analytical and Drafting Record: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

### 1.1 The Public Problem
India's existing education system faces major structural deficits that prevent the nation from fulfilling its human potential and achieving international research and technological leadership:
1. **Age Coverage Deficit**: The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (RTE Act) guarantees free education only for ages 6 to 14 (Classes 1 to 8). Pre-primary education (ages 3–6) and secondary/tertiary/vocational education (ages 14–22) remain excluded from statutory guarantees, creating severe financial barriers for working-class and middle-class families.
2. **Commercialization & Out-of-Pocket Burden**: The proliferation of unregulated private educational institutions charging high tuition fees, capitation fees, and arbitrary levies has created deep educational inequality, converting education from a public good into a commercial commodity.
3. **Curricular Rote-Memorization & Science Deficit**: School and university curricula remain dominated by theoretical rote-memorization, exam-oriented cramming, and pseudoscientific dogmas, failing to inculcate empirical scientific temper (Article 51A(h)), critical inquiry, environmental stewardship (Article 51A(g)), or civic law-abiding responsibility.
4. **Under-Funded R&D & Innovation Vacuum**: National expenditure on Research & Development has hovered at ~0.65% of GDP for decades (compared to 2.5–4.5% in advanced research nations). Undergraduate and postgraduate students lack hands-on research fellowships, state-of-the-art laboratory access, or industry innovation integration.

### 1.2 Legislative Objective
To formulate a comprehensive, rights-respecting statute—**The Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026**—that:
1. **Establishes Universal Free Education**: Expands the statutory guarantee of free, high-quality public education to cover every child and youth from age 3 (Early Childhood Care and Education) through age 22 (Undergraduate Degree, Technical Diploma, or Professional Vocational Training) without fee barriers.
2. **Embeds Constitutional Values & Scientific Temper**: Mandates a secular, scientific-temperament-focused curriculum (Article 51A(h)) that promotes rationality, equality, fraternity, anti-discrimination, constitutionalism, civic law-abiding behavior, traffic safety, and cleanliness.
3. **Institutes Practical Ecological Stewardship**: Replaces classroom-only environmental studies with mandatory hands-on ecological conservation, tree planting, waste management, and practical compassion for animals and nature (Article 51A(g)).
4. **Enforces 50:50 Experiential-Practical Learning**: Mandates a balanced 50:50 ratio between theoretical instruction and practical/laboratory/field training, skill development, personality building, and entrepreneurship incubators.
5. **Prioritizes & Incentivizes National R&D**: Mandates a progressive statutory national R&D budget allocation reaching **not less than 3 per cent of GDP within 10 years**, establishes the National Scientific Research Foundation, and provides mandatory Undergraduate Research Fellowships.
6. **Outlaws Commercialization & Capitation Fees**: Prohibits capitation fees, commercial profit extraction, and opaque admissions in educational institutions, enforcing strict public fee regulation and transparent digital allocation.

## 2. Constitutional Authority & Competence Map

| Constitutional Provision | Subject Matter | Competence & Legal Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Article 21A** | Fundamental Right to free and compulsory education. | Union Parliament and State Legislatures have competence to expand free education beyond age 14 to cover ages 3–22. |
| **Article 51A(h)** | Fundamental Duty to develop scientific temper, humanism, inquiry, and reform. | Guides mandatory national curriculum content, promoting empirical rationalism and critical inquiry. |
| **Article 51A(g)** | Fundamental Duty to protect the natural environment and have compassion for living creatures. | Directs mandatory practical environmental conservation and animal welfare education modules. |
| **Article 14, 15, 29, 30** | Fundamental rights to equality, anti-discrimination, and secular education. | All public and private educational institutions must comply with non-discriminatory admission standards while safeguarding constitutional minority rights. |
| **Concurrent List Entry 25** | Education, including technical, medical, and university education. | Parliamentary competence over national education standards. Central statute prevails over conflicting State laws under Article 254. |
| **Union List Entry 63–66** | Institutions of national importance; standards in higher education and research. | Exclusive Parliamentary competence over national research funding, Central Universities, and R&D standards. |

## 3. Core Reform Pillars of the Bill

### 3.1 Universal Free Education (Age 3 to 22)
- **Pre-Primary to Higher Education Guarantee**: Free tuition, textbooks, uniforms, nutritious meals, and learning materials in all public and state-assisted educational institutions from pre-school (age 3) to undergraduate degree/vocational qualification (age 22).
- **Public Infrastructure Expansion**: Mandates state construction of neighborhood composite public schools and regional university campuses to accommodate universal enrollment within 5 years.

### 3.2 Scientific Temper, Secularism & Constitutional Ethics
- **Empirical Scientific Curriculum**: Eliminates unscientific dogmas, superstition, and pseudoscientific assertions from textbooks. Focuses on peer-reviewed science, critical inquiry, evidence-based reasoning, and humanism.
- **Constitutional Citizenship & Civic Duty**: Mandatory practical modules on constitutional rights, equality, fraternity, anti-discrimination, gender equality, traffic safety rules, civic cleanliness (Swachhata), and respect for rule of law.

### 3.3 Practical Ecological Conservation & Animal Compassion
- **Field-Based Environmental Education**: Students engage in mandatory weekly practical activities including tree planting, organic gardening, watershed monitoring, plastic waste reduction, and local ecosystem restoration.
- **Compassion for Living Creatures**: Practical instruction on animal welfare, ethical treatment of animals, street animal care, and biodiversity protection.

### 3.4 50:50 Experiential Learning, Skills & Entrepreneurship
- **Curricular Balance**: 50% time allocated to academic theory and 50% to practical laboratories, workshops, vocational skills, coding, design thinking, entrepreneurship incubators, sports, and personality development.
- **District Vocational & Entrepreneurship Hubs**: Mandatory establishment of maker spaces, incubation centers, and vocational workshops in every district secondary school and college.

### 3.5 R&D Ecosystem & Statutory 3% GDP Budget Allocation
- **Statutory R&D Target**: Enacts a statutory schedule mandating that Union and State public R&D expenditure reach 3% of GDP within 10 years.
- **National Scientific Research Foundation (NSRF)**: Independent statutory body providing competitive research grants to universities, laboratories, and young researchers.
- **Undergraduate Research Fellowships**: Mandatory stipend-backed research projects for undergraduate students in science, engineering, humanities, and medicine.

### 3.6 Anti-Commercialization & Capitation Fee Ban
- **Strict Capitation Ban**: Outlaws any donation, capitation fee, or hidden charge in educational institutions, making violation a severe criminal offence (up to 10 years imprisonment).
- **State Education Fee Regulation Commissions**: Independent statutory commissions in every State to audit and cap private school and university fees on a non-profit, cost-recovery basis.

## 5. Detailed Clause Breakdown of the Bill

* **Clauses 1–3**: Short title, extent (All India), staged commencement, definitions (e.g., *Scientific Temper*, *Universal Free Education*, *Experiential Learning*, *Research and Development Expense*), and statutory purpose declaration.
* **Clauses 4–7**: Universal Free Education Guarantee from age 3 to 22, public school and university capacity expansion, prohibition of fee barriers, and mandatory free learning materials/nutrition.
* **Clauses 8–11**: National Curriculum Framework for Scientific Temper, Secularism, Constitutional Values, Civic Duty, Traffic Safety, and Cleanliness.
* **Clauses 12–14**: Practical Ecological Conservation Framework, hands-on environmental stewardship, and Practical Animal Welfare Education.
* **Clauses 15–18**: 50:50 Experiential and Practical Learning Mandate, District Skill & Entrepreneurship Incubators, and Personality Development Modules.
* **Clauses 19–23**: National Research and Development Framework, statutory 3% GDP budget allocation target, National Scientific Research Foundation (NSRF), and Undergraduate Research Fellowships.
* **Clauses 24–28**: Prohibition of Commercialization, ban on capitation fees, State Fee Regulation Commissions, and criminal penalties for illegal capitation fee collection (up to 10 years imprisonment).
* **Clauses 29–33**: National Education Quality & Integrity Inspectorate, annual CAG & academic audits, rulemaking powers, laying before Parliament, and repeal/savings.
