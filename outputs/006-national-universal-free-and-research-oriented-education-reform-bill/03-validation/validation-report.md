# Validation Audit Report: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Legislative Package, 2026

## 1. Validation Conclusion

**Conclusion**: **Legally and Constitutionally Robust with Article 21C Entrenchment Safeguard**.

The legislative package combines **The Constitution (One Hundred and Thirty-Ninth Amendment) Bill, 2026** and **The Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Act, 2026**. By elevating universal free education (ages 3–22) to a Part III Fundamental Right under Article 21A and inserting Article 21C (requiring a 2/3 special parliamentary majority + ratification by at least half of State Legislatures for any future dilution), the framework effectively insulates free education and scientific temper against political repeal or executive austerity.

## 4. Institutional Power Map & Oversight

- **National Scientific Research Foundation (NSRF)**: Independent statutory body providing competitive research grants to universities and stipend-backed Fellowships to 500,000 research students annually.
- **National Education Quality and Integrity Inspectorate**: Conducts unannounced academic, infrastructural, and financial audits, publishing district-wise education performance scorecards.
- **Special Anti-Corruption Courts**: Fast-track trials for corruption, safety negligence, and paper leaks within 12 months without prior executive sanction requirements.
