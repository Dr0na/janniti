# Validation Audit Report: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026

## 1. Validation Conclusion

**Conclusion**: **Legally and Constitutionally Robust**.

The Bill expands free education under Article 21A, operationalizes Fundamental Duties under Article 51A(g/h), and establishes a non-commercialized, research-oriented public education system while fully respecting fundamental rights and minority educational protections under Articles 14, 15, 29, and 30 of the Constitution of India.

---

## 2. Scope and Source Record

- **Instrument Reviewed**: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026.
- **Jurisdiction**: India (Union Parliament enactment under Concurrent List Entry 25 and Union List Entries 63–66).
- **As-of Date**: 2026-07-27.
- **Primary Statutory & Constitutional Baseline**:
  - Constitution of India (Articles 14, 15, 21A, 29, 30, 51A(g/h), Schedule VII).
  - Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (RTE Act).
  - University Grants Commission Act, 1956.
  - Supreme Court Precedents: *Mohini Jain* (1992), *Unni Krishnan* (1993), *Society for Unaided Private Schools of Rajasthan* (2012).

## 4. Institutional Power Map & Oversight

- **National Scientific Research Foundation (NSRF)**: Independent statutory body providing competitive research grants to universities and stipend-backed Fellowships to 500,000 undergraduate/postgraduate research students annually.
- **National Education Quality and Integrity Inspectorate**: Conducts unannounced academic, infrastructural, and financial audits, publishing district-wise education performance scorecards.
- **State Education Fee Regulation Commissions**: Statutory state commissions headed by retired High Court judges enforcing cost-recovery fee caps on private institutions.
