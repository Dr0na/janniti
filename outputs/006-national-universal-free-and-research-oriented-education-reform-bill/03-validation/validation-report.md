# Validation Audit Report: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026

## 1. Validation Conclusion

**Conclusion**: **Legally and Constitutionally Robust with Expanded Safeguards**.

The Bill expands free education under Article 21A, operationalizes Fundamental Duties under Article 51A(g/h), enforces national minimum facility benchmarks across all schools (Schedule I), establishes absolute stream parity across Arts, Science, Commerce, Vocational, and Sports, integrates early skills and foreign languages, institutes daily secular Vipassana mindfulness, and enacts severe criminal penalties for financial corruption and safety negligence under Special Anti-Corruption Courts while respecting Articles 14, 15, 25, 28, 29, and 30 of the Constitution of India.

## 2. Scope and Source Record

- **Instrument Reviewed**: Right to Free, Universal, Scientific and Research-Oriented Education Bill, 2026.
- **Jurisdiction**: India (Union Parliament enactment under Concurrent List Entry 25 and Union List Entries 63–66).
- **As-of Date**: 2026-07-27.
- **Primary Statutory & Constitutional Baseline**:
  - Constitution of India (Articles 14, 15, 21A, 25, 28, 29, 30, 51A(g/h), Schedule VII).
  - Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (RTE Act).
  - University Grants Commission Act, 1956.
  - Supreme Court Precedents: *Mohini Jain* (1992), *Unni Krishnan* (1993), *Society for Unaided Private Schools of Rajasthan* (2012).

## 4. Institutional Power Map & Oversight

- **National Scientific Research Foundation (NSRF)**: Independent statutory body providing competitive research grants to universities and stipend-backed Fellowships to 500,000 research students annually.
- **National Education Quality and Integrity Inspectorate**: Conducts unannounced academic, infrastructural, and financial audits, publishing district-wise education performance scorecards.
- **Special Anti-Corruption Courts**: Fast-track trials for corruption, safety negligence, and paper leaks within 12 months without prior executive sanction requirements.
