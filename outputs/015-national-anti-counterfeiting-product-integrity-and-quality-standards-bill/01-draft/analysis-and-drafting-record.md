## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives

The *National Anti-Counterfeiting, Product Integrity, and Quality Standards Bill, 2026* creates a unified, comprehensive statutory framework to eliminate the manufacturing, distribution, importation, online listing, and sale of counterfeit, fake, sub-standard, and pirated goods in India.

### Key Statutory Innovations
1. **Mandatory Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization**: Statutory duty requiring manufacturers and importers to embed cryptographically secure, tamper-evident digital codes (QR/RFID) on all consumer commodities.
2. **National Product Authenticity Portal**: Centralized public database allowing consumers and enforcement officers to verify product authenticity instantly using mobile devices.
3. **Graduated Criminal Penalties**:
   - *Life-Critical Goods* (medicines, food, infant products, auto/aviation parts, electrical appliances): Mandatory minimum 7 years imprisonment up to **Life Imprisonment** and fine up to ₹5 Crores.
   - *General Consumer Goods*: Mandatory minimum 3 years up to 10 years imprisonment and fine up to ₹1 Crore.
4. **Asset Forfeiture & Syndicate Demolition**: Summary court-ordered confiscation of manufacturing equipment, factories, storage facilities, vehicles, and ill-gotten bank accounts of counterfeiting syndicates.
5. **E-Commerce Platform Liability**: Strict co-defendant liability for e-commerce marketplaces listing unverified or fake products.
6. **Consumer Compensation**: Statutory compensation of 10 times purchase price or ₹10 Lakhs (whichever is higher) payable to consumers harmed by counterfeits.
7. **Small Retailer Safeguard**: Protection for innocent micro-retailers possessing valid tax invoices and genuine-appearing track-and-trace codes.

## 2. Constitutional & Legislative Competence

- **Seventh Schedule Alignment**:
  - **List I, Item 49**: Patents, trademarks, merchandise marks, and copyright.
  - **List III, Item 33**: Trade and commerce in, and production, supply, and distribution of products.
  - **List III, Item 34**: Price control and quality standards.
  - **List III, Item 1**: Criminal law.
- **Article 21 (Right to Life & Health)**: Grounded in Supreme Court jurisprudence (*Centre for Public Interest Litigation v. Union of India* (2013) 16 SCC 279), establishing that state failure to prevent spurious and hazardous products violates Article 21.

1. **National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority (NAPIA)**: Independent statutory enforcement agency.
2. **National Product Authenticity Information Portal (NAPIP)**: Open cryptographic verification platform.
3. **Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts**: Fast-track district courts conducting summary trials within 90 days.
