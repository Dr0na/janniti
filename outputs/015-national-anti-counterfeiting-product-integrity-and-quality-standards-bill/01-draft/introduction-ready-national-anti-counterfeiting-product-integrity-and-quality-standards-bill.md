# THE NATIONAL ANTI-COUNTERFEITING, PRODUCT INTEGRITY, AND QUALITY STANDARDS BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to prohibit the manufacturing, distribution, importation, online listing, and sale of counterfeit, fake, sub-standard, and pirated goods; to mandate a digital track-and-trace authentication architecture for all consumer and industrial products; to establish severe criminal penalties and summary asset forfeiture for counterfeiting syndicates; to enforce strict liability on e-commerce platforms; to guarantee statutory consumer compensation; to establish the National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the National Anti-Counterfeiting, Product Integrity, and Quality Standards Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint, provided that the mandatory digital track-and-trace provisions under section 4 shall come into force within twelve months of enactment for life-critical commodities and within twenty-four months for all other consumer goods.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Asset Forfeiture" means the permanent judicial confiscation of manufacturing machinery, factories, storage facilities, vehicles, electronic hardware, and financial accounts utilized in or derived from counterfeiting activities under section 8;

(b) "Bona Fide Retailer" means a retail seller who purchases goods in good faith accompanied by valid tax invoices and genuine-appearing digital track-and-trace credentials without knowledge of their counterfeit nature;

(c) "Counterfeit Product" means any good, packaging, component, label, or commodity that bears without authorization a trademark, brand name, quality mark, patent, or design identical to or deceptively resembling a valid registered mark or statutory quality standard;

(d) "Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization" means the unique, cryptographically secure, tamper-evident digital code, quick-response (QR) code, or radio-frequency identification (RFID) tag affixed to a product package under section 4;

(e) "Life-Critical Commodity" means any pharmaceutical drug, medical device, food item, infant formula, automotive safety component, aviation part, electrical appliance, or agricultural seed or chemical;

(f) "National Anti-Counterfeiting Authority" means the National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority established under section 6;

(g) "National Authenticity Portal" means the centralized digital verification database established under section 5; and

(h) "Sub-Standard Product" means any commodity failing to comply with compulsory quality standards prescribed under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 or rules made under this Act.

### 3. Prohibition of Counterfeiting and Sub-Standard Manufacturing

(1) No person shall manufacture, assemble, import, store, transport, distribute, offer for sale, or sell any Counterfeit Product or Sub-Standard Product.

(2) No person shall manufacture, possess, or supply any fake packaging material, counterfeit security label, dye, mould, or digital serialization printer intended for use in counterfeiting.

(3) No e-commerce platform or online marketplace shall list, host, or facilitate the sale of any product without verifying the seller's business registration and digital track-and-trace credentials.

### 4. Mandatory Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization

(1) Every manufacturer, packager, and importer of goods shall affix a unique Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization code to every unit container, batch, and master carton prior to distribution.

(2) The Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization code shall store immutable data regarding the manufacturer identity, production facility location, batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, and statutory quality certification details.

(3) All serialization data generated pursuant to sub-section (1) shall be transmitted in real time to the National Authenticity Portal.

### 5. National Product Authenticity Information Portal

(1) The Central Government shall establish and maintain the National Product Authenticity Information Portal as an open, real-time digital verification database.

(2) The Portal shall enable consumers, retailers, and enforcement officers to verify product authenticity instantly through mobile application, short message service (SMS), or web interface without charge.

### 6. National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority.

(2) The Authority shall consist of a Chairperson who is a retired judge of the Supreme Court or a High Court, and members representing law enforcement, customs, technical standards, consumer rights, and intellectual property.

(3) The Authority shall have independent powers of investigation, search, seizure, plant sealing, border interception, and prosecution across India.

### 7. Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts and Fast-Track Trial

(1) The State Government shall, in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court, designate one or more Courts of Session in each district as Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts.

(2) Every offence under this Act shall be tried summarily by the Special Anti-Counterfeiting Court, and the trial shall be concluded within ninety days from the date of filing of the charge sheet.

### 8. Offence of Counterfeiting Life-Critical Commodities and Penalties

(1) Whoever contravenes section 3 by manufacturing, importing, or distributing any counterfeit Life-Critical Commodity shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than seven years but which may extend to life imprisonment, and with fine which shall not be less than fifty lakh rupees but which may extend to five crore rupees.

(2) Whoever contravenes section 3 in respect of general consumer goods shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than three years but which may extend to ten years, and with fine which shall not be less than ten lakh rupees but which may extend to one crore rupees.

(3) Upon conviction under sub-section (1) or sub-section (2), the Special Anti-Counterfeiting Court shall order the mandatory Asset Forfeiture of all manufacturing units, equipment, storage facilities, transport vehicles, and financial assets belonging to the convicted entity or syndicate.

### 9. E-Commerce Liability and Digital Takedown

(1) Where an e-commerce platform lists or facilitates the sale of a Counterfeit Product, the National Anti-Counterfeiting Authority shall issue an immediate digital takedown notice requiring the removal of the listing within twelve hours.

(2) If an e-commerce platform fails to comply with sub-section (1) or repeatedly lists unverified sellers, it shall be liable as a co-defendant to the criminal penalties under section 8 and civil damages under section 10.

### 10. Statutory Consumer Compensation and Health Protection

(1) Any consumer who suffers financial loss, health injury, or property damage due to a Counterfeit Product shall be entitled to statutory compensation from the manufacturer, importer, distributor, and non-verified retailer equal to ten times the purchase price of the product or ten lakh rupees, whichever is higher.

(2) In cases of severe bodily injury or death caused by a counterfeit Life-Critical Commodity, statutory compensation shall not be less than fifty lakh rupees, in addition to criminal liability under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

### 11. Protection of Bona Fide Retailers and Small Businesses

(1) No criminal prosecution shall lie under this Act against a Bona Fide Retailer who establishes that the counterfeit goods were purchased under valid tax invoices from an authorized distributor and bore genuine-appearing track-and-trace credentials.

(2) Where a Bona Fide Retailer surrenders counterfeit goods upon notice, the executing authority shall seal the goods and proceed exclusively against the upstream manufacturer, importer, or distributor.

### 12. Protection of Action Taken in Good Faith

No suit, prosecution, or other legal proceeding shall lie against the Central Government, State Government, Authority, or any officer for anything done or intended to be done in good faith under this Act or rules made thereunder.

### 13. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) In particular, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing power, such rules may provide for—

  (a) technical specifications and cryptographic encryption standards for Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization under section 4;

  (b) operating protocols for the National Product Authenticity Information Portal under section 5; and

  (c) procedures for asset seizure, forfeiture, and public auction under section 8.

(3) Every rule made under this section shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is made, before each House of Parliament while it is in session.

### 14. Overriding Effect

The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force, other than the Constitution of India.

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Counterfeit, fake, sub-standard, and spurious goods inflict immense damage on public safety, consumer health, and the national economy in India, resulting in financial losses exceeding one lakh fifty thousand crore rupees annually and causing tragic fatalities due to fake medicines, sub-standard auto parts, and defective electrical items.

2. Existing statutes such as the Trade Marks Act 1999, Bureau of Indian Standards Act 2016, and Consumer Protection Act 2019 are fragmented, lack mandatory digital supply-chain serialization, lack asset forfeiture mechanisms, and fail to provide fast-track criminal deterrence against organized counterfeiting cartels.

3. The National Anti-Counterfeiting, Product Integrity, and Quality Standards Bill, 2026 establishes mandatory digital track-and-trace serialization, opens the National Product Authenticity Information Portal for instant consumer verification, imposes severe criminal penalties (up to Life Imprisonment for fake life-critical goods), orders summary asset forfeiture, enforces e-commerce strict liability, guarantees 10x consumer compensation, and protects bona fide small retailers.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 5 mandates the creation and operation of the National Product Authenticity Information Portal. Clause 6 establishes the National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority. Clause 7 provides for Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts.

2. Non-recurring capital expenditure for digital portal architecture, forensic testing laboratories, and enforcement hardware is estimated at approximately rupees two thousand five hundred crores over a three-year period.

3. Recurring annual operational expenditure for authority operations, forensic testing, and digital portal maintenance is estimated at approximately rupees five hundred crores annually, which shall be fully met through Parliamentary appropriations and funds recovered from syndicate asset forfeitures and civil penalties under section 8.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 13 empowers the Central Government to make rules regarding technical cryptographic encryption standards for digital track-and-trace serialization, portal operating protocols, and procedures for asset seizure and auction.

2. The matters in respect of which rules may be made are administrative and technical details, and the delegation of legislative power is, therefore, of a normal and administrative character.
