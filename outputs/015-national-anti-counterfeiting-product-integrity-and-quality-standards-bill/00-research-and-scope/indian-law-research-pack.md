# Research Pack: Anti-Counterfeiting, Product Integrity & Quality Standards in India

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/015-national-anti-counterfeiting-product-integrity-and-quality-standards-bill`  
**Jurisdiction:** India (Union & States)  
**As-of Date:** 2026-07-29  

---

## 1. Statutory & Constitutional Landscape

1. **Constitutional Basis**:
   - **Article 21 (Right to Life & Health)**: Protects citizens against dangerous, fake, sub-standard, and toxic products (*Centre for Public Interest Litigation v. Union of India* (2013) 16 SCC 279).
   - **Seventh Schedule Distribution**:
     - **List I, Item 49**: Patents, inventions and designs; copyright; trade-marks and merchandise marks.
     - **List III, Item 33**: Trade and commerce in, and the production, supply and distribution of products.
     - **List III, Item 34**: Price control and quality standards.
     - **List III, Item 1**: Criminal law.

2. **Existing Enactments & Fragmented Enforcement**:
   - **Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016**: Prescribes quality standards, but lacks track-and-trace powers and anti-counterfeiting criminal enforcement.
   - **Trade Marks Act, 1999 & Copyright Act, 1957**: Provides civil remedies and limited criminal penalties for trademark infringement, but treats counterfeiting primarily as a private commercial dispute rather than a public health/economic crime.
   - **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**: Regulates unfair trade practices and product liability, but lacks asset forfeiture mechanisms and track-and-trace serialization mandates.
   - **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**: Regulates spurious drugs, but leaves non-pharma counterfeiting (electricals, auto parts, seeds, food) under weaker statutes.

---

## 2. Empirical Impact of Counterfeiting in India

- **Economic Loss**: Counterfeit products account for over ₹1.5 Lakh Crores in annual economic losses in India across FMCG, pharmaceuticals, auto parts, agro-chemicals, apparel, and electronics.
- **Public Safety Hazards**: Spurious drugs, fake electrical wiring, sub-standard auto brakes, and fake pesticides cause thousands of preventable deaths, fires, crop failures, and injuries annually.
- **E-Commerce Exploitation**: Unregulated online sellers exploit digital marketplaces to distribute fake goods without identity verification or track-and-trace credentials.

---

## 3. Reform Imperatives

1. **Mandatory Digital Track-and-Trace Serialization**: QR code/RFID cryptographic authentication linked to an open National Product Authenticity Portal.
2. **Severe Criminal Penalties**: Life imprisonment for life-critical counterfeits (medicines, food, auto parts); 3–10 years for general counterfeits.
3. **Asset Forfeiture**: Summary seizure and forfeiture of factories, machinery, warehouses, vehicles, and illicit profits belonging to counterfeiting syndicates.
4. **E-Commerce Strict Liability**: Marketplace liability for listing unverified sellers or fake goods.
5. **Consumer Protection**: Mandatory 10x purchase price or ₹10 Lakhs statutory consumer compensation.
6. **Bona Fide Retailer Safeguard**: Protection for small innocent retailers stocking goods with valid tax invoices and genuine-appearing track-and-trace codes.
