## 1. Loophole & Abuse Risk Audit

| Risk Scenario | Statutory Safeguard in Bill |
|---|---|
| **E-Commerce Safe Harbor Abuse** | Clause 9 enforces strict liability for platforms failing to remove counterfeit listings within 12 hours. |
| **Harassment of Small Retailers** | Clause 11 provides a statutory *bona fide purchaser* defense for innocent retailers with valid invoices. |
| **Misuse of Criminal Law in Civil Disputes** | Clause 2 restricts counterfeiting to unauthorized deceptive imitations; ordinary civil trademark/patent disputes are excluded. |
| **Syndicate Asset Concealment** | Clause 8(3) mandates summary forfeiture of manufacturing units, vehicles, and bank accounts upon conviction. |
