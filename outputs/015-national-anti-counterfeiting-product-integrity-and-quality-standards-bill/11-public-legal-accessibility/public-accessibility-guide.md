# Public Legal Accessibility Guide: Anti-Counterfeiting & Product Integrity

---

## What This Bill Means for Every Citizen

1. **Instant QR Code Verification**: You can scan any product's QR code using your smartphone or SMS to instantly check if it is genuine.
2. **Severe Punishment for Fake Medicines and Food**: People who make or sell fake medicines, food, infant products, or auto parts face up to **Life Imprisonment** and heavy fines.
3. **Seizure of Counterfeit Factories**: Law enforcement will seize and auction factories, machines, and bank accounts of fake product syndicates.
4. **10x Consumer Compensation**: If you are harmed by a fake product, you are entitled to statutory compensation of 10 times the product price or ₹10 Lakhs.
5. **Protection for Innocent Small Shopkeepers**: Small shopkeepers who buy goods in good faith with tax invoices are protected from criminal cases.
