## Power Distribution Summary

- **National Anti-Counterfeiting and Product Integrity Authority (NAPIA)**: Independent statutory agency for track-and-trace monitoring, search/seizure, border interception, and prosecution.
- **Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts**: Fast-track judicial trial courts with summary powers and 90-day trial completion mandates.
- **High Courts / Supreme Court**: Appellate and judicial review jurisdiction.
