## 1. Summary of Review Findings

The *National Anti-Counterfeiting, Product Integrity, and Quality Standards Bill, 2026* is legally sound, constitutionally valid, and structurally effective. It operationalizes Article 21 rights by protecting consumers against spurious and hazardous counterfeits, while maintaining fair-trade protections for innocent small retailers under Clause 11.

### Key Dimensions Checked
- **Legislative Competence**: Grounded in List I Item 49 (Trademarks/Patents), List III Item 33 (Trade/Commerce), and List III Item 34 (Quality Standards).
- **Proportionality & Due Process**: Life imprisonment is restricted to counterfeit *Life-Critical Commodities* (medicines, food, auto/aviation parts); summary trials before Special Anti-Counterfeiting Courts require strict judicial proof.
- **Protection for Small Businesses**: Clause 11 shields innocent micro-retailers who possess valid tax invoices and genuine-appearing track-and-trace credentials.
