## Adversarial Risk Matrix

| Attack Vector | Countermeasure in Bill |
|---|---|
| **Arbitrary Harassment of Small Merchants** | Clause 11 statutory *bona fide purchaser* immunity for retailers with tax invoices and valid track-and-trace codes. |
| **E-Commerce Market Take-Down Delays** | Clause 9 enforces mandatory 12-hour takedown upon notice and strict co-defendant liability for non-compliance. |
| **Counterfeit Syndicate Asset Concealment** | Clause 8(3) orders mandatory summary asset forfeiture of factories, machinery, vehicles, and illicit bank accounts upon conviction. |
