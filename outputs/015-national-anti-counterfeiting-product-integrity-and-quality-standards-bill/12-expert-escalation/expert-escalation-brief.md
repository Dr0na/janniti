# Expert Escalation Brief: Anti-Counterfeiting & Quality Standards

**Matter Reference:** `outputs/015-national-anti-counterfeiting-product-integrity-and-quality-standards-bill`  

---

## Senior Counsel Escalation Items

1. **Life Imprisonment Proportionality**: Restricting life imprisonment penalties to counterfeit *Life-Critical Commodities* (spurious drugs, toxic food, defective auto/aviation parts) causing severe harm/death to satisfy constitutional proportionality.
2. **E-Commerce Platform Strict Liability**: Balancing 12-hour takedown obligations with intermediate safe harbor protections under Section 79 IT Act.
