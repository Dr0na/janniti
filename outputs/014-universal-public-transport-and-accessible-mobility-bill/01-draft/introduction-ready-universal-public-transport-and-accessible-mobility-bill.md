# THE UNIVERSAL PUBLIC TRANSPORT AND ACCESSIBLE MOBILITY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee every person the statutory right to accessible, zero-emission public transportation within one kilometre of every urban residence; to mandate one hundred per cent disability accessibility across all transit hubs, buses, and rail infrastructure; to enforce dedicated non-motorized transport lanes for bicycles and pedestrians; to establish the National Public Transit Authority; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Universal Public Transport and Accessible Mobility Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

  (a) "Accessibility Standard" means the universal design guidelines prescribed under section 4 ensuring complete accessibility for persons with disabilities, elderly persons, and children;

  (b) "National Transit Authority" means the National Public Transit Authority established under section 5;

  (c) "Non-Motorized Transport Lane" means a dedicated, physically segregated path reserved exclusively for bicycles, wheelchairs, and pedestrians; and

  (d) "Zero-Emission Transit Vehicle" means an electric, hydrogen, or non-polluting public transport vehicle.

### 3. Statutory Right to Public Transit Access

(1) Every resident of an urban area shall have an enforceable statutory right to access a functional public transport stop within a maximum walking distance of one kilometre from their residence.

(2) Local municipal authorities and state transport corporations shall ensure continuous, frequent, and affordable public bus and rail services meeting the density mandate under sub-section (1).

### 4. Mandatory Disability Accessibility Standards

(1) All public transport vehicles, buses, trains, stations, bus stops, and foot-over-bridges shall be constructed and operated in compliance with universal Accessibility Standards.

(2) Accessibility features mandated under sub-section (1) shall include low-floor kneeling buses, ramps, tactile paving, audio-visual announcements, and accessible toilet facilities.

### 5. Dedicated Non-Motorized Transport Infrastructure

(1) Municipal corporations and road authorities shall reserve not less than fifteen per cent of total road surface width on major urban arteries for physically segregated Non-Motorized Transport Lanes.

(2) Motor vehicles trespassing into designated Non-Motorized Transport Lanes shall be subject to automated impoundment and civil fine.

### 6. National Public Transit Authority

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Public Transit Authority.

(2) The Authority shall administer public transit expansion grants, enforce accessibility audits, and monitor urban fleet electrification.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Lack of accessible, frequent public transportation severely restricts mobility for millions of citizens, exacerbates urban traffic congestion, and discriminates against persons with disabilities.

2. The Universal Public Transport and Accessible Mobility Bill, 2026 guarantees a 1 km transit stop radius entitlement, mandates 100% disability accessibility, enforces dedicated bicycle/pedestrian lanes, and establishes the National Public Transit Authority.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3 and Clause 4 mandate transit fleet expansion, zero-emission bus procurement, and accessibility retrofitting.

2. Central financial assistance from the Consolidated Fund of India is estimated at rupees twenty thousand crores over a five-year period.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 7 empowers administrative rule-making.
