# Public Asset Integrity Code — Source-Matter Crosswalk

## Consolidation decision

This is a policy and drafting decision, not current law. Matters 005, 024 and 028 overlap on public-asset disclosure, audit, anti-corruption, recovery and public oversight. A consolidated Code must not create a super-agency that investigates, prosecutes, audits, decides and recovers in the same matter.

| Source matter | Retain / modify / retire | Destination | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 005 citizen-wealth/public-asset transparency | Modify | Public Asset Stewardship module | Retain transparent public-asset registers, conflict disclosure and public-benefit reporting; separate wealth-tax/antitrust proposals into their own competence review. |
| 005 anti-corruption directorate/courts/offences | Retire as drafted | Referral protocol | No new integrated enforcement directorate or special court without criminal-law and judicial-capacity review. Use existing lawful investigators, prosecutors and courts with transparent referral rules. |
| 024 open-contracting data and defect liability | Retain and expand | Procurement Integrity module | One open-contract data standard, bidder-beneficial-ownership disclosure, conflict screen, independent complaints/review and recovery process. |
| 024 procurement ombudsman | Modify | Procurement Review Office | Intake, interim preservation, review and systemic recommendation only; no prosecution, final recovery or self-appeal. |
| 028 audit/review commission | Modify | Public Asset Audit Office | Independent audit, publication and court referral only; no direct quashing, prosecution, recruitment review or final asset recovery. |
| 028 High Court recovery petitions | Modify | Judicial remedy module | Audit/review body may seek preservation/recovery relief only through competent court with notice, evidence and appeal. |

## Non-duplication lifecycle

Disclosure → independent procurement review → audit → conflict-screened investigation → independent prosecution → court decision → recovery/repair → public reporting. No one actor may own more than one coercive decision in the same matter.
