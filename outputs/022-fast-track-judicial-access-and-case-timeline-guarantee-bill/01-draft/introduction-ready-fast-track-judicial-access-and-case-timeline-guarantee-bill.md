# THE FAST-TRACK JUDICIAL ACCESS AND CASE TIMELINE GUARANTEE BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee the fundamental right to speedy trial and timely civil justice under Article 21; to prescribe mandatory maximum timelines of twelve months for criminal trials and eighteen months for civil suits; to mandate automatic judicial vacancy recruitment; to institute open digital e-court telemetry; to provide statutory compensation for un-explicated trial delays; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Fast-Track Judicial Access and Case Timeline Guarantee Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Automatic Vacancy Recruitment" means the mandatory procedure under section 4 initiating judicial appointment six months prior to any retirement;

(b) "Statutory Trial Limit" means the maximum period of twelve months for criminal trials and eighteen months for civil suits prescribed under section 3; and

(c) "Trial Delay Compensation" means the statutory monetary compensation payable to a litigant under section 6 for un-explicated trial delays.

### 3. Guarantee of Statutory Trial Completion Timelines

(1) Every criminal trial shall be concluded within a maximum period of twelve months from the date of filing of the charge sheet.

(2) Every civil suit shall be concluded within a maximum period of eighteen months from the date of institution.

### 4. Automatic Judicial Vacancy Recruitment

(1) The High Court and State Judicial Service Commission shall automatically initiate the selection process to fill any judicial vacancy six months prior to the date of superannuation of the incumbent judge.

(2) No judicial post in any Subordinate Court or High Court shall remain vacant for a period exceeding thirty days.

### 5. Mandatory Open Digital E-Court Telemetry

(1) Every court shall publish real-time open digital telemetry displaying case filing dates, hearing schedules, adjournment logs, and judge-wise clearance rates on a public portal.

(2) No court shall grant more than two adjournments in any case, and any adjournment granted shall be accompanied by written recorded reasons and costs.

### 6. Statutory Trial Delay Compensation

(1) Where a trial exceeds the Statutory Trial Limit without valid recorded reasons attributable to the litigant, the State shall pay Trial Delay Compensation of not less than fifty thousand rupees per year of delay.

(2) Compensation paid under sub-section (1) may be recovered from officers responsible for deliberate procedural obstruction.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may, in consultation with the Chief Justice of India, make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Case pendency exceeding four and a half crore cases violates the fundamental right to speedy trial under Article 21 (*Hussainara Khatoon* judgment).

2. The Fast-Track Judicial Access and Case Timeline Guarantee Bill, 2026 mandates 12-month criminal and 18-month civil trial completion caps, automatic judicial recruitment, e-court telemetry, and delay compensation.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 and Clause 6 mandate judicial recruitment and delay compensation funds.

2. Central financial assistance is estimated at rupees four thousand crores over three years.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 7 empowers administrative rule-making.
