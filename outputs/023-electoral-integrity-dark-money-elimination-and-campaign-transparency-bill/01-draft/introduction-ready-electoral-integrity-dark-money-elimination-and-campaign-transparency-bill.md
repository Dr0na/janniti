# THE ELECTORAL INTEGRITY, DARK MONEY ELIMINATION, AND CAMPAIGN TRANSPARENCY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to enforce the voter's fundamental right to information under Article 19(1)(a); to prohibit all anonymous political party donations and dark money funding; to mandate real-time digital contribution disclosure; to establish state-matched public campaign funding; to ban government-funded political advertising prior to elections; to mandate intra-party democracy; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Electoral Integrity, Dark Money Elimination, and Campaign Transparency Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Campaign Contribution" means any monetary or in-kind donation, grant, or financial assistance provided to a political party or election candidate;

(b) "Digital Contribution Portal" means the open verification database maintained under section 4; and

(c) "Political Party" means a political party registered under section 29A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951.

### 3. Absolute Prohibition of Anonymous Political Donations

(1) No political party or candidate shall accept any anonymous donation, cash contribution exceeding five hundred rupees, or secret financial grant from any domestic or foreign source.

(2) Any contribution received in contravention of sub-section (1) shall be forfeited to the Election Commission of India, and the political party concerned shall be liable to a penalty equal to five times the illicit contribution.

### 4. Mandatory Real-Time Digital Contribution Portal

(1) Every registered political party shall maintain an open digital portal linked to the Election Commission of India, recording every donation exceeding five hundred rupees in real time.

(2) The Portal shall display the donor identity, Permanent Account Number (PAN), transaction reference number, amount, and date of contribution within forty-eight hours of receipt.

### 5. Ban on State-Funded Political Advertising Prior to Elections

(1) No Government or public authority shall publish or broadcast any government-funded advertisement featuring the photograph, name, or symbol of any ruling political leader or party within six months prior to the expiry of a legislative term.

(2) Expenses incurred in violation of sub-section (1) shall be recovered personally from the elected representatives or ministers who authorized the advertisement.

### 6. Mandatory Intra-Party Democratic Elections

(1) Every registered political party shall conduct transparent, secret-ballot internal elections every three years to elect its executive committee and office-bearers under the supervision of the Election Commission of India.

(2) Failure to comply with sub-section (1) shall result in the suspension of the political party's election symbol.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may, in consultation with the Election Commission of India, make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Anonymous political funding and dark money corrupt elections and violate the voter's fundamental right to information under Article 19(1)(a) (*ADR v. Union of India* judgment).

2. The Electoral Integrity, Dark Money Elimination, and Campaign Transparency Bill, 2026 bans anonymous political cash donations, mandates real-time digital contribution disclosure, bans pre-election state advertising, and enforces intra-party democratic elections.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 mandates the operation of the National Digital Contribution Portal.

2. Recurring annual operational expenditure for portal monitoring is estimated at rupees fifty crores.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 7 empowers administrative rule-making.
