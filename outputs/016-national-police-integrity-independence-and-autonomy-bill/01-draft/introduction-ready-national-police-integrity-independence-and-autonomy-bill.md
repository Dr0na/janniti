# THE NATIONAL POLICE INTEGRITY, INDEPENDENCE, AND AUTONOMY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to insulate police administration and criminal investigation from arbitrary executive and political interference; to establish independent State Security Commissions and Police Establishment Boards; to guarantee a minimum statutory tenure of two years for police chiefs and operational commanders; to mandate the complete statutory separation of criminal investigation cadres from law-and-order duties; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the National Police Integrity, Independence, and Autonomy Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Investigation Cadre" means the specialized branch of police officers dedicated exclusively to criminal investigation under section 4;

(b) "Police Establishment Board" means the autonomous board established under section 5 to decide postings, transfers, and promotions; and

(c) "State Security Commission" means the independent statutory commission established under section 3 to lay down broad policy guidelines for police administration.

### 3. Establishment of Independent State Security Commission

(1) Every State Government and Union Territory Administration shall establish an independent State Security Commission.

(2) The State Security Commission shall consist of—

  (a) the Chief Minister or Home Minister as ex-officio Chairperson;

  (b) a retired Judge of the High Court nominated by the Chief Justice as Vice-Chairperson;

  (c) the Leader of Opposition in the State Legislative Assembly; and

  (d) three independent members of public eminence selected by a panel comprising the Chief Justice, Speaker, and Leader of Opposition.

(3) The Commission shall evaluate police performance, lay down annual policy guidelines, and ensure that no executive authority issues direction to any police officer in any specific criminal investigation.

### 4. Statutory Separation of Investigation from Law and Order

(1) Every State police force shall maintain a complete statutory separation between the Crime Investigation Cadre and the Law and Order Cadre at the station level and above.

(2) Officers assigned to the Crime Investigation Cadre shall not be deployed for routine bandobast, VIP security, or crowd control duties except during declared national emergencies.

### 5. Fixed Tenure and Police Establishment Board

(1) The Director General of Police, Superintendents of Police in charge of districts, and Station House Officers shall have a statutory minimum tenure of two years in their respective postings.

(2) An officer referred to in sub-section (1) may be transferred prior to the completion of two years only upon conviction in a criminal case, proven physical incapacity, or by a unanimous recommendation of the Police Establishment Board following a formal inquiry.

(3) All postings, transfers, and promotions of police officers below the rank of Director General of Police shall be decided exclusively by the Police Establishment Board.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Arbitrary political interference, partisan transfers, and conflation of law-and-order duties with criminal investigations severely compromise police integrity, causing low conviction rates and public distrust.

2. The National Police Integrity, Independence, and Autonomy Bill, 2026 codifies the binding directives of the Supreme Court in *Prakash Singh v. Union of India* (2006) 8 SCC 1 by establishing State Security Commissions, guaranteeing a 2-year fixed tenure, and separating criminal investigation from law-and-order.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3 and Clause 5 establish State Security Commissions and Police Establishment Boards.

2. Recurring annual operational expenditure for commission operations is estimated at rupees one hundred crores.
