# Validation & Anti-Corruption Audit Report: National Wealth and Human Capital Decentralization Bill, 2026

## 1. Validation Conclusion

**Conclusion**: **Legally and Constitutionally Robust with Enhanced Anti-Corruption Protections**.

Following a comprehensive audit against hostile-executive, corrupt-official, and captured-agency exploitation scenarios, the Bill has been revised to incorporate an uncompromising anti-corruption framework. The revised statute features independent integrity oversight, real-time digital audit trails, whistleblower protection, Special Anti-Corruption Courts, and severe statutory punishments (including mandatory minimum 10-year to life imprisonment and 300% asset forfeiture for fund misappropriation or collusive valuation fraud).

## 4. Anti-Corruption & Integrity Architecture

The revised Bill introduces a dedicated **Chapter IV: Integrity, Anti-Corruption, Whistleblower Protection and Offences**:

1. **Independent Special Anti-Corruption Directorate**: Headed by a Special Integrity Ombudsperson appointed by a consensus panel (Chief Justice of India, Prime Minister, Leader of Opposition) with autonomous investigative powers.
2. **Mandatory Annual Asset & Conflict Disclosures**: Statutory duty for all Trustees, officers, valuation assessors, and auditors to publicly disclose global personal assets and financial interests annually on an open digital portal.
3. **Severe Statutory Penalties**:
   * *Misappropriation of Citizen Wealth Funds*: Minimum 10 years up to Life Imprisonment + 300% Fine + Civil Forfeiture of all proceeds.
   * *Collusive Tax Evasion & Valuation Fraud*: Minimum 7 years up to 14 years Imprisonment + 300% Tax Penalty + Personal Corporate Director Liability.
   * *Regulatory Corruption in Antitrust Unbundling*: Minimum 7 years Imprisonment + Corporate License Revocation.
4. **Whistleblower Protection & Financial Rewards**: Statutory protection against retaliation and a 10% financial reward (capped at ₹5 Crore) from recovered proceeds for reporting verified valuation fraud or fund misappropriation.
5. **Special Courts & Day-to-Day Trial**: Exclusive Special Courts mandated to conduct trials on a day-to-day basis and complete proceedings within 12 months.

## 5. Stress-Test Matrix & Hostile Scenarios

- **Hostile Executive Scenario**: Executive attempts to bypass anti-corruption checks by issuing informal directions to Trustees. *Result*: Blocked by Clause 18 (unlawful executive interference is a criminal offense) and Clause 16 (special parliamentary entrenchment).
- **Corrupt Assessee Collusion Scenario**: Ultra-rich assessee hides wealth in offshore trusts with assessor assistance. *Result*: Blocked by Clause 20 (joint and several criminal liability for corporate officers and assessors, plus 300% asset forfeiture under PMLA frameworks).
- **Digital Dividend Theft Scenario**: Cyber criminals create fake beneficiary lists. *Result*: Blocked by Clause 6(3) & 18(4) (real-time zero-trust cryptographic transaction verification and mandatory annual CAG audit).
