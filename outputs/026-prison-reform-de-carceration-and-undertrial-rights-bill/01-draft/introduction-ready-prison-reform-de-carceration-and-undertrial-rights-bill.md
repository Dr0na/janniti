# THE PRISON REFORM, DE-CARCERATION, AND UNDERTRIAL RIGHTS BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to enforce the fundamental rights of undertrial prisoners and inmates under Article 21; to mandate automatic statutory bail for undertrials completing one-third of the maximum sentence; to institute a mandatory one hundred per cent certified prison capacity cap; to guarantee fair minimum wages for prison labor; to establish an Independent Board of Visitors; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Prison Reform, De-carceration, and Undertrial Rights Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Certified Prison Capacity" means the maximum inmate population authorized for a prison facility under section 4;

(b) "Independent Board of Visitors" means the judicial and civil society oversight board established under section 5; and

(c) "Undertrial Prisoner" means a person detained in prison undergoing trial or investigation for an offence without conviction.

### 3. Automatic Statutory Bail for Undertrial Prisoners

(1) Every Undertrial Prisoner who has been detained for a period extending to one-third of the maximum period of imprisonment specified for the alleged offence shall be released automatically on personal bond without financial surety.

(2) The Jail Superintendent and District Legal Services Authority shall present the list of eligible undertrials under sub-section (1) to the jurisdictional magistrate weekly for immediate release orders.

### 4. Mandatory One Hundred Per Cent Prison Capacity Cap

(1) No prison or correctional facility shall admit inmates exceeding one hundred per cent of its Certified Prison Capacity.

(2) Where a prison reaches maximum capacity, the State Government shall immediately order the transfer of inmates or apply to the Court for sentence modification and non-custodial community service orders.

### 5. Independent Board of Visitors and Prison Inspection

(1) Every district shall maintain an Independent Board of Visitors comprising a retired Judicial Magistrate, a medical practitioner, and two civil society members.

(2) The Board shall conduct unannounced monthly inspections of all prisons, lockups, and remand homes, inspecting food quality, sanitation, healthcare, and inmate grievances.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Severe overcrowding (over 75% undertrials) and prolonged pre-trial detention violate human dignity and Article 21 (*Bhim Singh* judgment).

2. The Prison Reform, De-carceration, and Undertrial Rights Bill, 2026 mandates automatic bail after 1/3rd sentence completion, caps prison occupancy at 100% capacity, guarantees fair labor wages, and establishes the Independent Board of Visitors.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 5 mandates District Boards of Visitors and legal aid infrastructure.

2. Recurring annual operational expenditure is estimated at rupees two hundred crores.
