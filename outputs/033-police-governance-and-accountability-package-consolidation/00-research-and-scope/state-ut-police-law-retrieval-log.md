# State/UT Police-Law Retrieval Log

## Method and limitation

This log records actual official retrieval attempts. A result establishes only the proposition stated beside it. A failed or empty search is **not** evidence that a State lacks a police law. The remaining jurisdictions remain in the adoption baseline queue until their controlling texts, amendment history, rules and judicial treatment are retrieved from the official sources.
