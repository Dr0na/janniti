## Scope and status

This is a **research-routing and coverage record**, current as of 11 August 2026. The repository’s State/UT source-verification queue validates 144 records: 139 reviewed official source endpoints and 5 legislative-source entries correctly marked not applicable for Union Territories without a legislature. This verifies a route to research; it is **not** a verification of the current police statute, rules, Gazette amendment history or local case law in any jurisdiction.

The direct official endpoint, publisher, review metadata and access limitations for each row are retained in [the verified source queue](../../../knowledge-base/state-ut-source-verification-queue.json). The online health-check attempt in this matter produced no accepted result and is not treated as evidence of current availability or legal effect.

## Mandatory per-jurisdiction retrieval protocol

For each row, retrieve from its listed official portals: (1) the current police Act and every amendment Act; (2) service, transfer, discipline, custody, camera/evidence and complaints rules/orders; (3) Gazette commencement and amendment record; (4) relevant High Court and Supreme Court decisions; (5) current budget/staffing and local-language/accessibility evidence. Save source metadata, immutable accepted snapshot and pinpointed adoption crosswalk before creating a jurisdiction-specific Bill.

No row may be converted from “not yet retrieved” merely because a portal search returns no result.
