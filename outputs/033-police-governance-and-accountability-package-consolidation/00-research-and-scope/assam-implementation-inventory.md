# Assam Police Implementation and Later-Change Inventory

## Status

This is an official-source inventory, retrieved 16 August 2026. It identifies material that must be source-accepted and read before an Assam amendment Bill is drafted. It does not establish the content, validity or continued force of a linked document merely because it appears on an official portal.

| Material | Official source | What the source establishes | Required follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consolidated Assam Police Act page | [Assam Police: The Assam Police Act 2007](https://police.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/the-assam-police-act-2007) | Official Assam Police page lists the 2007 Act and two amendment PDFs; the page was reported updated 25 July 2026. | Download, hash-pin and identify the two amendment Acts; compare against Legislative Department/Gazette text. |
| Police Manual amendments | [Home-A notifications](https://police.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/notifications-of-homea-department) | Official listing identifies Assam Police Manual (Amendment) Rules, 2023 dated 27 January 2023. | Obtain the notification/PDF, Gazette details, affected manual rules and current status. |
| Police Manual and Service amendments | [Home-A notifications](https://police.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/notification-of-home-a) | Official listing identifies Assam Police Manual (Amendment) Rules, 2025 and Assam Police Service (Amendment) Rules, 2025. | Obtain current Gazette texts and determine transfer, discipline, staffing and accountability consequences. |
| Prosecution rules | [Home-A notifications](https://police.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/notifications-of-homea-department) | Official listing identifies a Gazette notification for Assam State Prosecution Service (Amendment) Rules, 2024. | Retrieve exact text and preserve prosecutor-independence boundary. |
| Accountability Commission materials | [Assam Police accountability portal](https://police.assam.gov.in/policies/website-governance-6) | Portal identifies a State Police Accountability Commission governance structure; source is operational information, not a statutory composition finding. | Retrieve current appointment/order, annual reports, rules, complaint forms and publicly available aggregate outcomes. |
| Discipline guidance | [Handbook of Inquiry Officers & Disciplinary Authorities](https://police.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/handbook-of-inquiry-officers-disciplinary-authorities) | Official portal lists a handbook updated 28 July 2026. | Determine whether it is binding, its parent authority, and compatibility with fair-process safeguards. |

## Consequence for the proposed framework

No Assam-specific Bill should create a duplicate transfer, discipline, prosecution or accountability process until this inventory is reduced to accepted current texts with a conflict and savings schedule.
