# Assam Bill Fiscal and Implementation Assessment

## Required costed plan before commencement

1. Independent Commission and Panel staffing, regional access points and secure case system.
2. Legal-aid/enforcement fund and witness/complainant support.
3. Independent medical/forensic access and evidence-custodian capacity.
4. Tamper-evident evidence system, external technical audit and privacy controls.
5. Translation, disability access, offline filing and public-data infrastructure.
6. Investigation/prosecution allocation, training, workforce-rest and equality audit capacity.

## Financial safeguard

The Bill creates no blank-cheque delegated power. Clause 15 requires a published costed plan before commencement; section 87Z requires separately published operating allocations and disclosure of shortfalls. Exact amounts need current Assam budget, staffing and procurement evidence.
