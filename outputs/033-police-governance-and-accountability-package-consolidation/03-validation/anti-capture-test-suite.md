# Anti-Capture Test Suite

Every proposed law must pass these questions before approval:

1. Can one bloc appoint, fund, direct, investigate, prosecute, decide and remove the same body?
2. Can an emergency, secrecy claim, contractor, algorithm or funding condition evade a safeguard?
3. Can political criticism, journalism, protest, identity or association trigger selective enforcement?
4. Is every coercive power defined by legal source, objective trigger, record, independent check, deadline, remedy and expiry?
5. Can an ordinary person safely complain, obtain counsel, preserve evidence and reach a court?
6. Does a right or remedy depend on a discretionary official with no review?
7. Are data, appointments, budgets, conflicts and non-compliance publicly auditable without exposing victims?
8. Are investigation, prosecution, discipline and adjudication genuinely separated?
9. Does the design protect minority rights and due process even against an electoral majority?
10. Does the law retain a peaceful public route—petition, hearing, election, information and court remedy—to correct capture?

A “yes” to questions 1–6 is a High or Critical drafting failure unless an exact structural repair is made.
