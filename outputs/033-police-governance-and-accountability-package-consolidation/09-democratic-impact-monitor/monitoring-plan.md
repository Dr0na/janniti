# Democratic Impact Monitoring Plan — Police Package

## Purpose

Measure whether the adopted State framework reduces arbitrary and retaliatory policing while protecting privacy, equality, public safety, officer wellbeing and fair process. Indicators are proposed design items, not current facts.

| Outcome | Public indicator | Disaggregation / privacy | Independent check | Correction trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Custody safety | Deaths, serious injuries, medical referrals, preservation notices and time to referral. | Small-cell suppression; disability, gender, caste/tribe and language analysis only where lawful and safe. | Medical/forensic and complaints audit. | Unexplained rise, recurring outage or missed preservation deadline triggers public remedial plan. |
| Impartial enforcement | Complaint, arrest, transfer, investigation and prosecution comparator indicators. | De-identified aggregates and methodology. | Independent annual audit. | Material unexplained disparity triggers equality review and published response. |
| Access to remedy | Acknowledgement, referral, closure, review and legal-aid timeliness. | District, language and disability-access data with privacy controls. | Legislative committee and civil-society observer process. | Persistent delay triggers staffing and process correction. |
| Evidence integrity | Outage rate, access-log anomalies, deletion exceptions and preservation-order compliance. | No publication of case evidence. | Independent technical audit. | Tampering or systemic failure triggers procurement review and court notification procedure. |
| Workforce wellbeing | Duty hours, rest exceptions, vacancies, confidential-support uptake in aggregate and injury/burnout indicators. | No individual health disclosure. | Occupational-health reviewer. | Repeated emergency exceptions trigger staffing plan review. |

The adopting State must set baselines, methodology, publication dates, auditor selection and correction authority after consultation. The review under clause 45 must publish its evidence, uncertainty and response.
