# Not-Ready Assam Police Governance and Custody Integrity Amendment Schedule

## Status

This is a **source-accepted drafting schedule**, not an introduction-ready Bill. It implements the non-duplication decision: amend Assam’s existing police-governance architecture rather than create another commission or parallel complaint process. Exact final wording remains subject to accepted-current-text verification of the outstanding Service and Prosecution Rules, Gazette/later-history review, consultation and qualified Assam review.

## A. Assam Police Act, 2007 — proposed amendment instructions

| Existing provision | Verified baseline | Required amendment | Safeguard and reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sections 43–44 — State Security Commission | Existing Commission. | Replace appointment provisions with a published, plural selection process; staggered fixed terms; conflict disclosures; recorded reasons; removal only after notice, hearing and independent review. | No single political bloc may control policy oversight. The Commission remains barred from case-specific policing, investigation, prosecution, discipline and court matters. |
| Sections 43–44 — functions | Existing governance function. | Insert a written-direction, publication and anti-evasion rule: any executive policy direction must state legal basis, public purpose and duration; an indirect or funding-based case direction is void. | Prevent informal political interference while retaining democratically accountable general policy. |
| Sections 45–46 — Police Establishment Board / transfer-posting | Existing Board and transfer framework. | Require published criteria, recorded reasons, conflict rules, periodic equality audit, access to reasons and review. Insert express ban on politically retaliatory transfer/posting/discipline. | Align service integrity with impartial policing; preserve ordinary service administration. |
| Sections 55–63 — investigation units | Existing specialised investigation/CID framework. | Require phased functional-separation plan, workload evidence, forensic capacity, training and annual independent assessment. Add pre-set conflict allocation for police-misconduct and politically sensitive cases. | Use existing units rather than create an unaccountable separate police force. |
| Sections 70–77 — State Police Accountability Commission | Existing State Commission, appointed by State Government. | Keep it as accessible complaint intake, evidence-preservation, referral, monitoring and reporting body only. Add plural appointments, budget transparency, disability/language access, time limits, reasons and review. | The Commission must not investigate, prosecute, discipline, decide criminal guilt or hear its own appeals. |
| Section 78 — serious misconduct | Existing serious-misconduct inquiry jurisdiction. | Require immediate preservation notice and pre-set external investigator referral for custody death, disappearance, torture, sexual violence, serious injury, evidence tampering, retaliatory policing or a conflict in the ordinary unit. | Separates complaint intake from coercive investigation; protects life, liberty and evidence. |
| Sections 83–87 — District Accountability Authorities | 2021/2022 amendments alter functions, staffing and composition. | Repeal and replace the 2022 composition with independent non-police majority, a non-serving independent chair, public criteria, conflict screening and due-process removal. Exclude serving officers from deciding complaints about their chain. | The 2022 design places serving police and political actors in a complaint body, risking capture and conflicts. |
| New custody-evidence chapter | No current exact equivalent verified in this schedule. | Add necessity-based custody record/preservation, confidential-zone exclusions, independent evidence custodian, access logs, retention/deletion, outage reporting, independent audit and urgent court-preservation remedy. | Accountability without blanket surveillance or automatic reverse criminal burden. |
| New remedies / anti-evasion / review clauses | No current exact equivalent verified in this schedule. | Add accessible court remedy; data minimisation; contractor/algorithm anti-evasion; bounded rule-making; three-year independent review and five-year statutory review. | Ensures every power has a record, check, challenge and correction route. |

## B. Assam Police Manual — identified consequential amendments

| Instrument and provision | Verified effect | Drafting consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Manual (Amendment) Rules, 2023, rules 73 and 75 | Changes authority for Inspector transfers/postings within and between districts. | Add a savings and consistency clause: the Manual’s operational competence remains, but every transfer/posting decision must comply with amended Act-level objective criteria, written reasons, anti-retaliation rule and review. |
| Manual (Amendment) Rules, 2025, Part III rule 16 | Changes promotion eligibility for Assistant Sub-Inspector (UB). | Do not amend this rule for the present package; retain it as a service-rule matter. Require that any future promotion criterion undergo equality and accessibility assessment. |

## D. Non-negotiable drafting constraints

- No body may intake a complaint, investigate, prosecute, impose discipline, adjudicate and hear its own appeal.
- No case-specific executive direction; no political-vendetta use of complaint, transfer, arrest, audit or discipline powers.
- Every restriction, evidence measure or exceptional deployment requires a clear legal basis, necessity, proportionality, written reasons, expiry and independent review.
- Preserve constitutional and BNSS remedies; no ouster clause, immunity or secrecy-based evasion.
