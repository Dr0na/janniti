# MODEL STATE POLICE (DEMOCRATIC GOVERNANCE AND CUSTODY INTEGRITY) BILL

> Draft model only. A State/UT must substitute its own police-law sections, verify competence and obtain local review before introduction.

## Core model clauses

1. **Purpose.** Secure impartial, rights-respecting, democratically accountable policing; prevent torture, discrimination, retaliation and institutional capture.
2. **Governance.** Establish a plural State Police Governance Commission that may set transparent general policy and audit aggregate outcomes but may never direct an individual case.
3. **Service integrity.** Require published criteria, reasons, conflict rules, equality audit and review for police appointments, postings, transfers and discipline; prohibit political retaliation.
4. **Functional separation.** Publish a phased investigation/law-and-order separation plan; use conflict-screened random or pre-set allocation for police-misconduct and politically sensitive cases.
5. **Independent complaints.** Create accessible State and district complaint bodies limited to intake, preservation, referral, monitoring and reporting; they may not investigate, prosecute, punish or hear their own appeal.
6. **Custody integrity.** Require accessible arrest/custody information, independent medical access, necessity-based evidence preservation, confidential-zone exclusion, access logs, audits and urgent court preservation.
7. **Consequences.** Require fair disciplinary action and criminal referral for substantiated grave misconduct; protect the criminal burden of proof, hearing, reasons and appeal.
8. **Public power.** Provide citizen petitions, public hearings, advisory lottery-selected oversight panels, open accountability data, legal aid, whistleblower/source protection and public non-compliance reporting.
9. **Anti-capture.** Protect independent appointments, budgets, records, vacancies, audit and court access; prohibit case-specific executive direction, emergency evasion, outsourcing evasion and immunity.
10. **Review.** Require equality/human-rights audit, automatic notice of grave custody events, legislative debate, independent review and sunset/re-enactment for exceptional technology or emergency measures.

Every State adaptation must retain these floors, identify the responsible court and authorities, and add its exact existing-law amendment/repeal schedule.
