# Assam Bill Consultation Kit

Public consultation is pending. Publish the Bill, clause/source table, source snapshots, fiscal assessment and plain-language guide for at least sixty days. Provide Assamese, Bengali, Bodo and English versions prepared by qualified translators; accessible digital/offline formats; confidential submissions; and anti-retaliation protection.

Invite survivors, police personnel, legal-aid providers, medical/forensic experts, disability organisations, caste/tribe/minority organisations, journalists, local governments and rural communities. Publish every material issue raised and a reasoned response before introduction.
