# Public Deliberation Plan — Police Package

Consultation remains pending. Before State adoption, the State should publish the model text, a plain-language guide, source pack, fiscal note, privacy assessment and an accessible response form for at least sixty days.

Required participation includes survivors and families, police personnel and unions/associations, lawyers and legal-aid providers, medical and forensic professionals, disability organisations, Dalit, Adivasi, religious-minority and gender-diverse organisations, children’s-rights groups, journalists, privacy researchers, local governments and rural/remote communities.

Participation must provide confidential channels, translation, sign-language/accessible formats, travel or digital-access support where needed, non-retaliation protection, a published response-to-comments document, and an independent summary of under-represented views. A consultation volume count is not evidence of consent or constitutional validity.
