# Assam Adoption Crosswalk — Model State Police Governance and Custody Integrity Bill

## Bottom line

**Current-law findings are limited to the source-identified provisions below; recommendations are policy options.** Assam already has a police-governance Act with a State Security Commission, Police Establishment Board, specialised investigation units, and police-accountability bodies. The 2022 amendment materially changes the District Accountability Authority. A new parallel complaints body would therefore duplicate and potentially weaken the existing architecture. The correct next design is a targeted Assam amendment package after accepted-current-text, rules and later-history verification.

## Primary-source findings

| Existing Assam provision | Verified limited content | Model Bill module affected | Crosswalk decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2007 Act, sections 43–44 (OCR/visual finding) | Establishes State Security Commission and its membership/functions. | Clauses 4–6, 10. | Amend existing sections only if necessary to strengthen plural appointment, written non-case-direction, transparent publication and independent review. Do not create a second commission. |
| 2007 Act, sections 45–46 (OCR/visual finding) | Establishes Police Establishment Board; addresses complaints by police officers and postings/transfers. | Clause 7. | Targeted amendment: objective criteria, reasons, equality audit, accessible review and express anti-retaliation protection. |
| 2007 Act, sections 55–63 (OCR/visual finding) | Special Crime Investigation Unit and Criminal Investigation Department; specialist/scientific investigation functions. | Clauses 8–9, 29–30. | Build a phased functional-separation and conflict-allocation protocol on these existing units rather than impose a wholly new cadre. |
| 2007 Act, Chapter VII, sections 70–87 (OCR/visual finding) | State Police Accountability Commission; District Accountability Authorities; complaint/inquiry functions. | Clauses 20–28. | Amend the existing system’s jurisdiction, independence, preservation/referral process, access and remedy; do not create a new “Authority”. |
| 2007 Act, section 78 (OCR/visual finding) | Commission may inquire into serious misconduct, including custody death and due-process arrest/detention allegations. | Clauses 16, 22–25. | Add automatic evidence-preservation and pre-set external investigation referral; protect against investigation/prosecution/discipline concentration. |
| 2021 Act, sections 2–5 (Assam Act XLI of 2021; OCR/visual finding) | Amends sections 76, 78, 83 and 85 of the 2007 Act. It changes Commission staffing, narrows/relabels elements of section 78 serious-misconduct handling, and defines “misconduct” in section 85 as a wilful breach or neglect of applicable law/rule/regulation adversely affecting a member of the public. | Clauses 20–28. | Use the accepted 2021 text as the starting point for any definition, staffing and referral reform. Do not recreate the pre-2021 complaint scope without an evidence-based, rights-tested decision. |
| 2022 Act, section 2 (amending section 84) | Replaces District Accountability Authority composition. Its ex officio chair is the range DIG/IG or Guwahati Commissioner; it includes police and political/public representatives, State-nominated members and a nominated social worker with legal background. | Clauses 20–26. | High capture/conflict risk. Replace case-specific complaint decision roles of serving police with an independent chair and non-police majority selected through a published plural process; set fixed terms and due-process removal. |
| 2022 Act, section 3 (amending section 85(2)) | Permits the District Authority to cause inquiry by an officer entrusted by the Authority. | Clauses 22, 25–26. | Specify pre-set conflict screening, investigator independence, chain of custody, prosecutor separation and court-access review. |
| 2022 Act, section 4 (amending section 87(3)) | Commission may request State Government to initiate removal proceedings where section 84(4) conditions are met. | Clauses 5, 21, 26. | Add independent removal panel, notice, hearing, published reasons and judicial review; avoid executive removal leverage. |

## Proposed Assam-specific amendment sequence

1. Confirm the accepted consolidated text of the 2007 Act, Assam Act XLI of 2021, Assam Act XLIV of 2022 and any later amendments, and the applicable rules/orders.
2. Amend existing sections 43–46 and 70–87 rather than duplicate institutions.
3. Add a custody-evidence chapter only after BNSS/evidence-law and privacy interactions are precisely checked.
4. Add a statutory anti-evasion, anti-vendetta, preservation and urgent-court-remedy clause.
5. Publish cost, staffing, privacy, disability/language-access and equality impact assessments before introduction.
