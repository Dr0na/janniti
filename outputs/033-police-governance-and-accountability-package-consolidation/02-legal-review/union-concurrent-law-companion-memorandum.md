# Union/Concurrent-Law Companion Memorandum

## Scope

Police organisation remains principally State law. Parliament may consider a separately verified Concurrent-list package only for criminal procedure, evidence, prosecution, custody safeguards and remedies, subject to Articles 246 and 254 and exact current-text verification.

## Candidate measures

| Subject | National minimum | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Custody records and medical care | Prompt documented safeguards, independent care and preservation. | Preserve BNSS rights; no reverse criminal burden. |
| Evidence preservation | Court-accessible preservation order, tamper-evident logs and privacy controls. | No blanket surveillance; confidential zones excluded. |
| Serious police misconduct | Conflict-screened independent investigation and prosecutorial reasons. | State police organisation remains State-controlled. |
| Remedies | Legal aid, witness/complainant protection and enforceable court remedies. | No ouster clause or immunity. |
| Data | National interoperable aggregate reporting standard. | Minimisation, de-identification and independent audit. |

No Union Bill should direct ordinary State transfers, postings, police-command structure or local complaints-body composition without a valid constitutional route.
