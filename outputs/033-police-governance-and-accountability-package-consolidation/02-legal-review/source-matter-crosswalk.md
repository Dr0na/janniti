# Source-Matter Crosswalk and Package Boundary Decision

## Bottom line

This is a **policy and drafting decision**, not current law. Matters 016–021 should not be merged into one purported Union Act. Their common objective can be retained through one State-adoptable framework with bounded modules and a separately researched Union/Concurrent procedure memorandum. The original drafts remain historical source artefacts and are not overwritten.

| Source matter / provision | Retain, modify or retire | Destination | Reason and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 016, State Security Commission | Modify | State Governance Module | Retain transparent policy, performance and non-interference functions; exclude case direction, investigation, prosecution and adjudication. |
| 016, investigation/law-and-order separation | Modify | State Governance Module | Require a phased, evidence-based functional separation plan rather than an absolute national cadre mandate. |
| 016, tenure and Police Establishment Board | Modify | State Governance Module | Retain published, reasoned, reviewable transfer/posting criteria; no board may be insulated from court review or equality audit. |
| 017, ban on torture and immediate safeguards | Retain and align | Custody Integrity Module / BNSS amendment memorandum | Retain zero tolerance for torture and prompt counsel, medical, notification and production safeguards, but cross-check BNSS exact text. |
| 017, reverse burden, mandatory death penalty and fixed compensation | Retire as drafted | Remedy design note | They risk fair-trial, proportionality and fiscal defects. Use preservation duties, independent investigation, ordinary burden of proof, judicial remedy and victim support instead. |
| 018, CCTV and body cameras | Modify | Custody Evidence and Data Module | Preserve relevant evidence under necessity, privacy, access-log, retention and independent-audit controls. No blanket recording or automatic guilt inference. |
| 018, Digital Audit Authority | Retire as drafted | Existing external audit/complaint architecture | A new single technology authority would duplicate oversight and create a data-power concentration. |
| 019, complaints authority | Modify | Independent Complaints and Referral Module | Retain accessible intake, preservation, transparency and review. It must not simultaneously investigate, prosecute, decide discipline and hear appeal. |
| 019, direct FIR/investigation and binding outcomes | Modify | Referral protocol | Complaint body refers by predetermined allocation; independent investigator gathers evidence; prosecutor and disciplinary authority decide under separate processes; court review remains open. |
| 020, independent prosecution directorate | Modify | Prosecution Independence Module | Retain written-direction ban, comparator audits and reasoned decisions; appointment and operational design require State/Concurrent-law research. |
| 020, universal pre-arrest judicial scrutiny and automatic compensation recovery | Retire as drafted | BNSS review memorandum | Both require exact statutory and constitutional analysis; they cannot be inserted as a generic State-police rule. |
| 021, shifts, rest, mental-health and housing | Modify | Workforce Wellbeing Module | Retain minimum wellbeing planning, fatigue records, confidential care, staffing plans and review; avoid an unfunded national board and a rigid one-size schedule. |

## Non-duplication rule

For an allegation concerning a police officer, one matter identifier follows the person from complaint through closure. The intake body may preserve and refer; the assigned investigator investigates; the independent prosecutor makes prosecution decisions; the disciplinary authority decides service consequences; and a court provides judicial remedies. Each must share only the minimum recorded information necessary for its legal role. No actor may issue a case-specific direction outside its assigned role.

## Proposed package outputs

1. Model State Police Governance and Custody Integrity Bill — the framework and five modules above.
2. Union/Concurrent Criminal Procedure Safeguards Memorandum — only after exact BNSS, evidence-law, Gazette and State consultation verification.
3. State adoption matrix — one row for every State and Union Territory, mapping its police statute, complaints regime, service law, rules, budget route and local court authority.

## Completeness blockers

- State-by-State primary-law retrieval and route decision.
- Exact official source acceptance for any Union/Concurrent change.
- Fiscal, staffing, retention, accessibility and privacy impact evidence.
- Consultation with survivors, police personnel, disability and language-access groups, lawyers, forensic/medical professionals, journalists and State institutions.
