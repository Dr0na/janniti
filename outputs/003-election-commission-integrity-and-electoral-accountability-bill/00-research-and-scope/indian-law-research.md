# Indian Law Landscape Research Pack: Election Commission Integrity, ECI Accountability, Voter Roll Protection, and Paper Ballot Auditing

## 1. Scope and search record

- Matter ID: 003
- Topic and objective: Formulate a comprehensive Constitutional Amendment Bill ("The Constitution (One Hundred and Thirty-Seventh Amendment) Bill, 2026") and companion statutory legislation ("The Election Commission Integrity, Voter Protection, and Electoral Accountability Act, 2026") to reform the appointment, security of tenure, accountability, voter roll integrity, and voting procedures of the Election Commission of India (ECI). The law must establish a multi-partisan selection committee (Prime Minister, Leader of Opposition, and Chief Justice of India), insulate Election Commissioners equally with the CEC under Article 324(5), mandate 100% VVPAT paper slip counting / paper ballot options, criminalize arbitrary voter deletions, introduce severe penal accountability for complicit election officers, enforce 2/3rd parliamentary supermajority entrenchment for election laws, and establish independent judicial audit mechanisms.
- Jurisdiction(s): Union of India (All-India constitutional and statutory application across Parliamentary and State Legislative Assembly elections).
- As-of date: 2026-07-27
- Search terms and synonyms: Election Commission of India, Chief Election Commissioner, Anoop Baranwal, Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act 2023, Article 324, voter deletion, EVM VVPAT counting, Subramanian Swamy, Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR), paper ballot, electoral roll audit, supermajority entrenchment.
- Requested legal question: How can India constitutionally guarantee the absolute independence and public accountability of the ECI and CEC, prevent voter list manipulation, establish auditable voting procedures, and enforce severe penal sanctions for electoral fraud?
- Sources/repositories searched and retrieval dates: India Code (indiacode.nic.in), Constitution of India official text (legislative.gov.in), Supreme Court of India judgments (sci.gov.in).

## 2. Executive finding and reliance limits

The legal framework governing elections in India faces critical constitutional vulnerabilities:
1. **Appointment Capture (CEC & ECs Act, 2023)**: Following the Supreme Court's landmark ruling in *Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India* (2023 6 SCC 161)—which instituted a selection panel comprising the PM, LOP, and CJI—Parliament enacted the 2023 Act replacing the CJI with a Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM. This gives the ruling executive a guaranteed 2:1 majority, compromising institutional neutrality.
2. **Asymmetry in Protection**: Under Article 324(5), only the CEC enjoys Supreme Court judge-level removal protection; Election Commissioners can be removed on the recommendation of the CEC, creating intra-commission vulnerability.
3. **Voter Roll Deletion Vulnerability**: The Representation of the People Act 1950 (RPA 1950) lacks mandatory prior personal notice and independent judicial appeal before voter names are removed, enabling mass arbitrary deletions.
4. **Voting Machine Transparency Deficit**: Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) lack full 100% physical paper slip verification under current MoEF/ECI rules (*ADR v. ECI* 2024 SC).

A Constitutional Amendment under Article 368 is required to entrench selection panel neutrality, equalize EC removal protections, mandate 100% VVPAT/paper ballot options, enforce strict voter roll audit protocols, and institute supermajority amendment safeguards.

## 5. State and Union Territory coverage

Elections to all 28 State Legislative Assemblies and Legislative Councils, as well as Union Territories with legislatures (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K), are conducted under Central election laws. Overhauling ECI structure under Article 368 requires ratification by at least 50% of State Assemblies.

## 6. Bills, amendments, ordinances, history, and Gazette

- Law Commission of India Report No. 255 (2015) recommended a 3-member Selection Committee (PM, LOP, CJI) for ECI appointments and equal removal protections for ECs under Article 324(5).
- Goswami Committee on Electoral Reforms (1990) recommended multi-member ECI and statutory selection panel.

## 7. Delegated law and implementation

Subordinate rules under the Conduct of Elections Rules 1961 currently permit sample VVPAT counting (5 polling stations per assembly segment). Proposed legislation mandates 100% VVPAT paper slip counting and paper ballot voting options.

## 9. Rights, equality, federalism, and public-interest assessment

- **Popular Sovereignty & Basic Structure**: Free and fair elections represent the ultimate democratic public good. Arbitrary voter deletions or technological black-boxes violate Article 19(1)(a) and Article 21.
- **Due Process & Retrospective Prosecution**: Retrospective accountability for past intentional electoral fraud is enforced under existing offences of corruption, forgery, and criminal conspiracy (PCA 1988 & IPC/BNS), complying with Article 20(1).
- **Federal Equilibrium**: Entrenches election law amendments under Article 368, requiring a 2/3rd supermajority of total parliamentary seats (ignoring MP suspensions) + 2/3rd State Assembly ratification.
