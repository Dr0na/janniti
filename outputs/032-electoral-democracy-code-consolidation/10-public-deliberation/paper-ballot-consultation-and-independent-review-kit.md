# Paper-Ballot Consultation and Independent Review Kit

## Consultation question

How should India implement the statutory transition to accessible, secret, secure and auditable paper-ballot voting while preventing intimidation, invalid votes, capture, delay and discrimination?

## Publication set

Publish the Bill, this kit, the operations/fiscal plan, redline matrix, complaint specification, power map, stress test, source crosswalk and a plain-language comparison of paper ballots and the current EVM/VVPAT framework. All materials require English, Hindi and relevant State/UT languages, easy-read, screen-reader, captioned, sign-language, low-data, telephone and offline versions.[^bill]

[^bill]: Internal draft-text reference: `01-draft/not-ready-electoral-democracy-code-bill.md`, version reviewed on 2026-08-11. It is not an external legal authority.

## Required participants and routes

| Group | Route | Safeguard against capture |
|---|---|---|
| Voters in rural, urban, remote and conflict-affected areas | Facilitated local hearings, post, phone and offline forms. | Randomly sampled invitations plus open participation. |
| Disabled voters and carers | Paid accessibility workshops and independent usability tests. | Accessibility organisations choose their representatives. |
| Election staff and returning officers | Protected technical sessions and anonymous operational submissions. | No discipline, transfer or reprisal for a good-faith submission. |
| Parties, candidates and agents | Equal written/hearing opportunity and published lobbying disclosure. | No party receives privileged draft access. |
| Journalists, civil society and researchers | Open evidence hearings and data room. | Publish conflicts, methods and response to evidence. |
| State/UT governments and local bodies | Formal implementation hearing for every State and UT. | Central response must identify each accepted/rejected proposal and reason. |

## Questions to answer with evidence

1. What printing, storage, transport and count model best protects ballots in each geography?
2. What accessibility arrangements allow independent secret marking by every voter?
3. What recount threshold and decision timeline prevent both fraud and strategic delay?
4. How should party agents, media and observers inspect without exposing votes or endangering staff?
5. What cost, environmental and workforce assumptions are realistic and independently reproducible?
6. Which existing paper-ballot and EVM/VVPAT safeguards must be retained, strengthened or repealed?

## Independent review brief

An independent panel of election-law counsel, disability-access specialists, election administrators, security/logistics experts, statisticians and public-finance specialists must publish:

- conflicts and methods before review;
- evidence relied on and uncertainty;
- adversarial testing results;
- minority/disagreement statement;
- exact proposed redlines; and
- a public response to every material consultation theme.

Neither the Commission, government, political party, vendor nor panel may treat consultation as a referendum on equal rights or as authority to suppress criticism.
