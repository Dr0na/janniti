# Consultation Publication Pack — Electoral Consolidation

Publish before the 90-day consultation begins:

1. Plain-language explanation of each retained objective and each excluded prior mechanism.
2. The provisional blueprint, crosswalk, power map, stress test, research pack, citation-gap register and fiscal/evidence worksheet.
3. Alternatives: retain separate Bills; consolidate statutory machinery only; constitutional amendment plus statute; implementation/rule reform without new primary legislation.
4. Accessible English, Hindi and relevant State/UT language versions; screen-reader, easy-read, captioned, sign-language, low-data, phone and offline routes.
5. A privacy notice, anti-harassment policy, moderation methodology, conflict/lobbying disclosure, and response-to-comments template.

The consultation must not be represented as a referendum on constitutional rights or as authority to discriminate, retaliate, censor, or remove judicial remedies.
