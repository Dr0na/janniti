# Interim Primary-Source Register — Electoral Consolidation

**Status:** research in progress; not a complete research pack and not a drafting authority.

| Political-finance transparency | [Association for Democratic Reforms v Union of India, 2024 INSC 113, Supreme Court](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2017/27935/27935_2017_1_1501_50573_Judgement_15-Feb-2024.pdf) | Official judgment is a required baseline for any donor-disclosure/financial-transparency redesign. | Verify exact holdings, operative directions, subsequent implementation and any later authority. |

A consolidated measure needs evidence for any expanded audit and must not presume that a particular count method is constitutionally compelled. | Retrieve Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961; court authority; accessibility and operational-cost evidence. |

## Research blockers before drafting

1. Current text and legislative history of the Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023.
2. Current RPA 1950, RPA 1951, Conduct of Elections Rules 1961, Election Symbols Order 1968, and ECI instructions.
3. Verified Supreme Court holdings and later history on anti-defection, ECI appointment, political finance, voting audit, and due process.
4. State/UT source review of implementation rules, notifications, courts, languages, disability access, and local-body interfaces.
5. Fiscal, privacy, retaliation-risk, and public-consultation evidence for disclosure, audit, and party-governance proposals.
