# Indian Law Landscape Research Pack — Electoral Democracy Consolidation

## 1. Scope and search record

- Matter ID: 032
- Topic and objective: consolidate proposed anti-defection, Election Commission, voter-roll, voting-audit, political-finance, campaign, and party-governance measures without losing democratic safeguards.
- Jurisdiction(s): India; Union law with State/UT election-administration interfaces.
- As-of date: 2026-08-11.
- Search terms: Tenth Schedule; RPA 1950; RPA 1951; electoral roll; EVM; VVPAT; paper ballot; Conduct of Elections Rules 1961; CEC Act 2023; party registration; election symbols; political finance.
- Sources/repositories searched and retrieval dates: India Code, ECI, Supreme Court, and the State/UT routing registry on 2026-08-11.

## 2. Executive finding and reliance limits

Existing Union law already regulates electoral rolls, party registration, ECI appointments, election procedure and symbols. Consolidation should be by targeted constitutional amendment plus statutory amendments, not a parallel Code that displaces existing remedies. State/UT source routing is complete (139 verified records; 5 not applicable), but topic-specific State/UT rules and implementation documents have not yet been retrieved; no conclusion about their content is made.

## 9. Rights, equality, federalism, and public-interest assessment

Reform must protect equal voting access, accessible notice/hearing and appeal, political association, voter information, privacy and protection from political retaliation. Measures targeting complaints, donations, parties, election officials or voting technology require objective thresholds, independent adjudication, reasons, proportionate sanctions and judicial remedies. State/UT implementation must account for language, disability, digital exclusion and local capacity.
