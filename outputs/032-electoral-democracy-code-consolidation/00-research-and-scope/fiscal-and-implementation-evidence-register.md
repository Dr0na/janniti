# Fiscal and Implementation Evidence Register

| Workstream | Evidence required before final drafting | Cost/implementation decision |
|---|---|---|
| Roll notices and appeals | current volume, delivery channels, language/disability access, appeal disposal time, legal-aid need | choose funded minimum service standard and phased commencement |
| Audit and voting technology | equipment lifecycle, accessibility, security testing, audit sample evidence, counting impact, independent technical review | do not mandate a technology or count method without evidence |
| Political-finance disclosure | reporting burden, donor-retaliation risk, privacy controls, verification capacity, data-security assessment | publish proportionate threshold and protected-disclosure exceptions |
| Party-governance compliance | party size/diversity, member access, dispute process, ECI capacity, appeal workload | use graduated sanctions and funded independent review |
| Enforcement/remedies | complaint volume, investigation/prosecution separation, court capacity, legal aid, compensation risk | provide costed staffing and no-double-process protocol |
| State/UT delivery | CEO/ERO staffing, local languages, rural/offline access, court geography, training | conditional grants and minimum interoperable standards, not unfunded mandates |
