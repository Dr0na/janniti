## Mandatory authenticated redline packet

Before filing, the drafter must place in this matter:

1. A clean official English source copy and hash for each instrument in PB-R01 to PB-R08.
2. A side-by-side redline: current text, deletion/insertion, reason, authority, commencement, transition, and affected form/manual.
3. A Gazette-history check showing every amendment in force on the filing date.
4. A legal certificate that no conflict remains between the Bill, rules, election-petition remedies and [constitutional jurisdiction](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/10232?locale=en).
5. An accessibility, fiscal and State/UT implementation sign-off record.

Until then, Bill sections 5 and 19 are a complete policy architecture but not an authenticated final amendment schedule.[^bill]
