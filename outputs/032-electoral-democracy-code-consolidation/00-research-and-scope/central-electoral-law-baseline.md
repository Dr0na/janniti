## Immediate drafting implications

- Preserve and improve the existing roll-correction and appeal framework rather than create a parallel route.
- Separate constitutional choices (Articles/Tenth Schedule) from statutory choices (appointment procedure, roll administration, finance, symbols and offences).
- Do not retain retrospective punishment, automatic loss of seat, or complaint-criminalisation language without a dedicated constitutional and due-process analysis.
- The requested paper-ballot transition is a policy choice. The official 2024 Supreme Court EVM/VVPAT judgment does not establish the asserted recent manipulation; final drafting needs current independent evidence on accessibility, security, fiscal cost, staffing, counting time and State/UT capacity.

## Open gates

The verified holding and later history of the relevant Supreme Court decisions, State/UT implementation map, and full delegated-law crosswalk remain outstanding.
