# Verified Case-Law Register — Electoral Consolidation

**Status:** limited, source-linked register; retrieve full texts and later history before relying on any proposition in a Bill.

|---|---|---|---|
| *Keisham Meghachandra Singh v Hon'ble Speaker, Manipur Legislative Assembly*, Civil Appeals 547–550 of 2020, judgment 21 January 2020 | Supreme Court judgment concerns Tenth Schedule disqualification proceedings and Speaker delay. | Retain timely and independent resolution as an objective, but do not substitute automatic deprivation of membership for an adjudicative process without further constitutional analysis. | [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/27562/27562_2019_4_1501_19772_Judgement_21-Jan-2020.pdf) |

Do not quote or treat the interim arrangement as current statute. | [Official Supreme Court material identifying the decision](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/146/146_2024_2_1501_51762_Judgement_22-Mar-2024.pdf) |
| *Association for Democratic Reforms v Union of India*, 2024 INSC 113 | Official judgment is the electoral-bonds political-finance decision; its text expressly addresses voter choice and political-party funding. | Political-finance disclosure must be designed against its exact reasoning, directions, privacy concerns, and later implementation—not merely an asserted ban on all anonymous giving. | [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2017/27935/27935_2017_1_1501_50573_Judgement_15-Feb-2024.pdf) |
