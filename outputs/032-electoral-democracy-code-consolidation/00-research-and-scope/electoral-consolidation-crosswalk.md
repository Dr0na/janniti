## Required research questions before drafting

1. Current Tenth Schedule text, Articles 75, 102, 164, 191, 324, 326, 329 and 368; Schedule VII competence; basic-structure and election-independence constraints.
2. Current Representation of the People Acts, election-finance rules, Election Symbols Order, 2023 Election Commissioner statute, and relevant Gazette changes.
3. Controlling Supreme Court authority on defection adjudication, voter information, election finance, election-commission appointments, voting technology, criminal due process, and retrospective penal law.
4. Election Commission operational material on roll revision, notice/appeal, VVPAT audit, candidate/party reporting, and complaint disposition.
5. A State/UT implementation map for roll administration, campaign enforcement, courts, languages, disability access, and local bodies.

## Non-negotiable drafting boundaries

- No automatic loss of seat, criminal liability, adverse election consequence, or financial penalty without notice, disclosed evidence, independent adjudication, reasons, proportionate sanction, and judicial remedy.
- No offence for criticism, unsuccessful complaints, or ordinary political activity. Bad-faith process abuse requires a high, objectively proved threshold and a proportionate, reviewable civil or procedural response.
- No design may give the executive, a party, or the Election Commission itself unchecked control over investigation, adjudication, disclosure, or removal.
- Political-finance transparency must protect donors from unlawful retaliation while preserving public information necessary for democratic accountability.
- Any voting-audit measure must be evidence-based, accessible, independently auditable, and proportionate; it cannot be asserted as constitutionally required without primary-source support.

## Completion condition

Research pack, source manifest, constitutional-stress-test record, institutional power map, and public-deliberation plan must be complete before a consolidated instrument is drafted.
