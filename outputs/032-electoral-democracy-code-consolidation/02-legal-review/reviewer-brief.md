# Independent Reviewer Brief — Electoral Consolidation

## Question

Can a narrow constitutional amendment and statutory Electoral Democracy Code lawfully consolidate the objectives of Matters 002, 003 and 023 while preserving free and fair elections, equality, political participation, due process, federalism, judicial review, and electoral independence?

## Materials supplied

- Research pack and manifest in `00-research-and-scope/`.
- Consolidation crosswalk and provisional blueprint.
- Citation-gap register, institutional power map, stress test, and public-deliberation plan.

## Reviewer decisions requested

1. Identify which objectives require constitutional amendment, ordinary amendment, rules, or non-legislative implementation.
2. Confirm legislative competence and Article 368/State-ratification implications.
3. Review Tenth Schedule, appointment, finance, roll, audit, party-governance and enforcement proposals for due process, proportionality, privacy, equality and basic-structure risk.
4. Verify case-law ratio/later history and statutory/rule pinpoints.
5. Identify any provision that should be removed, narrowed, or subjected to additional court/independent review.

## Required sign-off record

For each finding: source, pinpoint, recommendation, unresolved risk, whether drafting may proceed, and whether public consultation materials need change. The reviewer must be independent and disclose conflicts.
