# Appointment-Reform Filing Record

## Purpose

Bill section 16 directly substitutes sections 6 and 7 of the [Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19721?view_type=browse).[^bill] This record identifies the filing checks required before calling that amendment final.

[^bill]: Internal draft-text reference: `01-draft/not-ready-electoral-democracy-code-bill.md`, version reviewed on 2026-08-11. It is not an external legal authority.

| Filing check | Draft solution | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| Legislative route | Ordinary parliamentary amendment to the 2023 Act is proposed under [Article 324(2)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/10232?locale=en). | Current constitutional-competence opinion; check whether any connected change requires constitutional amendment. |
| Search process | Independent four-member Search Panel, public call, conflict screening, record and reasons.[^bill] | Exact current ss. 5–7 text; appointment/administrative consequences; current litigation status. |
| Selection process | Five-member plural Committee; three votes including two non-executive members; advance record and published reasons/votes.[^bill] | Confirm consistency with Article 324, privacy law, service conditions and judicial-independence principles. |
| Process review | Expedited review of legality, procedure, equality, reasons and proportionality; no judicial choice of candidate.[^bill] | Jurisdictional wording and court-rules capacity assessment. |
| Removal/term | Fixed non-renewable term and independent inquiry are set for the Bill’s Review Authority, not altered for Commissioners by implication.[^bill] | Separate source-backed analysis of [Article 324(5)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/10232?locale=en) and the 2023 Act before any Commissioner term/removal change. |

## Required filing attachments

1. Current official English text of the [2023 Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19721?view_type=browse), especially sections 5–7, with Gazette history and hash.
2. Final side-by-side amendment schedule and a plain-language explanation of why the executive cannot control the process alone.[^bill]
3. Current Supreme Court case-status record for challenges to [section 7](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/146/146_2024_2_1501_51762_Judgement_22-Mar-2024.pdf) and an adverse-authority note.
4. Privacy and personal-safety protocol for candidate disclosures, with court-controlled redaction process.
5. Fiscal and administrative memorandum for the Search Panel, Committee secretariat and expedited review.

## Anti-capture acceptance test

No person, party, executive bloc, court, regulator or interest group may control nomination, shortlist, appointment, funding, direction and removal. The final appointment package must show the holder, trigger, record, checker, challenge, remedy and expiry for each lever.
