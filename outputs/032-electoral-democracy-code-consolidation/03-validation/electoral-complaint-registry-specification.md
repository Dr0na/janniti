# Electoral Complaint Registry and Fast-Remedy Specification

## Public register fields

The section 17 register must publish, in searchable and downloadable form.[^bill]

[^bill]: Internal draft-text reference: `01-draft/not-ready-electoral-democracy-code-bill.md`, version reviewed on 2026-08-11. It is not an external legal authority.

| Field | Privacy rule |
|---|---|
| Unique case number and timestamps | Always public. |
| Issue category, constituency and authority | Public; do not reveal a protected complainant’s address or contact details. |
| Alleged provision/MCC category | Public. |
| Action taken, closure code, reasons and deadline performance | Public, with court-controlled redaction only. |
| Conflict/recusal and allocation information | Public in anonymised or safety-protective form. |
| Review, interim-protection and judicial outcome | Public, with lawful personal-data redaction. |
| Comparator statistics | Public and disaggregated by geography, party/candidate category where lawful, allegation type, result and delay. |

## Service standards

- Acknowledge within two working days.
- Reasoned decision or public progress record within seven working days.
- One further period of no more than seven working days only through an Independent Review Authority order based on recorded facts.
- Interim-protection request decided within seven working days.
- Offline, telephone, postal, disability-accessible and language-accessible submission routes are equal to the online route.

## Integrity controls

1. Random or published pre-set allocation; every deviation has a reason, expiry and review route.
2. Tamper-evident log; public change history; independent periodic export to the auditor.
3. No complaint may be closed solely because it concerns a powerful person, is politically sensitive, or criticises government.
4. No public accusation or coercive step follows an anonymous input without independently corroborated evidence and separate escalation approval.
5. Deliberate fabrication is addressed under generally applicable law and fair process; good-faith complaints remain protected.

## Remedy dashboard

Quarterly publication must show acknowledgement time, decision time, closure reasons, escalation approvals, interim relief, refusals, reversals, recusal, equality indicators, retaliation complaints, audit findings and corrective action. The auditor samples closed and unacted cases, not only completed enforcement cases.
