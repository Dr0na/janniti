# Anti-misuse Safeguards — Validation Record

## Bottom line

**Robust with conditions.** The revised provisional Bill materially reduces the most foreseeable routes for partisan, retaliatory or opaque misuse. It does not make misuse impossible: the exact amendment route, constitutional competence, current rule text, implementation capacity, public consultation and independent qualified legal review remain unresolved before introduction.

**Scope.** India; Union electoral-law framework with State/UT implementation interfaces; reviewed as at 2026-08-11. This record assesses draft text, not enacted law.

## Rights, equality and anti-vendetta assessment

The safeguards protect political participation, association, expression, journalism, protest, privacy, equal protection and access to remedy. They do not grant immunity for bribery, coercion, violence, fabrication or other independently evidenced wrongdoing. Any enforcement step still requires the ordinary applicable legal elements, neutral criteria, reasons, review and remedy.

## Residual risks and required gates

1. The statutory creation, selection, removal and funding design for the Review Authority needs an exact constitutional and legislative-competence check.
2. The Bill must be converted into a provision-by-provision amendment schedule against current official statutory and rule text.
3. No operational safeguard substitutes for trained staff, accessible legal aid, secure records, independent journalism, public reporting or a functioning court system.
4. Public consultation and independent qualified legal review remain pending by instruction; they must test whether the safeguards work for voters, candidates, parties, journalists, civil society, State/UT authorities and people facing language, disability, digital or economic barriers.
