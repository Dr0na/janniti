# Paper-Ballot Operations and Fiscal Plan

## Implementation outcome

By the statutory transition date, every polling station must be able to issue, secure, count and reconcile paper ballots without excluding voters or compromising secrecy. The plan uses public standards and distributed supervision; it does not give any one agency a power to certify itself compliant.

## Delivery sequence

| Phase | Deadline from assent | Deliverable | Accountable owner | Independent check |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Rule package | Day 180 | Gazette rules, forms and manuals matching the redline matrix. | Central Government and ECI. | Parliamentary laying, public comments and judicial review. |
| Capacity inventory | Day 210 | Constituency-level ballot, storage, transport, staffing, accessibility and counting-space inventory. | Chief Electoral Officers. | Public dataset and State/UT audit. |
| Procurement and custody | Day 270 | Neutral tender, serial-control register, ballot-paper security specification, warehouse map and transport protocol. | ECI/CEOs. | CAG-style external audit; party-agent observation. |
| Workforce and accessibility | Day 300 | Training completion, reserve staffing, language roster, disability-access equipment and voter-help channels. | ECI/CEOs. | Disability organisations and public test observers. |
| Public dry runs | Day 330 | Open simulations of polling, custody, count, recount and challenge workflow. | ECI/ROs. | Independent observers, parties, media and published defect register. |
| Statutory transition | Day 365 | Paper ballots exclusively used for every newly notified election. | ECI/ROs. | Independent Review Authority, courts and public record. |

## Minimum operational controls

1. Each ballot paper has a non-voter-identifying serial-control method; no record may link a voter to a marked ballot.
2. Printing, delivery, polling-station handover, sealing, transport, storage, count and post-result retention each require a two-person record and candidate-agent access.
3. Every count begins with reconciliation of ballot papers printed, issued, cancelled, tendered, spoiled, unused and found in each ballot box.
4. Rejected-ballot reasons use a closed published code set and are announced and recorded at the count.
5. A recount request receives a written decision before declaration where feasible; refusal is immediately reviewable under the Bill and preserved for an election petition.[^bill]
6. No camera, phone, facial-recognition system or ballot serial may be used to discover a voter’s choice.
7. Every polling station supplies an offline complaint route, large-print/language assistance, a privacy screen and disability-access equipment.

## Fiscal framework

No cost figure is asserted without evidence. Each CEO must publish a costed estimate using the following categories and assumptions:

| Cost head | Measurement basis | Publication requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Ballot printing and secure stock | ballots per elector, contingency rate, security stock, printing distance | unit price, vendor, tender, wastage and audit result |
| Storage and transport | ballot boxes, seals, warehouses, routes, guards and insurance | site capacity, route risk, vendor and incident record |
| Personnel | poll staff, count staff, accessibility aides, trainers and legal-aid staff | headcount, duty days, pay standard, vacancy and reserve rate |
| Accessibility and language | templates, large print, tactile guides, interpreters and voter help | eligible polling stations and user-testing result |
| Counting/recount capacity | tables, lighting, space, agents, observers and court support | forecast count duration and peak-case plan |
| Audit and public information | custody audit, publication platform, complaint line and independent review | scope, methodology, corrective-action completion |

## Non-negotiable funding safeguards

- Funds are allocated by published constituency-neutral criteria, not political affiliation or anticipated electoral outcome.
- Every material variance from the approved estimate is published within seven days with reasons and review route.
- No procurement vendor may print ballots, supply custody equipment and independently audit its own work.
- State/UT implementation grants carry a minimum-access standard and public outcomes, not political conditions.

## Readiness gate

The transition cannot be called operationally ready until every State/UT has published its inventory, cost estimate, accessibility plan, dry-run result, unresolved-defect register and contact for complaints. This gate reports readiness; it cannot postpone the Bill’s statutory prohibition unless Parliament amends the law.[^bill]
