## C. Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961

The current official Rules contain both pre-existing paper-ballot procedures and a separate EVM regime. The final Gazette rule package must preserve and modernise paper-ballot provisions rather than repeal shared safeguards. The following is the required side-by-side instruction to the rule-maker.

| Current rule/form | Current verified effect | Required final Gazette action | Replacement/retained safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part IV, Chapter II, rules 49A–49V | EVM design, preparation, voting, VVPAT, machine sealing/transmission and associated records; some provisions also carry forward general polling safeguards. | Repeal EVM-only text. Re-enact only the general safeguards needed for paper ballots in a dedicated paper-ballot chapter. | Keep privacy compartments, identity checks, challenged/tendered vote safeguards, agent access, poll close, sealing of papers, packet records and adjourned-poll continuity. |
| Rule 49P | Uses a tendered **paper ballot** in an EVM chapter. | Move intact in substance to the paper-ballot chapter; update cross-references to the new ballot-issue and record forms. | Tendered vote, assistance and sealed cover remain available. |
| Rule 49S and Form 17C | Creates account of votes recorded; current Form 17C contains EVM/VVPAT-specific fields. | Replace with a paper-ballot account and candidate-agent copy showing printed, issued, cancelled, tendered, spoiled, unused, box-wise counted and rejected ballots. | Reconciliation before declaration; public exception register. |
| Rule 66A, including substituted rules 55C, 56C, 56D and 57C | Creates EVM inspection, electronic count, VVPAT scrutiny and machine resealing procedures. | Omit rule 66A in full. Restore/retain the ordinary paper-ballot count, scrutiny, recount and sealing rules in their current or amended paper-ballot form. | Every valid paper ballot counted; written recount decision; candidate-agent observation; Form 20 correction where a recount changes the count. |
| Rules 92(1A), 92(2)(cc), 93(1)(cc), 93(1A), 94(aa), proviso to 94(b), and 95 | EVM/VVPAT custody, inspection, retention, disposal and ECI direction provisions. | Omit the EVM/VVPAT-specific clauses and rule 95. Retain paper-ballot custody, court-controlled inspection and record-retention safeguards. | Paper ballot packets remain preserved and inspectable only under the lawful court process; no EVM-specific discretion survives. |
| [Forms 17A, 17B, 17C and Form 20](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ViewFileUploaded?path=AC_CEN_3_81_00001_195143_1517807327542/rulesindividualfile/&file=2.conduct_of_election_rules%2C_1961.doc.pdf) | Voter register/tendered ballot records, EVM/VVPAT account and result sheet interfaces. | Issue a Gazette schedule of replacement forms and a cross-reference table. | No form may identify a voter with a marked ballot; agents receive the prescribed account; recount corrections flow into Form 20 or its lawful successor. |

## D. Instructions, manuals and operational documents

An official inventory, version, and withdrawal/replacement notice is required for every instruction that implements an EVM/VVPAT process. The current source pack establishes that ECI manuals and instructions exist but does not yet establish a complete, current instruction inventory. The accountable authority must publish the following before the statutory transition date:

1. An inventory of every live EVM/VVPAT instruction, manual, standard operating procedure, procurement specification, form, training package and storage/disposal direction.
2. For each item: title, number, date, legal basis, official URL, status, replacement paper-ballot instrument, owner and commencement date.
3. A withdrawal notice for every item authorising EVM voting or tabulation.
4. A paper-ballot manual covering printing, security stock, accessible marking, ballot issue, custody, counting, recount, result publication, retention, forensic preservation and disposal.

## E. Transition, savings and commencement

1. Bill s.19 fixes the paper-ballot transition at 365 days after assent and prohibits EVM use for any election notification issued after that date.[^bill]
2. Completed polls, declared results and pending election petitions remain governed by the law applicable when their election notification was issued, subject to court jurisdiction.
3. EVMs may only be retained for secure storage, court-ordered forensic examination or audited disposal; no machine may be used to conduct, record, tabulate or determine a vote after the transition date.
4. The final Bill must include the exact Gazette rule-repeal/replacement schedule and authenticated form schedule before introduction.[^bill]
