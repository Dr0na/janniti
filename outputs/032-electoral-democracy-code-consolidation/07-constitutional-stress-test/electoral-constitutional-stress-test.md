# Electoral Democracy Consolidation — Constitutional Stress Test

## Conclusion

**Resilient with conditions.** The revised provisional Bill now addresses high-risk automatic sanctions, concentrated enforcement, retaliatory complaint mechanisms, opaque allocation, conflict concealment, data targeting and covert case-specific direction. It is suitable for further drafting, but not introduction, until the unresolved constitutional route, exact cross-references, implementation evidence and external-review gates are completed.

| Scenario | Exploitation path | Failure prevented by | Rating if omitted |
|---|---|---|---|

## Minimum safeguards

1. No automatic disqualification, loss of seat, symbol sanction, prosecution, forfeiture, or voter deletion without notice, hearing, reasons, independent decision and judicial remedy.
2. Separate complaint intake, investigation, prosecution, adjudication, audit and appointment functions.
3. Ban undisclosed case-specific executive or party direction; publish aggregate enforcement and equality-comparator data.
4. Make emergency measures time-limited, publicly justified, legislatively notified and promptly reviewable.
5. Protect political expression, lawful criticism, whistleblowing and privacy; punish only deliberately false evidence or obstruction proved through fair process.
6. Require a recorded independent escalation decision before any complaint moves to a coercive or materially adverse step, and prohibit public allegation or penalty on an uncorroborated input alone.
7. Require auditable allocation, conflict disclosure, recusal, evidence-preservation and prompt interim protection; no actor may bypass these through a contractor, funding condition or data system.
8. Directly amend the 2023 appointment Act rather than relying on general Commission-transparency duties; require that an executive-and-opposition pair cannot make a recommendation without independently constituted support.
9. If paper ballots replace EVMs, prohibit electronic tabulation as well; require each ballot to be counted, reconciliation before declaration, accessible secret marking, equal observation, preserved records and an effective recount/election-petition route.

## Residual risks

No statute can eliminate political pressure, ballot theft, local intimidation, delay, misinformation or institutional capture. A legislature could also weaken future safeguards, subject to constitutional limits and judicial review. Public records, independent journalism, accessible remedies, periodic audit, public participation and free elections remain essential safeguards.
