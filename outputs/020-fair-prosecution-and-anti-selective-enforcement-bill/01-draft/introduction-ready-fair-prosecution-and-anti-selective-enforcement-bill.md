# THE FAIR PROSECUTION AND ANTI-SELECTIVE ENFORCEMENT BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to establish an independent Directorate of Prosecution separate from police and political executive control; to prohibit selective criminal enforcement and political vendetta prosecutions; to mandate pre-arrest judicial scrutiny for non-violent offences; to institute annual anti-vendetta comparator audits; to provide statutory compensation for malicious prosecution; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Fair Prosecution and Anti-Selective Enforcement Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Independent Prosecution Directorate" means the autonomous agency established under section 3;

(b) "Malicious Prosecution" means any criminal proceeding initiated without reasonable and probable cause, driven by improper motives or political vendetta; and

(c) "Selective Enforcement" means the discriminatory pattern of initiating criminal investigations or coercive steps against specific individuals while failing to enforce the law against similarly situated persons based on political affiliation.

### 3. Independent Directorate of Prosecution

(1) There shall be established an autonomous agency to be called the Independent Directorate of Prosecution headed by a Director General of Prosecution.

(2) The Director General of Prosecution shall be appointed by a panel comprising the Chief Justice of India, the Leader of Opposition, and a senior judge nominated by the Supreme Court.

(3) Public Prosecutors shall operate independently of the police department and political executive.

### 4. Pre-Arrest Judicial Scrutiny for Non-Violent Offences

(1) No person accused of a non-violent offence punishable with imprisonment up to seven years shall be arrested without prior written authorization from a Judicial Magistrate following a pre-arrest hearing.

(2) The Magistrate shall refuse arrest authorization unless satisfied that arrest is strictly necessary to prevent absconding, evidence destruction, or witness intimidation.

### 5. Annual Anti-Vendetta Comparator Audit

(1) The Independent Directorate of Prosecution shall publish an annual Anti-Vendetta Comparator Audit examining enforcement patterns across all investigative agencies.

(2) Where the audit reveals a statistically significant pattern of selective enforcement against political opponents or critics, Parliament and the High Court shall be notified immediately.

### 6. Statutory Compensation for Malicious Prosecution

(1) Any person acquitted of criminal charges upon a finding of Malicious Prosecution shall be entitled to statutory compensation from the State of not less than twenty-five lakh rupees.

(2) The compensation awarded under sub-section (1) shall be recoverable from the investigating officers and public prosecutors responsible for initiating the malicious proceeding.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Selective enforcement and political vendetta prosecutions undermine Article 14 equality and destroy public faith in criminal justice (*Jaisinghani* judgment).

2. The Fair Prosecution and Anti-Selective Enforcement Bill, 2026 establishes an Independent Directorate of Prosecution, mandates pre-arrest judicial scrutiny, institutes anti-vendetta audits, and provides ₹25 Lakhs compensation for malicious prosecution.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3 establishes the Independent Directorate of Prosecution.

2. Recurring annual operational expenditure is estimated at rupees four hundred crores.
