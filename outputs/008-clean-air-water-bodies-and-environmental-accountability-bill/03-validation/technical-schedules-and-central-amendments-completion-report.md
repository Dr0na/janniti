# Technical Schedules and Central Amendments — Completion Report

**Matter:** 008 — Clean Air, Water Bodies, Environmental Accountability and Single-Use Plastic Prohibition Bill
**Date:** 10 August 2026  
**Status:** Proposed drafting completed; not an enactment and not ready for introduction.

## Completed proposed text

The working Bill now contains the following proposed material.

| Subject | Location | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Criminal procedure | Schedule II, Part A | Uses the special-law route preserved by sections 4–5 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023; specifies classification, court and cognizance safeguards. |
| Tribunal jurisdiction | Schedule II, Part B | Exact proposed insertion of the Bill into Schedule I to the National Green Tribunal Act, 2010. |
| Existing Central environmental statutes | Schedule II, Part C | Expressly preserves the Water Act, Air Act, Environment (Protection) Act and consistent rules. No unsupported repeal or amendment is proposed. |
| Corruption and electoral law | Schedule II, Part C | Preserves the applicable Prevention of Corruption Act and constitutional/Representation of the People Act routes; creates no shortcut for sanction, removal or disqualification. |
| Permissions, contracts and records | Schedule II, Part D | Provides register, consistency review, necessary-extent suspension, contract review and preservation of evidence/proceedings. |
| Technical operation | Schedule III | Sets the reference-standard, baseline, traceability, laboratory, tanker, wastewater, groundwater, audit, publication and capacity-gate framework. |

## What remains deliberately incomplete

No exact State/UT amendment is claimed. The available State/UT registry identifies official source routes but does not yet contain verified substantive enactments. Schedule II therefore requires jurisdiction-specific concordances rather than pretending to amend 36 jurisdictions without their controlling text.

Likewise, Schedule III does not invent universal numerical values for litres per person, collection time, catchment withdrawals, ecological flows, wastewater reuse, or emerging contaminants. It requires source/version disclosure and independent health, disability, hydrogeological, laboratory, fiscal and equality review before any binding local number is set.

Before an introduction-ready instrument can be prepared, complete the Gazette-by-Gazette consolidation of the Plastic Waste Management Rules and other relevant subordinate law; the State/UT concordances; parliamentary drafting and criminal-procedure review; fiscal memorandum; and the required independent specialist reviews.
