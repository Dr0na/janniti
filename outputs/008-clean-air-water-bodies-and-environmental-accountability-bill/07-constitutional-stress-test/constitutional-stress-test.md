# Constitutional Stress Test — Working Draft

## Conclusion

**Vulnerable pending material conditions.** The draft contains meaningful safeguards, but it cannot be treated as constitutionally cleared because competence, State/UT interaction, current-law cross-references, technical schedule and fiscal capacity are unverified.

| Scenario | Exploitation path | Existing check | Remaining failure | Rating | Required fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hostile majority | Uses religious-waste enforcement against a minority community. | Content-neutral rule, collection duties, reasons, complaint and court review. | Local bias and delayed remedy. | High | Independent equality audit, prompt interim relief and published comparator data. |
| Captured regulator | Selectively inspects rivals or suppresses industry samples. | Risk criteria, continuous monitoring, chain of custody, split sample, conflict disclosure, public data and audit. | Laboratory, dashboard and Board dependence remains. | High | Independently govern data infrastructure, random external retesting and protected disclosure route. |
| Corrupt official | Sells a disposal exception or falsifies restoration. | Misconduct offence, records, audit, court review. | Detection may be slow. | High | Beneficial-control disclosure and mandatory independent review for high-risk authorisations. |

## Single points of failure

The principal dependencies are treatment capacity, credible water-body mapping, laboratory independence, State/local funding, timely courts/NGT, and a complete product schedule. No provision should be commenced for a class/area until the corresponding capacity and accessible transition safeguards are independently verified.

## Residual risk

Law cannot eliminate corruption, sampling error, false reporting, resource scarcity, political discrimination or judicial delay. Public data, free press, community participation, independent auditing and effective court remedies remain essential.
