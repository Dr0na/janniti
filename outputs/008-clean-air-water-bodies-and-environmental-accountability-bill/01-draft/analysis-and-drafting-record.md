# Consolidation, Repair and Filing Record — Clean Air, Water Bodies, Environmental Accountability and Single-Use Plastic Prohibition Bill

## Consolidation decision

Matter 008's earlier short draft and the later Matter 030 draft address the same proposed Union instrument. They have been consolidated into this matter. The operative draft is `not-ready-for-introduction.md`; the earlier text is retained as `legacy-pre-research-draft.md` for audit, not as a second Bill.

The consolidation retains the earlier Bill's aims of public environmental monitoring, enforceable environmental accountability, restoration and serious corporate responsibility. It implements them through sections 11D, 13–19 and 20–25 of the operative draft: a phased, capacity-gated public monitoring network; public data and independent audit; separated investigation, sampling, prosecution and adjudication; individual fault and fair-process requirements; restoration and financial assurance; and judicial remedies. It does **not** retain unsupported fixed nationwide sensor-grid targets, unsupported fiscal figures, an unreviewable combined-power authority, “fundamental right” wording in an ordinary Bill, automatic corporate-officer guilt, or absolute criminal liability. Those features would be constitutionally or evidentially unsafe without further authority and review.

## Filing target

Proposed target: Parliament of India, Union Bill, dated 9 August 2026. No sponsor, Bill number, House, Minister, President's recommendation, Money-Bill determination, financial-incidence clearance, authentication or Secretariat approval has been claimed or invented.

## Exact blockers

1. Source-backed constitutional competence, Article 254 and State/UT implementation analysis.
2. Independent technical verification of the completed Schedule I, alternatives and health/disability/emergency exception operation.
3. Exact amendments, repeal/savings and treatment of current permits/consents/rules.
4. Costed Central and State/UT implementation, wastewater/waste-treatment and just-transition plan.
5. Verified criminal-procedure, sentencing, tribunal/court and enforcement interface.
6. Independent expert and community review record addressing Critical and High issues.
7. A costed Financial Memorandum; sponsor route, House and any required Presidential recommendation.
8. Maritime-law verification of treaty status, port/flag/coastal-State enforcement routes and the final India-nexus provisions.
