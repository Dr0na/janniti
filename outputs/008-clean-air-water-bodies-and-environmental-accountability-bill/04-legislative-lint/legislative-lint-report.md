# Legislative Lint Report — Working Draft

**Mechanical result:** 203 warnings on the current preflight after consolidating the public environmental-monitoring network into section 11D. The tool flags every penalty, emergency, rule-making and privacy-related clause by design. This is a review prompt, not a determination of invalidity.

| Rule group | Severity | Location | Exploitation path | Exact treatment in the draft / remaining action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L010 penalty provisions | High | Clauses 20–25; Schedule II | Punishment without clear culpability or proportionality. | Clauses use intentional, knowing or reckless thresholds, notice/hearing and court conviction. Schedule II now states the proposed court and bail classifications. The intentional-or-knowing circumvention offence expressly covers Ministers, elected political officeholders, political appointees and entities; it requires independent investigation, prosecutorial reasons, judicial safeguards and a no-double-punishment rule. Its final interaction with anti-corruption, privilege and disqualification law still requires specialist review. |
| L008 emergency powers | High | Clauses 5, 18 and 28 | Emergency label bypasses rights/process. | Necessity, least harm, record, short reporting, judicial review and 90-day rule expiry included. Align exactly with current law before introduction. |
| L005 exceptions | Medium | Clause 7(4); Schedule I | General commercial exception becomes loophole. | Product/purpose-specific rule, evidence, audit, anti-diversion and two-year expiry included. The schedule now also requires traceability, take-back and public recording; its final compatibility with every waste rule remains to be checked. |
| L006 delegated power | High | Clauses 11B–11F and 28 | Executive changes core policy or creates new offences. | Financial assurance, technical monitoring, waste-chain and climate-standard subjects are bounded; consultation, laying and hard no-delegation limits apply. Verify each rule-making and Parliament-procedure interaction. |
| L001 vague/subjective thresholds | High | Clauses 5–8, 11A–11F, 12, 18–19 and 26–28; Schedule III | Selective enforcement. | Objective evidence, reasons, notice, records and independent review are included. Schedule III supplies technical controls, public baselines, custody records, audits and a published-standard discipline, but catchment-specific and contaminant-specific numerical thresholds still need independent specialist completion. |
| L003 retaliation/immunity | High | Clauses 16 and 30 | Protected reporting becomes impunity, or good-faith clause shields officials. | Clause 16 preserves generally applicable law for knowingly false harm; clause 30 now expressly preserves all accountability and remedies. |

**Lint status:** material findings remain. The draft must not be called introduction-ready until the outstanding competence, schedule, current-law, evidence, fiscal and specialist-review findings are resolved.
