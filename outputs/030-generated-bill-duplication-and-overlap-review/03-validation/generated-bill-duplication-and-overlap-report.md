# Generated Bill Duplication and Overlap Review

## Bottom line and assumptions

**Conclusion: materially vulnerable to duplication unless consolidated by subject-matter.** This is a repository-internal design review, not an opinion on enacted Indian law. It reviewed 28 standalone introduction-ready instruments in Matters 001–028, as present on 11 August 2026. Matter 008 is expressly not ready for introduction and Matter 029 has no complete Bill, so neither is counted as an introduction-ready instrument.

No two instruments are textually identical. The material issue is overlapping subject matter, institutions, remedies, and rule-making—not duplicate filenames.

## Bills that are complementary rather than duplicative

- 009 (whistleblowing/anti-SLAPP) complements the accountability Bills but should supply a common retaliation-protection cross-reference.
- 013 (housing), 014 (transport), 027 (care) are separate social-rights delivery domains.
- 025 (financial scams) is distinct, though its special-court provisions must yield to the common criminal-process schedule recommended in O-08.

## Priority consolidation sequence

1. Consolidate 002, 003 and 023 before any electoral Bill is introduced.
2. Reconcile 016–021 as a police/justice package, beginning with O-03’s custody-evidence-investigation boundary.
3. Reconcile 005, 024 and 028 before creating any new national audit or anti-corruption body.
4. Harmonise 007, 010 and 015 regulator, testing, offence and compensation rules.
5. Incorporate 012’s general automated-decision rights into 011 by cross-reference.

## Residual limits

This review does not determine legislative competence, constitutionality, current-law conflict, fiscal feasibility, or introduction readiness. Those questions require the relevant research pack, constitutional stress test, citation verification, and an independent legal review for each consolidated package.
