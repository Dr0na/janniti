# THE HOUSING SECURITY, ANTI-EVICTION, AND URBAN LAND EQUITY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee every resident the statutory right to shelter and protection against arbitrary forced eviction or house demolition; to mandate a ninety-day written notice and judicial hearing prior to any urban displacement; to enforce a thirty per cent urban land pooling quota for low-income public housing; to provide statutory title security for long-term informal settlements; to establish the National Housing Rights Authority; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Housing Security, Anti-Eviction, and Urban Land Equity Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Forced Eviction" means the permanent or temporary removal against their will of individuals, families, or communities from their homes or land without the provision of, and access to, appropriate forms of legal or other protection;

(b) "Informal Settlement" means any residential area occupied continuously for seven years or more without formal land ownership title;

(c) "National Housing Authority" means the National Housing Rights Authority established under section 5; and

(d) "Urban Land Pooling Quota" means the mandatory reservation of thirty per cent of developed land in urban master plans for affordable public housing under section 4.

### 3. Prohibition of Forced Eviction and Arbitrary Demolition

(1) No eviction or demolition of any residential dwelling shall take place without a prior written notice of not less than ninety days detailing the specific legal grounds.

(2) No demolition or eviction shall be executed without an explicit order issued by a competent Judicial Magistrate following an open hearing.

(3) No eviction shall be carried out during inclement weather, night hours, or prior to the completion of in-situ or nearby rehabilitation housing equipped with basic utilities.

### 4. Statutory Land Title Security and Land Pooling

(1) Every family residing in an Informal Settlement continuously for seven years or more shall be entitled to statutory tenure security and non-alienable land lease titles.

(2) Every urban development authority and municipal corporation shall reserve not less than thirty per cent of all pooled or redeveloped urban land for low-income public housing.

### 5. National Housing Rights Authority

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Housing Rights Authority.

(2) The Authority shall monitor urban housing equity, enforce anti-eviction safeguards, and adjudicate displacement disputes.

### 6. Offences and Penalties for Illegal Demolition

(1) Any public official or private entity executing a forced eviction or house demolition in violation of section 3 shall be punishable with imprisonment up to three years and fine up to ten lakh rupees.

(2) In addition to criminal penalties, the illegal executing authority shall pay full reconstruction costs and relocation compensation to affected residents.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Forced evictions and summary house demolitions without due process inflict severe human suffering and violate the fundamental right to shelter under Article 21 (*Chameli Singh* judgment).

2. The Housing Security, Anti-Eviction, and Urban Land Equity Bill, 2026 prohibits forced evictions, mandates 90-day notices and judicial hearings, enforces a 30% urban land quota for affordable housing, and provides land title security for long-term informal residents.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 mandates urban public housing development and rehabilitation infrastructure.

2. Central financial assistance for urban housing equity is estimated at rupees five thousand crores annually.
