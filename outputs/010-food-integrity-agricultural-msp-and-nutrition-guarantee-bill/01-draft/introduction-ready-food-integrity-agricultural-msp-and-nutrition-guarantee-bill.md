# THE FOOD INTEGRITY, AGRICULTURAL MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE, AND NUTRITION GUARANTEE BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee every agricultural producer the statutory right to a Minimum Support Price calculated at comprehensive cost plus fifty per cent; to ensure 100% nutritional security and unadulterated food for all persons in India; to establish a real-time digital supply chain testing framework against synthetic adulterants and pesticide residues; to penalize food adulteration networks and price-rigging cartels; to establish the National Agricultural Price and Food Integrity Authority; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Food Integrity, Agricultural Minimum Support Price, and Nutrition Guarantee Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Comprehensive Cost" or "C2" means the total cost of agricultural production including paid-out costs, family labor, rent of land, and interest on capital;

(b) "Minimum Support Price" or "MSP" means the statutory floor price for agricultural produce determined under section 3;

(c) "Food Adulteration" means the addition of any harmful, synthetic, toxic, or sub-standard substance to food commodities; and

(d) "National Authority" means the National Agricultural Price and Food Integrity Authority established under section 5.

### 3. Statutory Right to Minimum Support Price

(1) Every farmer selling agricultural produce shall have an enforceable statutory right to receive a price not less than the Minimum Support Price.

(2) The Minimum Support Price shall be calculated based on the formula of Comprehensive Cost (C2) plus fifty per cent.

(3) No trader, processor, or corporate buyer shall purchase agricultural produce below the statutory Minimum Support Price.

### 4. Food Safety and Digital Adulteration Testing

(1) The National Authority shall establish automated digital food testing laboratories at all wholesale mandis, processing hubs, and public distribution centres.

(2) The testing framework shall screen for synthetic chemicals, heavy metals, microplastics, and unapproved pesticide residues.

### 5. National Agricultural Price and Food Integrity Authority

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Agricultural Price and Food Integrity Authority.

(2) The Authority shall monitor MSP implementation, conduct food audits, and enforce price stability.

### 6. Penalties for Purchasing Below MSP and Food Adulteration

(1) Whoever purchases agricultural produce below the prescribed Minimum Support Price shall be punishable with imprisonment up to three years and fine up to five lakh rupees.

(2) Whoever engages in commercial food adulteration threatening public health shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than five years and fine up to fifty lakh rupees.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Farmers in India suffer from acute financial distress due to agricultural price volatility, while consumers face widespread food adulteration and pesticide contamination.

2. The Food Integrity, Agricultural Minimum Support Price, and Nutrition Guarantee Bill, 2026 guarantees a statutory MSP (C2+50%), digital food testing, 100% nutritional security, and severe penalties for adulteration.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 mandates digital food testing labs across all wholesale mandis.

2. Recurring annual expenditure for procurement support and testing infrastructure is estimated at rupees ten thousand crores.
