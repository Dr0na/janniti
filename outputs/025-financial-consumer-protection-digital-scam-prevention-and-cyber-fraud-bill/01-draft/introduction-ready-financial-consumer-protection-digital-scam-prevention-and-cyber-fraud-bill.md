# THE FINANCIAL CONSUMER PROTECTION, DIGITAL SCAM PREVENTION, AND CYBER-FRAUD BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to protect financial consumers against digital banking scams, cyber fraud, unauthorized online transfers, and illegal loan application extortion; to enforce strict financial institution liability and mandatory five-day customer reimbursement; to prohibit unlicensed digital lending platforms; to establish Fast-Track Cyber Crime Special Courts; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Financial Consumer Protection, Digital Scam Prevention, and Cyber-Fraud Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Cyber Fraud" means any unauthorized electronic fund transfer, phishing, SIM-swap scam, identity theft, or deceptive digital transaction resulting in financial loss to a consumer;

(b) "Financial Institution" means any commercial bank, payment bank, non-banking financial company, or digital payment gateway; and

(c) "Unlicensed Lending Platform" means any digital application offering credit or loans without authorization from the Reserve Bank of India.

### 3. Strict Financial Institution Liability and Five-Day Customer Reimbursement

(1) Where a financial consumer reports an unauthorized Cyber Fraud transaction to their Financial Institution within seventy-two hours of occurrence, the Financial Institution shall credit the full disputed amount back to the consumer's account within five working days.

(2) A Financial Institution shall be exempt from sub-section (1) only if it establishes before the Cyber Crime Special Court that the consumer intentionally shared authentication credentials or acted with gross negligence.

### 4. Absolute Prohibition of Unlicensed Lending Apps and Extortion

(1) No person or entity shall operate, host, or advertise any Unlicensed Lending Platform.

(2) Whosoever operates an Unlicensed Lending Platform or engages in harassment, public shaming, or unauthorized contact access for loan recovery shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than five years but which may extend to fourteen years, and fine.

### 5. Fast-Track Cyber Crime Special Courts and Fund Freezing

(1) The State Government shall designate Special Courts in each district for the summary trial of Cyber Fraud offences.

(2) Upon receiving a Cyber Fraud alert, the Special Court or designated Cyber Police Officer shall immediately issue a digital freezing order directing all recipient banks and wallets to hold disputed funds within one hour.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Digital banking scams, SIM-swapping, and predatory illegal loan apps inflict immense financial loss and mental trauma on millions of citizens in India.

2. The Financial Consumer Protection, Digital Scam Prevention, and Cyber-Fraud Bill, 2026 enforces strict bank liability, mandates 5-day customer reimbursement, bans illegal lending apps, and establishes Fast-Track Cyber Courts.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 5 mandates Cyber Crime Special Courts and digital freezing infrastructure.

2. Financial assistance is estimated at rupees one thousand crores over three years.
