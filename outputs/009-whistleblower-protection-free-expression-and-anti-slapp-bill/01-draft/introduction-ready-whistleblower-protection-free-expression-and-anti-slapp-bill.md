# THE WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION, FREE EXPRESSION, AND ANTI-SLAPP BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to protect persons making public interest disclosures regarding corruption, safety hazards, environmental violations, or unlawful acts; to establish a summary judicial procedure for the dismissal of Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation (SLAPP); to decriminalize speech defamation; to provide statutory identity protection and physical security for whistleblowers and journalists; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Whistleblower Protection, Free Expression, and Anti-SLAPP Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Anti-SLAPP Motion" means an application filed under section 5 seeking summary dismissal of a judicial proceeding initiated primarily to suppress public interest speech;

(b) "Public Interest Disclosure" means any communication made in good faith disclosing corruption, illegal conduct, environmental damage, or threat to public health;

(c) "Strategic Lawsuit Against Public Participation" or "SLAPP" means any civil or criminal proceeding brought against a person arising from public interest speech or civic participation; and

(d) "Whistleblower" means any person who makes a Public Interest Disclosure.

### 3. Statutory Immunity for Public Interest Disclosures

(1) No whistleblower making a Public Interest Disclosure shall be subject to any civil liability, departmental penalty, dismissal, or criminal prosecution arising from such disclosure.

(2) The immunity under sub-section (1) shall extend to journalists, civic auditors, and civil society researchers publishing public interest disclosures.

### 4. Identity and Physical Security Protection

(1) The Competent Authority shall maintain absolute confidentiality regarding the identity of any whistleblower.

(2) Where a whistleblower or their family faces threat of physical injury, harassment, or retaliation, the Competent Authority shall immediately issue binding directions to police authorities to provide 24x7 armed physical protection.

### 5. Summary Anti-SLAPP Dismissal Motion

(1) In any legal proceeding arising from public interest speech, the defendant may file an Anti-SLAPP Motion for summary dismissal within forty-five days of service.

(2) Upon the filing of an Anti-SLAPP Motion, all discovery and further proceedings in the main action shall be stayed automatically until the motion is decided.

(3) If the court finds that the proceeding was initiated to suppress public interest participation, it shall dismiss the proceeding with prejudice and award mandatory exemplary costs against the plaintiff.

### 6. Decriminalization of Defamation

(1) Offences relating to defamation under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 are hereby repealed.

(2) Defamation shall remain actionable solely as a civil tort subject to statutory ceilings on damages.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Whistleblowers, journalists, and civic activists in India face severe retaliation, physical violence, and abusive litigation (SLAPPs) designed to silence public interest reporting.

2. The Whistleblower Protection, Free Expression, and Anti-SLAPP Bill, 2026 establishes absolute statutory immunity, physical security protection, summary judicial dismissal of SLAPP suits with mandatory costs, and decriminalizes speech defamation to protect democracy.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 mandates armed physical security protection for whistleblowers facing threats.

2. Recurring annual expenditure for whistleblower protection programs is estimated at rupees two hundred crores.

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 7 empowers rule-making of an administrative character.
