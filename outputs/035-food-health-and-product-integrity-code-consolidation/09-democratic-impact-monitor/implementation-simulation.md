# Implementation Simulation Protocol

Before commencement, independently test the package against an ordinary person and a bad-faith actor.

| Actor | Scenario | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Small vendor | Minor remediable hygiene/expiry lapse. | Clear notice, affordable support, correction period, no bribe demand, reasoned review. |
| Large manufacturer | Serious batch contamination. | Containment, recall, public warning, preserved evidence and accountable remediation. |
| Delivery platform | Sponsored cloud-kitchen listing and delayed refund. | Ownership/price/fee disclosure, no dark pattern, timely refund and independent dispute route. |
| Inspector/laboratory | Sample and adverse result. | Objective grounds, receipt, chain of custody, split-sample/defence path, accredited method and review. |
| Captured authority | Selective inspections against critics. | Auditable allocation, comparator data, misconduct complaint and independent remedy. |
| Person with disability/limited literacy | Refund or closure challenge. | Assisted access, language support, no digital-only barrier, reasons and appeal. |

Use role-play, document review and lawfully available anonymised data. Publish pass/fail result, evidence, unresolved failures, corrective owner and deadline. No provision should commence until critical failures are corrected or publicly justified by a competent legislature.
