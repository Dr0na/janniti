# Monitoring and Evaluation Framework

Before commencement, establish baseline data for safety incidents, recalls, lab turnaround, vendor costs, food prices, refunds, enforcement disparities, privacy incidents, waste, appeals and misconduct complaints.

Set publicly justified outcome targets, disaggregated equity measures, data-quality limits, independent evaluator, six-month implementation review, annual evaluation, five-year statutory review and triggers for correction, suspension or repeal. Do not treat enforcement volume or revenue as success without safety, fairness, cost and rights measures.
