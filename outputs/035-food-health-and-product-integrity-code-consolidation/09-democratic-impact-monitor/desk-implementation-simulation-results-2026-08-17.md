# Desk Implementation Simulation Results — 17 August 2026

**Method:** document-based role simulation using the working Bill, standalone instruments, SOPs, red-team manual, overlap audit and accessibility toolkit. No public authority, laboratory, platform, vendor, consumer, worker or court participated. Results therefore assess design only, not real-world performance.

| Scenario | Design result | Evidence in package | Operational result | Required action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small vendor, remediable hygiene lapse | Pass with conditions | Correction direction, proportionality, reasons, review and small-operator support are specified. | Untested | Pilot notice, support and review workflow with vendors before commencement. |
| Large manufacturer, serious contamination | Pass with conditions | Preservation, assessment, warning, recall, compensation and public reporting are specified. | Untested | Test a multi-State recall with independent observers and real traceability data. |
| Platform hides sponsorship/margin and delays refund | Pass with conditions | Required disclosures, records, human review, interest and independent route are specified. | Untested | Test a common price-display schema and refund timing with platforms/consumer groups. |
| Inspector samples food and laboratory reports adverse result | Pass with conditions | Objective grounds, receipt, chain of custody, split sample, conflict screen, disclosure and referral testing are specified. | Untested | Conduct blind proficiency, chain-of-custody and defence-testing pilot. |
| Captured authority targets a critic | Pass with conditions | Neutral criteria, auditable allocation, comparator data, ombudsman and court remedy are specified. | Untested | Publish case-allocation algorithm/method and independently test selective-enforcement complaints. |
| Person with disability or limited literacy challenges a refund/closure | Pass with conditions | Assisted, multilingual, non-digital access, legal aid, reasons and appeal are specified. | Untested | Conduct usability testing with affected users and publish remediation plan. |

## Overall result

**Design status: conditionally resilient. Operational status: not yet demonstrated.** No enactment or commencement decision should rely on this desk simulation alone.

## Preconditions for a real pilot

1. Accepted primary-source and legal-route records for each instrument.
2. Independent pilot governance, ethics/privacy review and public participation.
3. Trained inspector, laboratory, platform, ombudsman and legal-aid participants.
4. Test data with no real enforcement, penalty, closure or adverse consequence.
5. Published pass/fail criteria, incident protocol, evaluation report and corrective deadlines.
