# Accessibility Audit Checklist

Verify that every portal, form, notice, consultation, hearing and complaint route provides keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, readable contrast, plain language, translations, sign-language/interpretation where needed, telephone/paper/in-person alternatives, disability accommodation, rural/low-bandwidth access and reasonable time extensions.

Test with disabled people, low-literacy users, older people, workers, small vendors and affected language groups. Publish findings, correction owner and deadline. Accessibility failure cannot be used to deny a remedy or enforce an undisclosed obligation.
