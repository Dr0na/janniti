# Legal Aid and Compensation Protocol

Offer accessible, timely legal aid and practical support to consumers, workers, small vendors and whistleblowers affected by serious food-safety harm, retaliatory enforcement, false implication, unlawful closure, refund failure or evidence abuse.

Eligibility must be transparent, non-discriminatory and reviewable. Support may include advice, translation, disability access, emergency evidence preservation, representation, interim protection and referral to statutory compensation. Compensation must be determined by a competent independent authority on evidence and reasons; it must not require waiver of future lawful remedies.
