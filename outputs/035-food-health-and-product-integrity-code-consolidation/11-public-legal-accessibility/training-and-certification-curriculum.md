# Training and Certification Curriculum

| Audience | Core modules | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Vendors and food workers | hygiene, allergens, expiry, traceability, recall, non-plastic transition, complaint rights | accessible practical demonstration |
| Inspectors | evidence, sampling, proportionality, anti-extortion, equality, reasons, review | observed scenario and ethics declaration |
| Analysts | method validation, uncertainty, conflicts, chain of custody, disclosure | proficiency test and conflict screen |
| Platform staff | price/fee disclosure, refunds, dark patterns, privacy, worker dignity, recalls | simulated order/complaint exercise |
| Managers/public authorities | governance, independence, data publication, conflict, whistleblower protection | public accountability plan |

Training must be low-cost, multilingual, disability-accessible and free from party, industry or vendor capture. Certification does not displace independent inspection, consumer remedies or court review.
