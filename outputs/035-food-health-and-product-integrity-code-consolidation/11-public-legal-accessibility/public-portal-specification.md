# Public Portal Specification

Publish versioned drafts, accepted primary sources, consultation notices/submissions, audit reports, dashboard data, recall notices, rulemaking reasons and change history. Every item needs publication date, authority, version, source link, status and accessible download.

Provide English, Hindi and relevant local-language summaries; screen-reader access; low-bandwidth access; assisted offline access; open data with privacy safeguards; and an immutable public change log. The portal must not expose whistleblowers, personal data, active-case evidence or security-sensitive material.
