# Public Grievance and Whistleblower Forms

## Access rule

Offer online, paper, telephone-assisted and in-person routes in relevant languages and accessible formats. Do not require an identity where an anonymous report is lawful; minimise personal data and explain confidentiality limits.

## Food-safety / consumer grievance

1. What happened, when and where?
2. Food/feed business, platform or product involved.
3. Immediate health/safety risk, if any.
4. Order, batch, receipt, photo or other evidence available.
5. Remedy sought: warning, refund, replacement, recall, inspection, correction or other.
6. Accessibility/support need and safe contact method.

## Whistleblower / misconduct report

1. Public-safety, corruption, evidence-fabrication, extortion, retaliation or conflict concern.
2. Persons/office involved, facts observed and dates.
3. Evidence location and preservation risk.
4. Retaliation risk and requested interim protection.
5. Safe confidential contact route.

Every submission receives a reference, acknowledgement, preservation assessment, reasons, review route and anti-retaliation information. A good-faith report is not false merely because it is unsubstantiated.
