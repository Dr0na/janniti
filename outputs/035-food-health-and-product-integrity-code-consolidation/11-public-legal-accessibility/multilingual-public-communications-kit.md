# Multilingual Public Communications Kit

Create tested plain-language templates for recall, hygiene notice, food-safety warning, expiry alert, refund decision, inspection receipt, closure notice, appeal outcome and right-to-challenge information.

Every notice states what happened, who is affected, immediate safety action, evidence status/uncertainty, responsible authority, available support, deadline, review/court route and accessible contact. Provide English, Hindi, relevant local languages, audio, large print, screen-reader and low-bandwidth versions. Never publish personal data or prejudge guilt.
