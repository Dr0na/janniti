# Right to Challenge Guide

You may ask for reasons, inspect your lawful record, correct inaccurate information, challenge sampling/lab results through the available referral route, seek review of a licence action, closure, refund decision, ranking/delisting, data use or enforcement notice, complain about official misconduct, and approach the competent court or statutory forum.

Ask for the decision date, decision-maker, evidence relied on, deadline, review authority, accessibility support and interim protection. Preserve receipts, notices, order records, photographs and safe copies of relevant information. Good-faith complaints, journalism, whistleblowing and lawful criticism must not trigger retaliation.
