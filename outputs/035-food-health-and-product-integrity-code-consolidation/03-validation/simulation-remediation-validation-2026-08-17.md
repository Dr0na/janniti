# Simulation-Remediation Validation — 17 August 2026

**Jurisdiction:** India; Union framework with State/UT and local interfaces.  
**Status:** design validation only. This is not a finding that any body, source, pilot or statutory power is operational.

## Conclusion

**Robust with conditions.** The document-level remedies now close the identified design gaps without assigning final, unchecked power to an ombudsman, regulator, laboratory, platform or emergency decision-maker. Their effectiveness remains conditional on the external implementation gates below.

## Rights and power assessment

The remedies preserve equal access, non-discrimination, privacy, reasons, accessible challenge, independent review and judicial remedies. They prohibit political belief, criticism and similar protected characteristics as allocation inputs; prevent the ombudsman from finally investigating, prosecuting and adjudicating the same case; require defence testing and raw-data controls; and make emergency action time-bound and independently reviewable. No remedy authorises collective punishment, retrospective liability, prior restraint or secret law.

## Residual risks and gates

1. Human acceptance of primary sources is mandatory under `governance/SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.md`; an agent cannot perform it.
2. An ordinary document cannot establish an office, appropriate funds, accredit laboratories, configure a platform or compel a public authority to pilot a programme.
3. Exact statutory competence, constitutional validity and State/UT implementation must be checked against accepted current primary law before filing or commencement.
4. Public consultation, representative usability testing and independent pilot evaluation remain necessary before any operational deployment.

**Validation status:** clear at the design-remediation level, with the external gates above remaining open.
