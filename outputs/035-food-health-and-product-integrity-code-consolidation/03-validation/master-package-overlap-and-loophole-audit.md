## Non-duplication rules

1. A duty belongs in one primary instrument; other instruments may cross-reference it but must not re-enact a different version.
2. Criminal punishment requires a distinct prohibited act, mental element, causation/risk threshold, court and procedure; administrative correction cannot be used as covert criminal punishment.
3. A licence suspension, recall or emergency hold requires a separate evidence threshold and review route; it does not establish criminal guilt.
4. A State/UT or local instrument may add implementation detail only within its legal authority and may not lower Union safety standards or create secrecy, political exemptions or unreviewable discretion.

## Residual risks

No document can prevent a captured investigator, laboratory, prosecutor or local authority from attempting abuse. Protection depends on documented evidence, plural oversight, independent courts, free media, legal aid, whistleblower protection, public data and enforceable remedies.
