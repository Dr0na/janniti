# Release and Readiness Checklist

No instrument may be labelled enacted, verified, accepted, legally conclusive or introduction-ready unless every applicable item is complete.

1. Jurisdiction, legislature, competence and legal vehicle are settled.
2. Every current-law claim has a direct accepted primary source, version/date and pinpoint.
3. All amendment/repeal text, definitions, schedules and cross-references are exact and internally consistent.
4. Rights, equality, privacy, federalism, due process, scientific evidence and fiscal impacts are assessed.
5. Lint, citation verification, provenance, stress test, power map, monitoring and consultation requirements have passed or are expressly blocking.
6. Every enforcement power has objective trigger, record, independent check, appeal, remedy, audit and expiry/review.
7. Formal memoranda, sponsor/House, recommendation, authentication and filing requirements are determined by competent authority.
8. A designated human reviewer has accepted source changes under `governance/SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.md`.

If any answer is no, label the output `draft` or `not-ready-for-introduction` and identify the exact blocker.
