# Data Protection Impact Assessment Template

For each video, location, order, complaint, whistleblower or evidence system, record purpose, lawful authority, affected persons, fields, recipient, retention, security, automated decision, cross-border transfer, risk, alternative, mitigation, grievance and review date.

Do not deploy where a less intrusive method achieves the purpose. Kitchen-view/video systems require special analysis of worker dignity, public access, private areas, audio, identity masking and harassment risk. Publish a non-sensitive summary and obtain independent review for high-risk processing.
