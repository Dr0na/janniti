# Proposed Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Food-Delivery Amendment Rule

**Draft text; not enacted.** This is a proposed insertion after rule 4 of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. The official 2021 amendment confirms the resident nodal-officer baseline and is preserved.

## 4A. Additional duties of e-commerce entities facilitating food orders

(1) An e-commerce entity that lists, orders, delivers or arranges delivery of food shall, before an order is placed, disclose in plain language—

(a) the legal name, business address, food-business licence or registration identifier and beneficial owner, to the extent lawfully required, of the food business responsible for preparation;

(b) the physical preparation location and whether it is a restaurant, cloud kitchen, caterer or other prescribed food business;

(c) the item description, quantity or portion basis, applicable allergen/ingredient and safety information, and current recall or closure notice;

(d) the direct/offline price of an identical item where one exists, the platform food-item price, delivery charge, service/convenience/packaging charge, tax, discount, discount funder, platform commission or margin, and final payable amount;

(e) the cancellation, refund, replacement, complaint and independent-review route.

(2) The entity shall preserve a tamper-evident order record containing the material disclosure, price, payment, preparation, handover, delivery, refund and complaint events. It shall not materially misrepresent a food business, kitchen, menu, price, rating, availability, ownership, safety notice, discount or delivery estimate.

(3) An undisputed refund shall be initiated within three business days after the entitlement is established and credited through the original payment route within seven business days. A disputed refund must receive a written, evidence-based human decision within three business days; unreasonable delay attracts the applicable lawful interest and does not prevent compensation.

(4) A platform shall label sponsored placement, affiliated listing and material ranking influence. It shall not impose a price-parity, most-favoured-price, exclusivity, anti-steering or equivalent condition that prevents a food business from truthfully offering a lower price or better lawful term elsewhere.

(5) A public kitchen-view feature may be offered or required only under a valid food-safety law and in compliance with applicable data-protection law. It shall exclude private areas and audio unless lawfully necessary, protect workers and customers from identification or harassment, provide accessible non-video information, and not be used for unrelated surveillance, commercial profiling or retaliation.

(6) The resident nodal officer shall receive complaints, preserve records, identify the decision-maker, provide written reasons and enable the statutory consumer-dispute and court routes. The officer shall not decide guilt, suppress lawful criticism or condition a remedy on waiver of rights.

(7) Enforcement shall use published criteria, notice, proportionate correction time except in a documented urgent safety case, reasons, equality audit, independent appeal and court remedy. No enforcement action may be used for extortion, political retaliation, discriminatory treatment or anti-competitive exclusion.
