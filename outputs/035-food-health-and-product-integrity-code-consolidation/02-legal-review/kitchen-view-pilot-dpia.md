# Kitchen-View Pilot DPIA

**Pilot-only template; not an approval to deploy.**

Purpose: enable limited food-safety transparency. Alternatives: inspection report, still image, customer-visible preparation area and periodic independent audit. The pilot must use the least intrusive effective alternative.

Risks: worker surveillance, customer identification, audio capture, private-area exposure, harassment, unauthorised access, indefinite retention and secondary use. Before each pilot use, the data fiduciary must record necessity, proportionality, data minimisation, the least intrusive alternative considered, retention period and the named authorised decision-maker. Controls: no audio; camera geofence; automatic privacy masks; no private/changing/rest/payment areas; role-limited access; access logs; minimum retention; no profiling/discipline/immigration/unrelated policing use; worker consultation; non-video alternative; incident response; independent audit; accessible complaint and deletion/correction route where lawful.

Stop condition: any unmasked private-area capture, unauthorised access, retaliation, material access-log anomaly or failure of the non-video accessibility route pauses the pilot, isolates affected data, gives notice where safe and lawful, and requires independent review before restart.
