# Environment, Waste and Disposal Amendment Schedule

Draft schedule for clauses 59, 75, 80–83 and 85. The Union instrument must amend or supersede inconsistent food-contact-plastic permissions, require traceable destruction of unsafe/expired food at the responsible operator’s cost, and establish a separately audited waste charge only by clear statutory authority. Rules must specify measurement, exemptions, credits, collection, spending, public reporting, appeal and anti-extortion controls. State/UT and local instruments must provide collection, safe treatment, reuse infrastructure and vendor support; no levy or closure may be imposed through unpublished discretion. Exact Environment (Protection) Act, Plastic Waste Management Rules, State/UT and municipal cross-references remain pending primary-source verification.
