# Environment, Waste and Disposal Amendment Schedule

## Verified current-law baseline

The 2026 amendment to the Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 substitutes rule 11(2) so recycled plastic packaging/commodities must meet the specified Indian Standard and applicable FSSAI food-contact marking/labelling requirements. It also inserts rule 12(3A)–(3C), allocating local-body, local-authority, Gram Panchayat and district-panchayat enforcement functions for waste-generator and specified plastic restrictions.

Source: [Plastic Waste Management Rules amendment, 2026](https://egazette.gov.in/writeReadData/2026/271465.pdf). A hash-pinned official snapshot is retained in `14-legal-change-monitoring/snapshots/`; acceptance remains pending the designated human review required by repository governance.

## Proposed legislative route

1. The FSS Act amendment in Schedule A of the working Bill must expressly override a food-contact plastic permission under FSS law; it cannot repeal or impliedly amend the Plastic Waste Management Rules.

2. An Environment (Protection) Act/Plastic Waste Management Rules amendment should state that the packaging/material standard in rule 11(2) does not authorise a plastic article, material or component that is prohibited by a valid food-safety enactment or regulation. This is a coordination clause, not a substitute for the FSS Act prohibition.

3. State/UT and local instruments should implement collection, segregation, return, safe treatment, reuse infrastructure and public education under their lawful waste and local-government powers. They may not create a new tax, criminal offence, secret exemption or unreviewable closure power unless separately authorised by statute.

## Expired and unsafe-food destruction rule

An operator that controls an expired, recalled, condemned or unsafe food article shall preserve the batch, source, quantity, decision, destination, destruction method, date, responsible person and independent confirmation in a traceable record. The operator bears the reasonable cost of lawful disposal; public recovery of cost requires notice, itemised calculation, reasons, review and court remedy. A regulator may not require destruction before the sample/record necessary for an independent challenge is preserved, except where a documented immediate safety risk requires a proportionate urgent measure.

## Waste-charge safeguard

Any waste charge requires an Act that defines the liable person, measurable base, rate/rate-setting limit, exemptions, hardship support, use of proceeds, audit, public reporting, review and appeal. It must not be imposed through a licence condition, unpublished direction or discretionary demand. Collection, inspection and recovery functions must be separated from adjudication and independently auditable.
