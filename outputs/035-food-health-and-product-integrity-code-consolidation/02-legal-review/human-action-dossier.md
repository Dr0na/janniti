# Human-Action Dossier — Food, Feed and Delivery Integrity Package

## Decisions requiring a designated human authority

| Decision | Responsible authority | Why an agent cannot decide it | Required record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accept source snapshots | Designated source reviewer | The repository governance rule prohibits automated acceptance. | URL, issuer, version, status, pinpoint, hash, reviewer, date, impacts and corrections. |
| Choose legislative sponsor, House and filing route | Member/Minister and parliamentary/legislative secretariat | These are institutional decisions and may require recommendations or clearance. | Sponsor authority, legislative route, official form, admissibility determination and ancillary memoranda. |
| Set levy rates, public expenditure and transition funding | Competent legislature/executive under lawful budget process | A draft cannot appropriate funds or determine fiscal policy. | Financial Memorandum, cost model, budget authority, audit plan and review date. |
| Adopt State/UT/local measures | Competent State/UT legislature and local authority under State law | A model Act cannot legislate in a State or municipality. | Jurisdictional competence, local source review, consultation record and adopted text. |
| Final proportionality and scientific thresholds | Legislature/regulator with independent expert and public input | Penalty, materials and health thresholds need current evidence and democratic accountability. | Evidence review, conflict disclosures, alternatives, impact assessment and reasons. |

## Filing sequence

1. Human-review and accept every primary-source snapshot in the relevant matter folder.
2. Select the standalone BNS/BNSS amendment as the first filing candidate; keep it separate from the broader package.
3. Convert each FSSAI, consumer, Legal Metrology and environment design draft into a Gazette-ready redline against accepted controlling text.
4. Run the formal readiness gate for each instrument separately, including fiscal and delegated-legislation memoranda where applicable.
5. Conduct accessible public consultation and publish responses, especially on plastic transition, worker privacy, small-vendor impact, food costs and State/UT adoption.
6. Introduce only the instruments that pass their own competence, source, rights and formal gates.

## Do not do

Do not claim that the master package is enacted, sponsor-approved, source-accepted, legally conclusive or safe from all misuse. Do not bundle State/local, animal-feed, tax/levy or unrelated criminal provisions into the BNS amendment Bill merely to accelerate filing.
