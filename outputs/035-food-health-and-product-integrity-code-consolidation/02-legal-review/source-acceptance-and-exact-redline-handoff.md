# Source Acceptance and Exact Redline Handoff

This is the only corrective action that an agent cannot close. Under `governance/SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.md`, a designated human reviewer must accept each official source snapshot, recording identity, legal status, version/stage, date, pinpoint, authoritative language, completeness, hash, impacted artefacts and required correction.

After acceptance, the legal drafter shall produce a line-by-line redline for each affected FSSAI, Consumer, Legal Metrology, DPDP, Environment and State/UT instrument; rerun citation verification, legislative lint, constitutional stress test, power map and readiness gate; and record any unresolved item as a blocker.
