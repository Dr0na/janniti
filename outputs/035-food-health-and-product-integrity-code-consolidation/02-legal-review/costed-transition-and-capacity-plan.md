# Costed Transition and Capacity Plan

**Planning template, not a fiscal estimate.** Amounts must be supplied from verified procurement, State/UT and market data before any Financial Memorandum is finalised.

| Workstream | Unit | Required input | Funding safeguards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plastic-free food-contact transition | affected business / product line | approved alternative material cost, testing, redesign, stock run-off | time-limited grant/credit; public criteria; no vendor tied to political intermediary |
| Hygiene upgrades | premises | water, drainage, cold storage, handwashing, pest control, worker training | priority for high-risk/low-capacity operators; independent inspection of works |
| Laboratory capacity | lab / test | equipment, accreditation, staff, proficiency testing, referral capacity | conflict screen; published turnaround and method; no pay-to-pass model |
| Digital transparency | platform / public authority | record system, accessibility, cybersecurity, privacy controls | interoperable open standard; minimal data; independent security audit |
| Local implementation | local body | collection, disposal, assistance desk, language access, public warning | formula-based allocation; public ledger; audit and grievance route |

1. Publish baseline cost, beneficiary, selection method, disbursement, outcome and audit data.
2. Do not make compliance conditional on unaffordable technology where lower-cost safe alternatives or support can meet the purpose.
3. Use staged commencement only where safety allows it; prohibit new unsafe procurement while transition support is being built.
4. Recover remediation/disposal cost only after notice, itemised calculation, reasons, review and court remedy.
5. Review affordability, food prices, worker effects, rural access and disparate impact at six and twelve months.
