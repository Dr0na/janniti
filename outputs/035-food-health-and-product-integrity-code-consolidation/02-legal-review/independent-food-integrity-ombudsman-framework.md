# Independent Food Integrity Ombudsman Framework

The Ombudsman receives complaints against inspectors, laboratories, platforms, regulators and contracted entities concerning extortion, retaliation, discrimination, evidence handling, delay, conflict or abuse of power.

It must be structurally separate from implicated agencies; use published appointment/conflict rules; preserve evidence; provide interim anti-retaliation protection; hear affected persons fairly; publish reasons and anonymised statistics; recommend correction, discipline, restitution or referral; and preserve court/judicial-review routes. It may not prosecute, adjudicate criminal guilt, direct a pending court case or become an unchecked regulator.
