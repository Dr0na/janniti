# Model State/UT and Local Implementation Schedule

Draft implementation package for clauses 19–32, 41–47, 80–91 and 92–116. Each State/UT adoption measure must identify its constitutional and statutory authority; designate separate licensing, inspection, laboratory, investigation, prosecution and review functions; establish local hygiene, waste, reuse, complaint and public-warning systems; and fund accessible support for small and informal vendors. It must require recorded objective grounds, random/auditable allocation, independent testing, notice, reasons, appeal, court remedy, equality audits, anti-vendetta controls and sanctions for official extortion or selective enforcement. Local bodies may implement only within published State authority and may not create secret exemptions, new criminal offences, political filters or unreviewable closures. Each jurisdiction requires its own primary-source Act, Gazette, municipal-law and High Court review before adoption.
