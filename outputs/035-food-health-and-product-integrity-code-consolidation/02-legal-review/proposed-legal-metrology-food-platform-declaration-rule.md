# Proposed Legal Metrology Food-Platform Declaration Rule

**Draft text; not enacted.** This is proposed as a new rule 6A in the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. It supplements package declarations and does not displace FSS Act food-safety or labelling duties.

## 6A. Digital and food-service transaction declarations

(1) A person who offers a packaged food, prepared food or food-delivery order for retail sale through a digital interface shall, before the consumer is committed to payment, display prominently and accessibly—

(a) the responsible food business and the quantity, net quantity, portion basis or standard serving basis;

(b) the unit price where reasonably practicable, food-item price, mandatory charge, tax, delivery charge, service/convenience/packaging charge, discount, discount funder and final payable amount;

(c) the identity of the marketplace or delivery intermediary and its commission or margin where it receives one;

(d) the applicable mandatory declaration, ingredient, allergen, date, recall and food-safety information required by law; and

(e) the refund, complaint and independent-review route.

(2) A declaration shall not use a false reference price, hidden mandatory charge, misleading quantity comparison, false urgency, fictitious discount, deceptive package image, materially inaccurate menu/portion representation or other presentation likely to cause a consumer to make a transaction they would not otherwise make.

(3) A platform and responsible seller shall preserve the displayed declaration and material price calculation for at least one year after the transaction, subject to applicable data-protection law.

(4) The Central Government shall prescribe measurement and verification methods, accessibility standards, correction procedure, proportional sanctions and appeal. No person may be penalised without notice, evidence, reasons and an effective review route, except for a temporary, necessary and proportionate urgent safety measure.
