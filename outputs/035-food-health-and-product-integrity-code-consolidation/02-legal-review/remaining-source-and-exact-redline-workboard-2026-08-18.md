## Exact-redline template

Use this template once, and only once, each row has a complete accepted source set:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Instrument identity | Exact title, issuing authority, legal status, publication/commencement history and authoritative language. |
| Source ledger | Direct official URL, snapshot filename, SHA-256, retrieval time, acceptance decision and pinpoint. |
| Existing text | Exact operative text, with regulation/rule/section/sub-rule/paragraph identifier. |
| Proposed operation | Insert, substitute, omit, repeal, saving, transitional or commencement provision. |
| Proposed text | Clean legislative text only; no explanation inside the operative column. |
| Authority and competence | Parent Act provision and Union/State/local allocation. |
| Rights and safeguards | Trigger, evidence, reasons, checker, record, challenge, remedy, equality, privacy, anti-vendetta and expiry controls. |
| Consequential changes | Cross-references, forms, licences, records, offence/procedure, fiscal, data and State/local effects. |
| Verification | Citation, lint, constitutional stress test, power map, fiscal/privacy/equality review and acceptance status. |

## Exit criteria

A route moves from this workboard to an exact-redline schedule only when every required source is official, locally snapshotted, hash-pinned, accepted by a designated human reviewer, current at the stated date, and mapped to a particular clause. A route moves to filing review only when its competence, rights, fiscal and procedural gates pass.
