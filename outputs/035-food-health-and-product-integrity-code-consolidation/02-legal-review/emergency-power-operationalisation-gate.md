# Emergency-Power Operationalisation Gate

**Classification:** proposed implementation safeguard, not current law and not authority to exercise an emergency power.

## Rule

No food, feed, platform, waste or public-health emergency measure may be used until the competent legislature or rule-maker identifies the precise statutory vehicle and publishes a protocol that meets this gate. An administrative label, informal direction, funding condition, algorithmic flag or repeated short order cannot substitute for that authority.

## Required protocol

The published protocol must identify the objective trigger; evidence threshold; named decision-maker; geographic and subject-matter scope; least-restrictive alternatives considered; reasons; notice; affected-person hearing or urgent post-decision hearing; access to records; independent reviewer; court forum; restoration/compensation route; expiry; and public register. It must set an initial duration no longer than 48 hours (or a shorter existing lawful maximum), permit no renewal without a fresh recorded evidence assessment and independent written authorisation, and require legislative approval within seven days for any measure that would otherwise continue.

Any immediate measure must be limited to preventing an evidenced, imminent and serious harm, expire within the stated limit, and receive prompt independent review. It may not create a new offence, impose a punitive consequence, suppress lawful speech, target a person for political activity or criticism, or displace ordinary appeal and judicial remedies.

## Review roster and exercise

The relevant court or legally authorised reviewer must establish a conflict-screened, publicly described roster for urgent review. Case allocation must comply with the [Neutral Case Allocation Standard](../08-institutional-power-map/neutral-case-allocation-standard.md). The responsible authority must conduct a no-consequence tabletop exercise covering notice, reasons, review, expiry, restoration and publication; publish its result; correct any failure; and retest before operational use.

## Evidence of completion

Completion requires the enacted or lawfully notified authority, a source-accepted exact redline, published protocol and register with the duration/renewal controls above, reviewer roster confirmation, independent exercise report, and a named route for accessible legal aid. Until all are present, no emergency measure is cleared for implementation.
