# Side-by-Side Consumer E-Commerce and Delivery Amendment Schedule

## Status

Draft legislative schedule for working-Bill clauses 104–107. It is not current law or an introduction-ready instrument.

## Verified baseline

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2021 substituted rule 4(1) to require covered e-commerce entities to appoint an India-resident nodal officer or alternate senior designated functionary for compliance. The official dark-pattern guidelines apply to platforms, advertisers and sellers and prohibit specified deceptive interface practices.

Sources: [official e-Gazette E-Commerce amendment](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2021/227003.pdf); [official e-Gazette dark-pattern guidelines](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2023/250339.pdf), retrieved 16 August 2026.

## Proposed amendments

1. In the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, after rule 4, insert a rule requiring an e-commerce entity that lists, orders, delivers or arranges food to disclose before order placement: the food business’s legal and beneficial owner; physical preparation location; licence/registration identifier; current menu; the price charged by the food business for the identical offline item; every delivery-platform charge; platform commission or margin; taxes; discounts and the person funding each discount; and an accessible complaint/refund route.

2. Require the entity to preserve tamper-evident order, price, refund, disclosure and complaint records; prohibit undisclosed ranking, commission, charge, inventory, availability or price manipulation; and prohibit a platform from representing a food business, kitchen, menu, price, rating or offer in a materially false or misleading manner.

3. Require refunds within the stated period or, if no lawful shorter period is disclosed, within seven days after entitlement is established. A delay caused by the platform attracts interest at the published statutory rate and a reasoned, reviewable correction route.

4. Require a food-delivery platform to provide a live or recorded customer-accessible kitchen-view link only subject to the separate food-safety, privacy, worker-dignity, security, data-minimisation and accessibility safeguards in the Union food-safety instrument. The consumer rule must not create a surveillance exception.

5. Preserve the resident nodal-officer duty. The nodal officer must receive a complaint, preserve evidence, identify the decision-maker, give written reasons and provide a complaint reference; the officer may not decide individual guilt, suppress lawful criticism or deny access to the consumer commissions or courts.

6. Enforcement must be based on published criteria, notice, opportunity to correct non-urgent errors, reasons, proportionate remedy, equality audit and appeal. No rule permits extortion, selective enforcement, retaliation or compelled disclosure of personal data beyond lawful necessity.

## Required companion instruments

These amendments require exact coordination with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, Legal Metrology package-declaration law, DPDP law, Competition Act, food licensing rules and the Union food-safety Bill. They must not create a duplicate unchecked platform regulator.
