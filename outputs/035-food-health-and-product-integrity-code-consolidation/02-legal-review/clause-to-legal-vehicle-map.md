# Clause-to-Legal-Vehicle Map

## Status and method

This is a **drafting allocation**, not a statement of current law or a final competence opinion. It assigns every working-Bill clause to the legal vehicle that must be researched and scheduled before the Bill can be formalised. “Union Act” means an exact amendment or new Union statute; “FSS regulation” means a regulation under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006; “Model State/UT” means a model law or adoption measure, not a unilateral Union override; and “local rule” means a State-authorised municipal or local-body instrument.

## Compulsory drafting split

The working Bill cannot remain one undifferentiated enactment. Its next version must be separated into:

1. **Food, Feed and Delivery Integrity Bill (Union food/consumer core)** — provisions demonstrably within Parliament’s route, including the FSS Act amendment schedule and consumer/delivery amendments.
2. **Model State and Union Territory Food, Feed, Public Health and Local Enforcement Act** — inspection implementation, local food-service conditions, animal/feed interfaces, local waste and anti-harassment safeguards.
3. **Model local by-law package** — premises, visible-kitchen and local waste/reuse infrastructure, always subject to State authority and constitutional safeguards.
4. **Regulation packages** — FSS packaging, labelling/display, licensing, advertising/claims, laboratory/audit, recall and standards instruments, each tied to an enabling section and Gazette process.

## Sequenced schedule work

1. Finish the FSS Act/Packaging schedule, including the already drafted repeal and non-revival of the 2025 recycled-PET permission.
2. Draft the FSS regulation schedules for clauses 7–12, 19–24, 33–40, 48–79 and 80–83 only after obtaining the full chronological Gazette text.
3. Draft CPA/LMA/DPDP/Competition schedules for clauses 7–12, 41–57 and 104–107.
4. Create the separate State/UT and local packages for clauses 19–32, 41–47, 80–91 and 92–116.
5. Run the competence, fiscal, equality, privacy, criminal-law and institutional-power gates against each instrument, then create the formal Parliament package only for the Union core.
