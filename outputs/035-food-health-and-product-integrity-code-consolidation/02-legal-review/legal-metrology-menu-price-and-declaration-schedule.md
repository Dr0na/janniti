# Legal Metrology, Menu, Price and Declaration Schedule

Draft schedule for clauses 7–8, 58, 62, 73–74 and 104–107. Legal Metrology Act, 2009 section 18(1) is the verified statutory anchor for mandatory package declarations and section 18(2) for retail-price advertisements. The eventual Union package must amend the Packaged Commodities Rules, coordinate FSS ingredient/allergen/date disclosure, and require a food seller and delivery platform to show the actual responsible business, item, quantity/portion basis, mandatory charges, tax, platform margin/commission, discount funder, comparison basis and refund route before an order. It must prohibit misleading quantity, price, origin, menu, rating, availability and discount representations. Enforcement requires published measurement methods, correction, reasons, equality safeguards, independent appeal and no discretionary extortion. Current Rules amendment history remains pending primary-source verification.
