# Rulemaking and Notification Toolkit

## Use

For FSSAI, Consumer Affairs, Legal Metrology and State/UT departments. This toolkit is a drafting checklist, not a notification or proof of authority.

1. Identify enabling Act, exact section, competent rule-maker, consultation/layering requirement and commencement power.
2. State the problem, evidence, affected groups, alternatives, equality/privacy/federalism impact and expected cost.
3. Draft definitions, scope, duties, evidence threshold, records, reasons, correction route, independent review, appeal and expiry/review.
4. Publish a draft notification with an accessible consultation notice, translations, evidence register and response deadline.
5. Prepare a final notification with a reasoned consultation report, conflict disclosure, commencement/transition date, implementation guide and monitoring indicators.
6. Hash-pin official Gazette publication, run impact assessment and obtain designated human source acceptance before changing any operative package claim.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not use guidance, licence conditions, contracts, platform terms, algorithms or informal directions to create an offence, tax/levy, compulsory surveillance, final closure, secret exemption or restriction that requires statute or published rule.
