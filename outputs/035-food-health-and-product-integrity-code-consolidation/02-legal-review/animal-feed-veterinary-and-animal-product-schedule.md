# Animal Feed, Veterinary and Animal-Product Schedule

Draft schedule for clauses 92–103 and 108–111. The FSS Act definition of “food” expressly excludes animal feed, so these provisions cannot be treated as an FSS Act amendment. Existing State examples include the Andhra Pradesh Animal Feed (Regulation of Manufacture, Quality Control, Sale and Distribution) Act, 2020 and the Kerala Livestock Feed, Poultry Feed and Mineral Mixture (Regulation of Manufacture, Storage, Distribution, Sale and Quality Assurance) Act, 2023. The package must therefore be a model State/UT instrument covering feed composition and traceability, veterinary medicines and residues, zoonotic alerts, aquaculture, species testing, humane handling, slaughter/animal-product hygiene, upstream water/agricultural inputs, and protected reporting. It must establish species-appropriate scientific standards, independent laboratories, due process, recall and compensation routes, and equal support for small, traditional and community producers. Each State/UT route remains to be primary-source verified.
