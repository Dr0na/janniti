# Side-by-Side FSS Advertising and Claims Amendment Schedule

## Status

Draft schedule for working-Bill clauses 7–12. It is proposed text, not current law or an introduction-ready instrument.

## Verified baseline

FSSAI’s official regulatory index lists the Food Safety and Standards (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018 and amendments. Its official 2020 notification index identifies the First Amendment Regulations, 2020 concerning edible-vegetable-oil claims. The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 section 24 is the statutory baseline for advertising restrictions and unfair trade practices.

Sources: [FSSAI regulation index](https://fssai.gov.in/cms/food-safety-and-standards-regulations.php); [FSSAI 2020 notification index](https://fssai.gov.in/notifications.php?pages=17); [FSS Act — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/13658?locale=en), retrieved 16 August 2026.

## Proposed amendments

1. Under FSS Act section 24, prohibit a food advertisement, label, online listing, influencer promotion or environmental/nutrition/health claim unless it is specific, independently verifiable, supported by publicly accessible evidence appropriate to the claim, and identifies the responsible advertiser and sponsor.

2. Require a public evidence register containing the protocol, data availability, analytical method, funding, sponsor role, conflicts, adverse findings, limitations and independent replication status. A claim may not represent sponsored, selectively reported, ghost-written or manipulated research as independent.

3. Require a paid publisher, platform, broadcaster, agency or influencer with knowledge, control or material benefit to label paid content, preserve sponsor/evidence records and provide an accessible correction route. Neutral transmission without knowledge, control or material benefit does not itself create liability.

4. Provide proportionate remedies: correction, withdrawal, recall, consumer notice, recovery of unlawful gain and court-supervised market restriction for deliberate grave deception. No punitive action without notice, evidence disclosure, independent decision, reasons and appeal.

5. Preserve scientific inquiry, lawful criticism, media freedom, trade-secret limits, privacy and due process. A regulator may not use an advertising rule for political retaliation, censorship or selective enforcement.
