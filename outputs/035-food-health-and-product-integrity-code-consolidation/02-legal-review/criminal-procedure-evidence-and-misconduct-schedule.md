# Criminal Procedure, Evidence and Misconduct Schedule

Draft schedule for clauses 1–3, 13, 43–48, 55 and 78. The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 contains the existing food-adulteration offence; the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 supplies the court, prosecution and procedure framework. A final Union instrument must define each additional offence’s prohibited act, mental element, causation/risk threshold, defences, responsible person, penalty range, court, limitation, bail/cognisability classification, evidence rule, compensation and appeal. It must separate sampling, investigation, prosecution and adjudication; require independent laboratories, preservation, disclosure and defence testing; and prohibit reverse burdens, retrospective punishment, secret standards and collective liability. Misuse by an inspector, police officer, prosecutor, regulator or public official must have an independent complaint, evidence-preservation, investigation, reasons, proportionate consequence and appeal route. Exact BNS/BNSS schedules and evidence-law cross-references remain pending primary-source verification.
