## Gate result

**Not ready for introduction.** The formal gate cannot pass because the instrument is a 116-clause policy draft without a settled legislative route, exact current-text amendments/repeals, accepted Gazette-source set, competence allocation, financial analysis, criminal-procedure mapping or State/UT implementation schedule.

## Why no clean Bill was relabelled as ready

The official Manual describes different procedures for government and private Members’ Bills. This record cannot invent a sponsor, House, recommendation, financial-incidence conclusion or secretariat clearance. The draft also raises potential fiscal, criminal-law, privacy and State/local competence questions. Calling it ready before those facts are resolved would misstate both the law and the parliamentary process.
