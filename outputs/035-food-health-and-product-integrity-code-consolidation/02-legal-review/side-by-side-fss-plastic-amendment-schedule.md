# Side-by-Side FSS Plastic Amendment and Repeal Schedule

## Status

This is an exact proposed legislative schedule for the discrete recycled-PET permission identified in the official 2025 Gazette notification. It is **draft text**, not current law, enacted repeal, or proof that the full 116-clause Bill is ready for introduction.

## Current authority checked

The Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) First Amendment Regulations, 2025, official Gazette text dated 28 March 2025, substitutes clause (e) of sub-regulation (4) of regulation 4 of the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 2018. Its operative text allows products made of recycled polyethylene terephthalate to be used for packaging, storing, carrying or dispensing food when standards and guidelines are notified by the Food Authority, subject to applicable national standards or regulations. It states that it comes into force on publication in the Official Gazette.

Source: [official Gazette of India text](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/262130.pdf), retrieved 16 August 2026; local snapshot SHA-256 `5c1481cc3262a2abf909172db56ff6395207db4969b55fcadf3fb15db38dd4d1`, pending documented human source acceptance.

## Proposed amendment schedule

| Item | Current law / instrument | Proposed action | Exact proposed result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, section 23 (Packaging and labelling of foods) | Insert new section 23A after section 23 | A statutory prohibition on plastic in every stage of the human-food chain, including recycled PET; inconsistent delegated instruments are void to the extent of inconsistency. |
| 2 | Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) First Amendment Regulations, 2025 | Repeal the entire Regulations | Removes the 2025 substituted permission for recycled PET. |
| 3 | Earlier text of regulation 4(4)(e), if any | Non-revival clause | Prevents a prior plastic permission from returning merely because the 2025 amendment is repealed. |
| 4 | Existing FSSAI approvals, guidelines, standards and licence conditions | Cessation clause | Ends any authorisation of recycled PET or another prohibited plastic article when proposed section 23A commences. |
| 5 | Commencement | Fixed statutory timetable | New procurement/manufacture/import stops on assent; full prohibition begins 180 days after assent; Government/FSSAI must publish safeguards within 90 days but cannot create a plastic exception. |

This schedule resolves the identified 2025 recycled-PET permission. It does not settle the other current regulations, the whole-Bill competence allocation, State/UT implementation, fiscal design, privacy, criminal-law schedules, official language/formal filing requirements or source-acceptance record. Those issues keep the consolidated Bill in `not-ready-for-introduction` status.
