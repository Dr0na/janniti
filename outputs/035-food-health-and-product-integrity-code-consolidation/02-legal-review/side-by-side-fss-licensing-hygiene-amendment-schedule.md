# Side-by-Side FSS Licensing, Hygiene and Expiry Amendment Schedule

## Status

Draft legislative text for the verified 2026 licensing baseline; not current law and not proof that the complete Bill is introduction-ready.

## Verified current text

The Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Second Amendment Regulations, 2026 substitutes Schedule 2 Annexure 3 serial 8 so that daily production/raw-material records are not required of non-manufacturing businesses, and substitutes Schedule 4 Part II paragraph 5.2.5 so FIFO/FEFO storage does not apply to retailers.

## Proposed amendment text

In the Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011, as amended, substitute the following provisions:

1. In Schedule 2, Annexure 3, under “Conditions of License”, for serial number 8, substitute:

   “8. Every food business operator shall maintain daily, batch-linked records of food received, produced, prepared, stored, transferred, sold, returned, recalled, donated and destroyed, including the responsible supplier or recipient and applicable use-by/expiry date. A non-manufacturing business may use a simplified accessible record, but is not exempt from traceability, expiry, recall or safe-storage records.”

2. In Schedule 4, Part II, under “Food Operations and Controls”, for paragraph 5.2.5, substitute:

   “5.2.5 Raw materials, ingredients, work-in-progress and processed, cooked or packaged food products shall be stored and rotated according to first-in-first-out and first-expire-first-out principles. This requirement applies to every food business operator, including a retailer, marketplace, warehouse, delivery-platform fulfilment operation and food-service business, subject only to a documented, equally safe method approved under published criteria.”

3. No exemption, licence condition, direction, platform term or private contract may reduce the duties in paragraphs 1 and 2. Enforcement must use objective records, independent testing where relevant, reasons, proportionate correction time, equality safeguards, anti-extortion safeguards and prompt review.

## Bill linkage
