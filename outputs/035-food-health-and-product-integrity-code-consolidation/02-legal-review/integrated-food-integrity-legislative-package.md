# Integrated Food Integrity Legislative Package

## Scope and classification

This is a **draft assembly record**, not current law. It consolidates the legislative vehicles required for the working Food, Feed and Delivery Integrity Bill without pretending that a single Union Act can displace State, local, delegated or existing special-law authority.

## Implementation order

1. Introduce the narrow BNS/BNSS Bill as a standalone criminal-law amendment; do not wait for unrelated platform, plastic, feed or municipal provisions.
2. Use FSS Act and FSSAI regulation-making routes for human-food standards, licensing, sampling, labelling, claims and recalls, after publishing the evidence and transition assessment.
3. Use Consumer Protection and Legal Metrology delegated-law routes for platform/disclosure and price/quantity duties, with DPDP-compliant data conditions.
4. Use a model State/UT adoption package for animal feed, local hygiene, markets, municipal waste and local enforcement functions.
5. Conduct separate fiscal, environmental, accessibility, competition and federalism assessments before any waste charge, blanket plastic prohibition or State/local mandate.

## Non-negotiable safeguards for every instrument

1. No criminal or licence action without a published legal basis, objective evidence, notice, reasons, effective independent review and court remedy.
2. Separate inspection, laboratory analysis, prosecution recommendation, prosecution decision and adjudication; record each hand-off.
3. Preserve samples, disclose inculpatory and material exculpatory evidence, permit lawful defence testing, and protect whistleblowers and complainants from retaliation.
4. Do not use food-safety, advertising, licence, hygiene, data, plastic or market powers for political retaliation, discrimination, extortion, censorship, anti-competitive exclusion or punishment of lawful criticism.
5. Publish aggregate, privacy-protective enforcement, laboratory, recall, complaint, compensation, reversal, delay, equality and official-misconduct statistics.
6. Provide proportionate transition support, accessible compliance assistance and language/disability access; safety obligations apply equally, but enforcement must distinguish deliberate harm from an honest, remediable or technically uncertain failure.

## Matters deliberately reserved for human authority

1. Acceptance of primary-source snapshots, including the current version and authoritative language, under `governance/SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.md`.
2. Parliamentary/State-legislature form, sponsor, filing, admissibility, financial memorandum and delegated-legislation determination.
3. State/UT and municipal legal verification, legislative choice and local consultation.
4. Final proportionality, fiscal, environmental, scientific and competition determinations for criminal penalties, plastic restrictions and waste charges.
5. Enactment, appointment, prosecution, adjudication and commencement decisions.
