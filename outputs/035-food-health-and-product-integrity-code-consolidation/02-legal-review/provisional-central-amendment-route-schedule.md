# Provisional Central Amendment-Route Schedule

## Status and limitation

This is a **routing schedule**, not an exact amendment schedule. It identifies the currently verified central provisions that must be opened and checked before each working-Bill chapter is converted into statutory text. It does not claim completeness or resolve State/UT/local competence.

| Working-Bill subject | Current official baseline | Proposed legislative route | Exact work still required |
|---|---|---|---|
| General food safety, evidence, consumer interest and standards | FSS Act, 2006 s.18 | Amend FSS Act principles and add bounded standards/rulemaking provisions only where required | Retrieve current consolidated text and every amendment/regulation; specify clauses. |
| Additives, contaminants, residues, GM/novel food, nutrition | FSS Act ss.19–22 | Targeted FSS Act/FSSAI-regulation redline | Confirm current Food Products/Additives, labelling and related regulations, including every later Gazette amendment. |
| Packaging, labels, expiry, menu/online disclosures, advertising | FSS Act ss.23–24; Legal Metrology Act, 2009 s.36; Consumer Protection Act, 2019 s.21 | Coordinated FSS/legal-metrology/consumer amendments and rules | The FSSAI index confirms that the 2011 packaging-and-labelling regime is superseded and identifies 2025/2026 amendments to the 2020 Labelling and Display Regulations. Retrieve these controlling Gazette texts before reconciling declarations, online listing, menu/price and misleading-advertisement provisions. |
| Operator liability, supply chain, recall, platform chain records | FSS Act ss.26–28 | Amend FSS Act and regulations; preserve existing responsibility architecture | Check precise scope for platforms, warehouses, animal feed and related entities. |
| Inspection, sampling, emergency action, State implementation | FSS Act ss.29–42 | FSS Act amendment plus model State/local implementation instrument | Confirm State Commissioner/Food Safety Officer provisions and procedural safeguards in every jurisdiction. |
| Food penalties, unsafe food, false information, companies | FSS Act ss.49–67; BNS/BNSS/BSA | Consolidate/adjust FSS penalties; use targeted criminal-law amendment only after exact element/schedule review | No new serious criminal offence should be finalised until BNS/BNSS/BSA mapping is complete. |
| Consumer refunds, false claims, platform remedies | Consumer Protection Act, 2019 ss.18–24; consumer-commission chapters | Consumer amendment/rule route; preserve CCPA and Commission remedies | Verify e-commerce/dark-pattern rules and current case/regulatory material. |
| Online platform competition, commissions, ranking, merger/concentration | Competition Act, 2002 ss.3–6 | Competition-law amendment/guidance route; do not create overlapping platform enforcer without separation | Economic analysis, CCI procedure and constitutional trade/business review. |
| Digital live-stream, order, location and complaint data | DPDP Act, 2023 and 2025 Rules | Data-law-compatible sector obligations; data minimisation/retention schedule | Verify all commencement notices, rights, exemptions and rule text. |
| Complete food/feed-chain plastic ban, destruction, waste levy | Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 s.25; Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016; FSS (Packaging) Regulations, 2018 regulation 4(4)(e), as substituted by the 2025 First Amendment | Working draft Schedule A inserts proposed FSS Act s.23A; repeals the 2025 Packaging First Amendment; and prevents revival of an earlier plastic permission. Environment-rule and State/local waste/fiscal instruments remain necessary for the full chain. | The 2025 Packaging amendment permits recycled PET for specified food-contact uses. Schedule A now supplies proposed express repeal/non-revival text. Verify the complete historic/current text, occupational/hygiene implications, local waste powers and levy competence before formal introduction. |
| Animal feed, veterinary controls and animal products | FSS Act human-food scope does not alone settle full animal-feed architecture | Separate competence research; potential Union/State/model-law package | Retrieve veterinary, animal-husbandry, feed, slaughter, fisheries and State laws. |

## Official primary-source routes checked 16 August 2026

- [Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/13658?locale=en): ss.18–31 and ss.49–67 listed in the current India Code route.
- [Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15256?locale=en): CCPA/consumer route; s.21 is the false/misleading-advertisement provision.
- [Legal Metrology Act, 2009 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2102?locale=en): s.36 concerns non-standard package declarations.
- [Competition Act, 2002 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2010?locale=en): ss.3–6 cover anti-competitive agreements, abuse of dominance and combinations.
- [Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/18574): s.25 and linked Plastic Waste Management Rules route.
- [FSSAI regulations index](https://fssai.gov.in/cms/food-safety-and-standards-regulations.php): official index identifying the Packaging Regulations, 2018; Labelling and Display Regulations, 2020; and their resources. Retrieved 2026-08-16. This index is a routing source, not the controlling text of a regulation or amendment.
- [FSSAI superseded-regulations notice](https://fssai.gov.in/cms/superseded-regulations.php): states that the 2011 Packaging and Labelling Regulations were superseded by the 2020 Labelling and Display Regulations and warns that original notifications/amendments prevail over compendia. Retrieved 2026-08-16.
- [FSSAI Labelling and Display amendment index](https://fssai.gov.in/cms/amendment-fss-labelling-display.php): lists amendments including 8 August 2025 and 24 March 2026. Retrieved 2026-08-16. Exact Gazette text and commencement effect remain to be verified.
- [Packaging First Amendment Regulations, 2025](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FSS_Packaging_Amendment%20Regulations2025%20related%20to%20recycled%20plastics.pdf): official Gazette text dated 28 March 2025; substitutes regulation 4(4)(e) and states Gazette-publication commencement. Retrieved 2026-08-16.
- [Labelling and Display First Amendment Regulations, 2025](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2025/08/689d8d163d422coffee%20chicory_gazette.pdf): official Gazette text; Schedule II paragraph 2.2 substitution for coffee–chicory mixture; commencement 1 July 2026. Retrieved 2026-08-16.
- [Labelling and Display First Amendment Regulations, 2026](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2026/04/69cca2b3f3ce9Notification%20dt%2024.03.2026_NRC.pdf): official Gazette text dated 24 March 2026; amendments to regulations 5, 8 and 10; commencement 1 July 2027. Retrieved 2026-08-16.
- [Manual of Parliamentary Procedures](https://sansad.in/uploads/English_Manual_GOI_E_ccb7bb7ef7.pdf?updated_at=2022-09-13T06%3A19%3A45.349Z): paragraphs 9.23.1–9.23.4, private Member’s Bills and competence/Article 117 procedure. Retrieved 2026-08-16.
