# Public Procurement Integrity Standard

Apply open, competitive, accessible and conflict-screened procurement for laboratories, auditors, software providers, waste contractors and public-support providers.

Publish scope, eligibility, evaluation method, beneficial ownership, conflicts, bid record, award reasons, contract, variation, performance, payment, audit and complaint route. Separate specification, evaluation, award, contract management and audit. Prohibit outcome-linked laboratory payment, secret subcontracting, political direction, retaliation and vendor lock-in without an independently justified interoperability exception.
