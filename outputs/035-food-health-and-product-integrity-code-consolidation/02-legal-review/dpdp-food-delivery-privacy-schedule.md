# DPDP Food-Delivery Privacy Schedule

Draft schedule for clauses 19, 23, 32, 54 and 104–106. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is the statutory baseline for processing digital personal data. Before collecting kitchen video, order, location, worker, complaint or whistleblower data, the eventual Union instrument must specify lawful purpose, data minimisation, separate role-based access, retention/deletion dates, security controls, notice, accessible grievance route, independent audit and judicial remedy. Live-view must exclude private areas, personal identifiers and audio unless independently justified. No platform, regulator or employer may use safety data for political retaliation, discrimination, labour surveillance or commercial profiling. Exact rules and commencement cross-references remain pending current-source verification.
