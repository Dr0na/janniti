# DPDP Food-Delivery Privacy Schedule

## Verified current-law baseline

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is the statutory baseline for digital personal data. Gazette notification G.S.R. 843(E), published on 13 November 2025, brings specified institutional provisions into force immediately, specified provisions after one year, and sections 3–5, most of section 6, sections 7–17, most of section 27, sections 28–34, 36–37 and section 44(2) after eighteen months. As of 16 August 2026, the latter group is not yet in force.

Source: [G.S.R. 843(E), 13 November 2025](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf). The hash-pinned official snapshot in `14-legal-change-monitoring/snapshots/` was accepted by the designated human reviewer on 18 August 2026. The acceptance is limited to this commencement notification, not the complete DPDP Act/Rules source set.

## Proposed sectoral rule

For clauses 19, 23, 32, 54 and 104–106, the eventual food-delivery rule shall provide that a platform, food business, laboratory, regulator or service provider may process kitchen video, order, location, worker, complaint or whistleblower data only where it is necessary for a stated safety, recall, delivery, payment, refund, legal-compliance or dispute purpose and subject to the following conditions:—

1. Collect no more data than necessary for the stated purpose; do not collect continuous audio, private-area video, biometric data or precise location where a less intrusive record suffices.

2. Give an accessible, plain-language notice identifying the data fiduciary or responsible entity, purpose, categories, recipients, retention period, complaint route and material safety exception.

3. Use role-based access, immutable access logs, encryption, separation of customer, worker, food-business and regulator records, and a documented retention/deletion schedule.

4. Do not use safety data for political retaliation, discrimination, labour surveillance, commercial profiling, unrelated policing, behavioural advertising, ranking manipulation or a purpose incompatible with the disclosed lawful purpose.

5. A public kitchen-view facility, if required by a valid food-safety instrument, must exclude changing, rest, payment, private storage and other non-preparation areas; mask personal identifiers; operate without audio unless independently justified; provide a non-video accessibility alternative; and preserve no footage longer than necessary for a documented safety incident or complaint.

6. Provide a low-cost accessible grievance route, human review of a material automated decision, correction of inaccurate data, an independent audit, regulatory complaint and court remedy. A safety complaint or correction request must not be used to retaliate against a customer, worker, vendor, journalist or whistleblower.

## Commencement rule

No sectoral rule may purport to narrow, suspend or evade the DPDP Act. It shall state which duties apply immediately under its own lawful authority and which must commence no earlier than the corresponding DPDP provisions. Until the full DPDP duties commence, equivalent safeguards in this schedule remain conditions of the food-delivery licence, contract or regulatory approval only to the extent authorised by the enabling food/consumer law.
