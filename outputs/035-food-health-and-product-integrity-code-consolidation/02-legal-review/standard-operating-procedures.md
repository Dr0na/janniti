# Standard Operating Procedures

## Inspector

Record objective grounds or risk-allocation method; identify yourself; provide receipt; use calibrated equipment; preserve chain of custody; inform the affected person of sample/challenge rights; record reasons; and provide review route. Never demand a benefit, make political inquiry, use unnecessary force or alter evidence.

## Laboratory

Conflict-screen staff; validate method; log seal/condition; preserve raw data and uncertainty; use quality control and proficiency testing; report limitations; disclose material records; and enable lawful referral/defence testing. Do not accept outcome-linked payment or suppress adverse/negative results.

## Recall coordinator

Verify risk; preserve evidence; issue a targeted timely warning; notify supply-chain actors; monitor completion; publish a privacy-safe closure report; and maintain restoration/compensation route. Do not use recall notices to defame or punish without evidence.

## Platform complaint team

Give a reference number; preserve order record; provide human review; issue reasons; process safety refunds promptly; expose no unnecessary personal data; and provide independent dispute route. Do not use dark patterns, retaliate against complainants or suppress lawful reviews.

## Misconduct investigator

Operate outside the implicated chain of command; preserve evidence; protect complainant and accused from retaliation; disclose material inculpatory/exculpatory evidence; give hearing and reasons; and refer substantiated wrongdoing under applicable law.
