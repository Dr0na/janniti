# Independent Evidence Panel Protocol

## Mandate

Provide transparent, reproducible advice on additives, contaminants, nutrition, food-contact materials, claims, animal-feed safety and public-health thresholds. The panel advises; it does not prosecute, adjudicate, license or direct individual cases.

## Independence and method

Appointments use published criteria, open nominations, conflict screening, recorded reasons, staggered terms and selection no single political or commercial bloc controls. Members disclose financial, institutional and personal conflicts and recuse where required.

The panel shall register each question and decision context; publish its protocol, search strategy, evidence criteria, certainty method, raw-data availability and uncertainty treatment; consider replication, negative findings, vulnerable groups, traditional foods, environmental impact and alternatives; distinguish fact from policy option; and publish draft advice, peer-review responses and dissent.

No sponsor may select evidence, suppress results, ghost-write advice, condition funding on outcome or participate in a decision concerning its own product. Any market restriction requires lawful notice, independent decision, reasons and appeal.
