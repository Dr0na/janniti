# Plastic/Waste Fiscal and Competence Decision Template

Before a ban, charge or local enforcement scheme, record the statutory source, Union/State/local allocation, levy/tax character, liable person, measurement base, rate/rate cap, exemptions, support, collection authority, spending authority, audit, appeal, environmental evidence, alternative material safety, food-price impact and equality impact.

The decision must compare no charge, producer-responsibility, deposit-return, grant/credit and local-service alternatives. No rate, collection or spending rule is final until enacted or lawfully notified by the competent authority after fiscal/public review.
