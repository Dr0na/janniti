## Source-verification update — 16 August 2026

The official FSSAI regulation index was checked on this date. It identifies the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 2018; the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020; and later Labelling and Display amendments dated 8 August 2025 and 24 March 2026. FSSAI also warns that its compendia are ready-reference material and that the original Gazette notifications and amendments prevail in case of error or overlap. The exact official Gazette texts and commencement effect of the later amendments have not been retained and reviewed under the source-acceptance workflow. This keeps the exact-crosswalk blocker open.

The official Government of India Manual of Parliamentary Procedures was also retrieved. Paragraphs 9.23.1–9.23.3 state that, when a private Member gives notice to introduce a Bill, the Secretariat sends it to the concerned Ministry/Department, which consults the Ministry of Law and Justice on Parliament's competence. Paragraph 9.23.4 addresses circumstances in which a recommendation under Article 117 may be required. This is procedural context, not a determination that this draft may be introduced through a particular route.
