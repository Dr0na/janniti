# Cybersecurity and Incident-Response Plan

Protect evidence vaults, dashboards, laboratory interfaces, platform records and public portals through least privilege, multi-factor authentication, encryption, tested backups, access logs, vendor controls, vulnerability management and independent security testing.

For an incident: contain; preserve forensic evidence; assess safety/privacy impact; notify affected people and lawful authorities in accessible form; provide practical mitigation; publish a privacy-safe incident summary; correct root causes; and submit to independent review. Do not conceal an incident or condition support on waiver of rights.
