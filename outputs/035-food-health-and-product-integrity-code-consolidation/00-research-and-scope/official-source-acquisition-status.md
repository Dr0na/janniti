# Official Source Acquisition Status

## Purpose and status

This record identifies what has been retrieved from official publishers for the proposed Food, Feed and Delivery Integrity Bill and what must still be acquired before an exact amendment schedule can be written. It is a source-routing and verification record, not a finding that a source is legally accepted or that the Bill is ready for introduction.

As-of date: 16 August 2026. Jurisdiction: India, with State/UT and local implementation interfaces.

## Operational rule

Do not use a search result, regulation index, summary or compendium as a substitute for the official Gazette notification that controls. Do not mark any acquisition accepted merely because it is downloadable or hashable. Follow `governance/SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.md` for each material source change.

The official FSSAI archive also identifies a **draft** 2025 Packaging amendment concerning PFAS and BPA. It is not treated in this record as current law because a final, in-force Gazette instrument was not verified.
