# Ombudsman Appointment and Budget Model

## Appointment

An independent selection panel shall publish criteria, invite nominations, conduct conflict screening, publish a shortlist and reasons, and recommend appointments by a recorded supermajority. Membership must include judicial, public-health, consumer, worker, disability/accessibility and State/UT expertise; no serving political office-holder, regulated-entity executive or recent party office-holder may control selection.

The Ombudsman has a fixed non-renewable term, protected removal only for stated incapacity/misconduct through an independent fair process, public asset/conflict disclosure and post-service conflict restriction.

## Budget and powers

Funding is a separately published legislative appropriation with a reasoned public budget, audit and no case-specific executive direction. The Ombudsman may receive complaints, preserve evidence, order interim anti-retaliation protection where law permits, recommend correction/discipline/restitution and refer matters. It may not investigate/prosecute/adjudicate the same case finally or direct a court outcome.
