# Institutional Power Map — BNS Section 274 Minimum Sentence

| Controller | Power | Target | Independent check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parliament | Sets the penalty range by enacted law. | Persons charged under BNS section 274. | Constitutional review, public debate and election accountability. |
| Food-safety/police investigator | Collects evidence and refers a case under applicable law. | Suspected offender. | Lawful procedure, evidence disclosure, independent testing, judicial supervision where required. |
| Prosecutor | Decides whether proof supports a charge. | Accused person and public interest. | Ethical duty, recorded reasons, court scrutiny and defence challenge. |
| Court | Determines guilt and sentence within the enacted range. | Parties. | Open/reasoned process, appeal and constitutional remedies. |
| Public and press | Scrutinise aggregate enforcement and outcomes. | All institutions. | Access-to-information, protected reporting and privacy limits. |

No new authority, appointment, funding stream, regulator or delegated rulemaking power is created by this narrow amendment. The principal capture risk lies in selective investigation or unreliable testing, which cannot be solved by a higher sentence alone.
