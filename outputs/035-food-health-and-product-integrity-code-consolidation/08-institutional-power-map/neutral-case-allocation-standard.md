# Neutral Case Allocation Standard

Every inspection, complaint, laboratory referral, enforcement recommendation and misconduct inquiry must receive a unique identifier and be allocated through a published risk model or verifiable random method.

The method must log inputs, risk factors, override request, override approver, reason, time, conflict screen and outcome. Political belief, criticism, party association, caste, religion, gender, disability, income, business size or market rivalry must never be an input. Overrides require written reasons, independent review within seven days and quarterly public aggregate reporting. An external auditor tests comparator outcomes and publishes disparities, uncertainty and corrective action.
