# Evidence Vault and Retention Policy

Store samples, photographs, chain-of-custody events, laboratory raw data, orders, complaints and decision records in tamper-evident systems with role-based access and immutable access logs.

Retention must be lawful, necessary and published by category. Preserve material evidence through limitation, review and appeal periods; securely delete data no longer needed; issue legal holds for credible disputes; and disclose material inculpatory and exculpatory evidence through lawful process. No authority, laboratory, platform or contractor may alter, conceal, destroy or substitute material evidence.
