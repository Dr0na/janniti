# Interoperability and Minimum-Data Standard

FSSAI, States, local bodies, laboratories, platforms and consumer forums may exchange only verified, necessary, purpose-limited data using documented schemas, access controls, audit logs, retention rules and correction mechanisms.

Every exchange must identify sender, recipient, lawful purpose, fields, legal basis, time, retention, onward-transfer restriction and review route. No shared system may be used for political profiling, unrelated policing, market manipulation, labour surveillance or discriminatory scoring. A person must have lawful access, correction and grievance routes consistent with applicable data law.
