# Platform Disclosure Conformance Specification

Before an order is confirmed, a platform must expose a single machine-readable and human-readable record containing responsible business, address/identifier, item/portion, direct price when available, platform price, every mandatory charge, tax, discount/funder, platform margin/commission, sponsored/affiliated status, recall notice, cancellation/refund route and timestamp.

Acceptance tests must confirm that the same data are visible on app, web, low-bandwidth and assisted channels; totals reconcile; historical records are tamper-evident; sponsored ranking is labelled; refunds meet the configured clock; and an independent consumer test can retrieve reasons and appeal. No test may use real consumer data without lawful authority and privacy controls.
