# Laboratory and Evidence-Vault Pilot Acceptance Plan

## Preconditions

Use only accredited laboratories or a documented independent equivalent; conflict-screen analysts; run blind proficiency samples; preserve raw data; and test chain-of-custody, split sample, referral and disclosure routes without real enforcement consequence.

## Acceptance tests

1. Every sample transfer is logged, sealed and reconstructable.
2. A simulated adverse result includes method, uncertainty, quality controls and conflict record.
3. A simulated respondent can obtain lawful disclosure and referral/defence testing.
4. An attempted record alteration creates an immutable alert and independent incident record.
5. Retention/deletion and legal hold operate as configured.

No production use until an independent assessor publishes the method, failures, corrective action and retest result.
