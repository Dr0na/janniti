# Annual Democratic Integrity Review

An independent annual review shall test whether enforcement has become selective, opaque, discriminatory, politically directed, commercially captured or inaccessible.

It shall analyse appointments, funding, instructions, inspections, samples, prosecutions, closures, lab results, appeals, reversals, complaints, retaliation, platform conduct, media freedom, data sharing and disparities. The review must publish methods, data limits, dissent, findings, corrective recommendations and a time-bound response from each responsible authority. It cannot replace individual court remedies or obscure current wrongdoing.
