## Completed consolidation action

The duplicate BNS/BNSS Schedules B and C were removed from the broad working Bill on 17 August 2026. The companion BNS/BNSS Bill is now the only location for those operative amendments. No other document was deleted because its source role, legal route, State/UT function, evidence record or implementation function is distinct.

## Future consolidation rule

When an exact, source-accepted redline is created, it replaces only its proposed draft vehicle; the prior draft remains a provenance record with an explicit superseded label and link. Do not merge Union, State/UT, local and delegated-law text merely for convenience, because doing so would conceal competence and procedure requirements.
