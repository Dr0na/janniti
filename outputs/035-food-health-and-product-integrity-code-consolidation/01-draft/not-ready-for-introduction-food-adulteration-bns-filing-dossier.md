## Proposed Statement of Objects and Reasons

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 makes intentional adulteration of food or drink intended for sale, so as to make it noxious, an offence. The Bill proposes to strengthen the custodial range for that existing offence while retaining its elements and matching the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 First Schedule entry to the proposed maximum sentence. It is prospective and retains bailability, non-cognizability, proof requirements, defences and judicial remedies under applicable law.
