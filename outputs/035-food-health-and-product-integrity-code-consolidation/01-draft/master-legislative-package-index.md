## Assembly rule

The Union criminal Bill may proceed independently. Food-safety regulations, consumer/e-commerce rules, Legal Metrology rules, data safeguards, environment/waste provisions and State/UT laws must be made or amended through their respective enabling authorities and procedures. A provision must not be duplicated in another instrument merely to make the package appear stronger.
