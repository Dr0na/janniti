# Constitutional Stress Test — BNS Section 274 Minimum Sentence

**Target:** Draft amendment substituting a two-year minimum and five-year maximum term in BNS section 274. **Conclusion:** resilient with conditions; the amendment must remain narrow and prospective.

| Scenario | Exploitation path | Safeguard retained in draft | Remaining issue | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Captured food regulator or police | Uses an allegation of adulteration against a small vendor or political critic. | Existing BNS elements still require proof that the article was made noxious and that the accused intended or knew it was likely to be sold; trial and appeal remain. | BNSS classification, sampling, independent laboratory access and disclosure must be verified before enactment. | High |
| Lab error or trace contamination | A disputed test becomes the basis for a minimum custodial sentence. | The amendment does not alter the prosecution’s burden or create strict liability. | The wider Bill needs binding independent sampling, split-sample and defence-testing rules. | High |
| Deliberate mass-harm adulteration | A six-month ceiling fails to reflect culpability and deterrence. | Two-year minimum, five-year maximum and mandatory fine increase the baseline penalty while other proved offences remain available. | Sentencing evidence and victim-impact procedures need separate statutory/BNSS confirmation. | Medium |
| Retrospective use | Government tries to apply the increased sentence to earlier conduct. | Schedule B expressly makes the amendment prospective. | Commencement notification must be published clearly. | Medium |

## Minimum safeguards

1. Preserve section 274’s existing noxiousness and mens rea elements; do not introduce a reverse burden.
2. Apply the increased punishment only to post-commencement conduct, consistent with Article 20(1).
3. Verify and, if needed, amend the applicable procedure to require lawful sampling, accredited independent testing, material disclosure, defence testing and judicial remedies.
4. Publish disaggregated aggregate enforcement data and provide an independent complaint route for fabricated, selective or retaliatory cases.

## Residual risk

A mandatory minimum limits judicial sentence individualisation. No drafting can eliminate the risk of bad-faith investigation; independent evidence, open reasons, counsel, courts and public accountability remain essential.
