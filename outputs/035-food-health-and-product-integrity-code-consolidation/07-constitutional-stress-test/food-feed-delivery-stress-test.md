# Constitutional Stress Test — Consolidated Working Bill

## Conclusion

**Vulnerable pending repair.** The policy draft contains many anti-capture and fair-process safeguards, but its breadth, unsplit legislative competence, undefined terms and severe penalties create Critical/High risks until it is converted into a source-backed Union/State/local package.

| Scenario | Exploitation path | Existing defence | Unresolved failure | Rating | Minimum repair |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Captured food regulator | Uses closure, testing or public warnings against critics | Reasons, audits, appeals, anti-vendetta clauses | Appointments/funding/removal and external-review route not formally specified | Critical | Plural appointment, fixed budget, random allocation, independent review and court remedy. |
| Corrupt inspector/police | Extorts vendors or plants evidence | Section 43 safeguards | Exact independent investigation/prosecution route unknown | High | Link to verified misconduct/criminal procedure and chain-external body. |
| Dominant delivery platform | Evades commission/price rules through affiliates/data | Disclosure, audit, anti-evasion clauses | Competition/data enforcement route and economic effects unverified | High | Competition/data crosswalk, interoperable audit standards, judicial review. |
| Hostile majority | Uses plastic/live-stream/feed rules to burden informal, minority or traditional businesses | Equality, transition and support clauses | Objective criteria and State/local implementation not settled | High | Equality impact test, accessible assistance, comparator audit, prompt appeal. |
| Complete food/feed-chain plastic prohibition | Enforces before safe non-plastic substitutes, cleaning systems and supply infrastructure exist; or disguises a commercial exemption as an emergency | Final-prohibition, anti-evasion, independent-testing and transition clauses | Scope may affect hygiene equipment, machinery and small operators; competence and safe-substitute criteria not verified | Critical | Exact definition and commencement route; independent material/hygiene testing; no permanent exemption; funded transition; expedited neutral review. |
| Genuine outbreak/emergency | Serial urgent orders bypass ordinary protection | Expiry/review concepts | Maximum time, renewal authority and scope differ across clauses | High | Single emergency chapter with fixed expiry/renewal/court standards. |
| Ordinary person | Cannot use complex complaint/refund/remedy system | Multilingual/offline routes | Funding and legal-aid capacity unspecified | Medium | Funded service standards and independent access audit. |

## Single-point dependencies

1. FSSAI/standard-setting authority — split technical standards, licensing, investigation, prosecution and audit.
2. State/local inspection capacity — use model adoption, published minimum standards and independent comparator audits.
3. Laboratories/data systems — random allocation, accreditation, retention and privacy rules require source-backed governance.
4. Courts/tribunals — define forum, standing, timing, interim remedy and appellate route.

## Residual risk

No Bill can eliminate corruption, political pressure, false evidence or resource failure. Distributed power, public records, protected reporting, independent review, courts, legislative oversight and periodic revision remain necessary.
