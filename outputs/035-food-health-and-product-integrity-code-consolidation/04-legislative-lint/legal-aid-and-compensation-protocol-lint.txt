L008 [High] L5: Eligibility must be transparent, non-discriminatory and reviewable. Support may include advice, translation, disability access, emergency evidence preservation, representation, interim protection and referral to statutory compensation. Compensation must be determined by a competent independent authority on evidence and reasons; it must not require waiver of future lawful remedies.
  Review: Exceptional power may lack expiry; add duration and renewal safeguards.
