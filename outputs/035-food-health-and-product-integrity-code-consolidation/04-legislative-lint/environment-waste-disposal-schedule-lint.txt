L007 [High] L15: 3. State/UT and local instruments should implement collection, segregation, return, safe treatment, reuse infrastructure and public education under their lawful waste and local-government powers. They may not create a new tax, criminal offence, secret exemption or unreviewable closure power unless separately authorised by statute.

L001 [High] L19: An operator that controls an expired, recalled, condemned or unsafe food article shall preserve the batch, source, quantity, decision, destination, destruction method, date, responsible person and independent confirmation in a traceable record. The operator bears the reasonable cost of lawful disposal; public recovery of cost requires notice, itemised calculation, reasons, review and court remedy. A regulator may not require destruction before the sample/record necessary for an independent challenge is preserved, except where a documented immediate safety risk requires a proportionate urgent measure.
  Review: Subjective threshold; define objective facts and evidence.
