**Mechanical result:** ten high-severity prompts, principally triggered by the presence of a penal provision. See `bns-section-274-amendment-lint.txt`.

| Rule ID | Severity | Location | Issue and exploitation path | Exact treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L010 | High | Proposed clause 2 | A mandatory minimum can amplify selective enforcement or a wrongful laboratory result. | Retained the existing noxiousness and intent/knowledge elements; added prospective application; flagged independent sampling, disclosure and defence-testing verification as a pre-enactment condition. |

**Lint status:** clear with conditions. The text is internally exact and includes the required BNSS classification amendment, but it cannot be treated as a complete food-safety enforcement code. Existing FSS Act sampling and laboratory safeguards are preserved; parliamentary proportionality and filing review remain human-only gates.
