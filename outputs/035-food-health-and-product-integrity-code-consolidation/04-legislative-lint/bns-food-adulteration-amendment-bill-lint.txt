L010 [High] L37: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 makes it an offence to adulterate food or drink intended for sale so as to make it noxious. The present maximum sentence is six months’ imprisonment, a fine up to five thousand rupees, or both. The Bill increases the punishment for that intentional offence to imprisonment of not less than two years and up to five years, with fine.
  Review: Penalty provision; verify clear elements, mental state, defences, and proportionality.
