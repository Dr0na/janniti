# Consequential Amendment and Technical Schedule Matrix — Not Ready for Introduction

## Status

This is a drafting-control matrix, not an enacted schedule and not a verified amendment. Schedule II and Schedule III now supply the proposed Central statutory text and technical framework. It remains **not ready for introduction** because subordinate-law consolidation, State/UT law mapping, parliamentary-form review, fiscal costing and specialist sign-off remain incomplete. No placeholder below authorises a change to existing law.

## Required technical schedules

Before commencement of an operational duty, each schedule must state the authority, baseline, method, target, budget, workforce, access protection, review date, audit and remedy.
