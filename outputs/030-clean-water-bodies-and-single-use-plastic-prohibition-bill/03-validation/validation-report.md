# Validation Report — Clean Water Bodies and Single-Use Plastic Prohibition Bill

## Conclusion

**Insufficient material to validate for introduction.** The working text has strong anti-evasion, evidence, transparency, remedy and institutional-separation features, but the missing competence opinion, exact schedule, State/UT mapping, current-law cross-references, technical standards, fiscal plan and specialist review are material blockers.

## Rights and implementation assessment

The draft protects complaints, access, evidence disclosure, courts, accessible participation and content-neutral religious-waste stewardship. Residual risks include disparate locality-level enforcement, unsafe substitutes, informal-worker displacement, unaffordable alternatives, data error, capture and judicial delay. These require independent review and practical capacity, not merely more severe penalties.
