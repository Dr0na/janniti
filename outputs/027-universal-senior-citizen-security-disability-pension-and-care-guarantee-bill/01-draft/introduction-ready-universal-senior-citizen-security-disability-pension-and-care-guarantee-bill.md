# THE UNIVERSAL SENIOR CITIZEN SECURITY, DISABILITY PENSION, AND CARE GUARANTEE BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee social security and dignity for senior citizens and persons with disabilities under Article 21 and Article 41; to mandate a universal non-contributory pension indexed to inflation; to establish free government assisted living homes and day-care centers; to provide doorstep delivery of essential medicines and assistive devices; to establish the National Senior Citizen and Disability Security Authority; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Universal Senior Citizen Security, Disability Pension, and Care Guarantee Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Person with Disability" means a person with bench-mark disability as defined in the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016;

(b) "Senior Citizen" means any resident of India who has attained the age of sixty years; and

(c) "Universal Non-Contributory Pension" means the statutory monthly financial assistance guaranteed under section 3.

### 3. Statutory Guarantee of Universal Non-Contributory Pension

(1) Every Senior Citizen and Person with Disability who is not covered by a formal tax-funded retirement pension scheme shall have a statutory right to receive a Universal Non-Contributory Pension of not less than three thousand rupees per month.

(2) The pension amount under sub-section (1) shall be indexed annually to the Consumer Price Index to compensate for inflation.

### 4. Government Assisted Living Homes and Doorstep Care Delivery

(1) Every State Government shall establish and maintain at least one free, fully equipped government assisted living home in every district for destitute senior citizens and persons with disabilities.

(2) Local public health authorities shall ensure doorstep delivery of essential chronic disease medicines and assistive mobility devices to all registered senior citizens and disabled persons.

### 5. National Senior Citizen and Disability Security Authority

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Senior Citizen and Disability Security Authority.

(2) The Authority shall oversee pension disbursements, monitor assisted living home standards, and resolve elder abuse and disability rights grievances.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Lack of income security, social isolation, and inadequate care for senior citizens and persons with disabilities violate the constitutional promise of dignity under Article 21 and Article 41.

2. The Universal Senior Citizen Security, Disability Pension, and Care Guarantee Bill, 2026 mandates a universal monthly pension (min ₹3,000 indexed to inflation), free district assisted living homes, doorstep medicine delivery, and creates the National Authority.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3 and Clause 4 mandate pension disbursements and assisted living homes.

2. Financial assistance from the Consolidated Fund of India is estimated at rupees fifteen thousand crores annually.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 6 empowers administrative rule-making.
