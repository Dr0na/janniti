# Constitutional Stress Test Report: National Institutional Integrity, Public Asset Audit, and Review Commission Bill, 2026

## 1. Adversarial Scenario Testing

### Scenario A: Attempted Mass Retrospective Penalization / Executive Cancellation
- **Vulnerability**: Executive or political pressure to issue blanket retrospective cancellations or mass civil service terminations without individualized evidence.
- **Stress Test Result**: PASSED. Clause 10 explicitly prohibits retrospective criminal penalties under Article 20(1). Clauses 7, 8, and 9 require individualized audit records, UPSC concurrence for civil service reviews under Article 311, and High Court judicial determination for contract quashing under Article 300A.

### Scenario B: Executive Capture of Selection Process
- **Vulnerability**: Attempt by ruling party to pack the Commission with political appointees.
- **Stress Test Result**: PASSED. Clause 4(4) mandates appointment by a tripartite Selection Committee comprising the Prime Minister, Leader of the Opposition, and Chief Justice of India.

### Scenario C: Ouster of Judicial Review
- **Vulnerability**: Attempt to insulate Commission orders from High Court or Supreme Court scrutiny.
- **Stress Test Result**: PASSED. Clause 8 establishes the High Court Division Bench as the primary adjudicator for quashing/recovery suits. Clause 11 provides a direct statutory appeal to the Supreme Court of India. Writ jurisdiction under Articles 226 and 32 remains completely un-severed.

## 2. Summary of Quality Gate Verification
- **Article 20(1) Compliance**: Fully verified. No ex post facto offences or penalties.
- **Article 14 & 21 Compliance**: Fully verified. Full natural justice, notice, hearing, and written reasons.
- **Article 300A Compliance**: Fully verified. Property/lease deprivation requires High Court judicial order.
- **Article 311 Compliance**: Fully verified. Civil service reviews proceed with UPSC concurrence and statutory inquiry.
- **Basic Structure Compliance**: Fully verified. Judicial review preserved at all levels.
