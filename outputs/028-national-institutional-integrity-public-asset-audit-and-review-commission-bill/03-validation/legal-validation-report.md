# Legal Validation and Loophole Audit Report: National Institutional Integrity, Public Asset Audit, and Review Commission Bill, 2026

## 4. Loophole and Solution Register

| ID | Severity | Provision / Issue | Exploitation Path | Harm | Legal Basis | Exact Fix / Redline | Owner & Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LPH-001** | **Critical** | Risk of arbitrary executive cancellation without judicial order | Executive issuing summary eviction or cancellation notices. | Violation of Art. 300A & Art. 14; immediate High Court stay. | *BDA v. Ramachandra* | **Fix**: Clause 8 mandates that quashing and recovery can only be ordered by a Division Bench of the High Court. | High Court Registry |
| **LPH-002** | **Critical** | Retrospective criminal penalties risk | Opponents prosecuted under retrospective penal clauses. | Violation of Art. 20(1); void *ab initio*. | *Kedar Nath v. State of WB* | **Fix**: Clause 10 explicitly prohibits ex post facto criminal laws; limits referrals to laws in force at the time of the act. | Special Prosecutor |
| **LPH-003** | **High** | Frivolous or politically motivated land audit petitions | Bad-faith petitions targeting political opponents without evidence. | Selective harassment & judicial clutter. | Art. 14 Non-Arbitrariness | **Fix**: Clause 8(1) requires a formal Audit Determination Record with documented evidence of fraud or gross undervaluation before filing. | Commission Chairperson |
| **LPH-004** | **High** | Civil service appointee summary removal loophole | Removing civil servants without natural justice. | Quashed by CAT/High Court under Art. 311. | *Tulsiram Patel Case* | **Fix**: Clause 9 requires recruitment review in concurrence with UPSC and adherence to statutory inquiry rules. | UPSC Secretary |
| **LPH-005** | **Medium** | Asset stripping during pending audit | Private entities selling or transferring land during Commission audit. | Loss of public recovery target. | CPC Order 39 | **Fix**: Clause 8 allows Commission to apply for High Court provisional preservation orders (max 90 days). | High Court Bench |

## 5. Stress-Test Matrix

- **Hostile Political Majority Scenario**: Cannot dissolve or pack the Commission because appointments require Leader of Opposition and CJI concurrence (Clause 4), and removal requires Supreme Court inquiry (Clause 5).
- **Corrupt Private Actor Scenario**: Cannot evade audit by transferring land to third parties, as High Courts can issue provisional preservation orders and track beneficial ownership (Clause 8).
- **Litigation Delay Scenario**: 180-day statutory mandate for High Court petition disposal ensures rapid resolution (Clause 8(3)).

## 6. Residual Risks and Limits

- High Court docket backlog may require designating specialized Benches in major High Courts (Delhi, Bombay, Gujarat, Calcutta).
- Expert financial valuation is required for historical asset pricing as of 2014.
