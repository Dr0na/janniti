## 2. Executive finding and reliance limits

Existing statutory mechanisms—such as the Commissions of Inquiry Act, 1952, the Comptroller and Auditor General (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971, and standard departmental disciplinary frameworks under Article 311—provide fragmented mechanisms for investigating governance failures. However, they lack a unified, time-bound, judicially supervised statutory commission with specialized powers to audit public asset transactions, review lateral recruitment regularity, and initiate civil asset recovery.

Crucially, a law proposing blanket retrospective cancellation of all executive actions since 2014 or retroactive capital punishment/mass termination is unconstitutional under:
1. **Article 20(1)**: Absolute prohibition on ex post facto criminal legislation and enhanced retrospective penal sanctions.
2. **Article 14 & Article 21**: Non-arbitrariness, procedural fairness, and due process requirements.
3. **Article 311**: Mandates specific notice, inquiry, and hearing before dismissal or removal of civil servants.
4. **Article 300A**: Deprivation of property or contractual rights must be by authority of a valid law providing fair procedures, non-arbitrary criteria, and judicial review.

Therefore, the appropriate legislative vehicle is a new Central Act: **The National Institutional Integrity, Public Asset Audit, and Review Commission Bill, 2026**, establishing an independent statutory Commission headed by a former Chief Justice or Judge of the Supreme Court to conduct individualized, evidence-based reviews of contested disinvestments, major public land leases, high-value procurement contracts, and irregular lateral appointments, with full judicial review and due process.

## 9. Rights, equality, federalism, and public-interest assessment

- **Article 20(1) Immunity**: Retrospective penalization or retroactive capital punishment is strictly barred by Article 20(1). Any legislative attempt to impose retrospective criminal guilt is void *ab initio*.
- **Article 14 & Article 21 Fairness**: Summary mass termination of civil servants or arbitrary property cancellation without individualized notice, evidence, and opportunity of hearing violates non-arbitrariness and due process.
- **Rule of Law & Anti-Vendetta Safeguard**: Legal accountability must target specific illegalities (fraud, corruption, procedural violations, undervaluation) under objective statutory criteria, supervised by an independent judicial Commission and subject to High Court/Supreme Court judicial review under Articles 226/32.
