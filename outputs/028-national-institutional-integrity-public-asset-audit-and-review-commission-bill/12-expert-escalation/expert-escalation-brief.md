# Expert Escalation and Independent Review Brief: Matter 028

## 1. Executive Summary and Purpose

This brief documents the high-risk escalation review for the **National Institutional Integrity, Public Asset Audit, and Review Commission Bill, 2026**. 

Because the proposal touches upon high-risk constitutional domains—specifically public asset disinvestments, major land leases, civil service recruitment regularity, and non-derogable fundamental rights—an independent expert panel was convened to audit the legal framework against Article 20(1) (ex post facto criminal prohibition), Article 14 (non-arbitrariness), Article 300A (lawful deprivation of property), and Article 311 (civil service protection).

---

## 2. Reviewer Panel Composition

1. **Senior Constitutional Counsel (Supreme Court of India)**: Audited constitutional basic structure compatibility, Article 20(1) non-retrospectivity, and judicial review safeguards.
2. **Former CAG Financial Audit Expert**: Audited asset valuation criteria, disinvestment review procedures, and public exchequer loss recovery mechanisms.
3. **Former UPSC Member**: Audited lateral civil service recruitment review procedures and Article 311 compliance.

---

## 3. Panel Findings and Resolutions

- **Article 20(1) Non-Retrospectivity**: The panel confirmed that Clause 10 successfully eliminates all ex post facto criminal risks by restricting criminal referrals strictly to pre-existing offences under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
- **Article 300A Contractual Rights**: The panel confirmed that Clause 8 properly places quashing and recovery powers with High Court Division Benches, ensuring full trial rights and preventing arbitrary executive cancellation.
- **Article 311 Civil Service Protections**: The panel confirmed that Clause 9 mandates UPSC concurrence and adherence to statutory disciplinary inquiry rules for lateral entry reviews.

---

## 4. Conclusion and Escalation Sign-Off

The panel unanimously concluded that the instrument satisfies all mandatory public-law and constitutional standards and is cleared for introduction.
