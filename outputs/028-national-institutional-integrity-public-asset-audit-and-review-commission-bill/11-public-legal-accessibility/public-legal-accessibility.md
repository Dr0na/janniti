# Public Legal Accessibility Pack: National Institutional Integrity, Public Asset Audit, and Review Commission Bill, 2026

## 1. Plain-Language Executive Summary

This Bill establishes an independent, high-powered Commission to audit public asset sales (disinvestments), major public land leases, and senior civil service lateral entries executed since May 2014. 

The goal is to ensure that public property was not fraudulently sold at throwaway prices or illegally leased, and that civil service recruitments followed constitutional rules. To protect civic rights, the Bill **rejects arbitrary government cancellations or retroactive punishments**. Instead, all asset quashing and fund recovery must be decided by High Courts based on concrete evidence, ensuring complete fairness, due process, and constitutional rights for every citizen.

---

## 2. What the Bill Does and Does Not Do

### What the Bill DOES Do:
- Establishes a 5-member independent Judicial Commission led by a former Chief Justice of India or Supreme Court Judge.
- Audits major land leases, Central PSU sales, and high-value procurement contracts executed since May 2014.
- Empowers the Commission to file lawsuits in High Courts to cancel leases or recover money if fraud, bribery, or illegal undervaluation is proven.
- Reviews senior lateral entry appointments into civil services alongside the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC).
- Publishes all audit records, valuation reports, and court petitions on a free, public online portal within 14 days.

### What the Bill DOES NOT Do:
- It does **NOT** allow arbitrary executive cancellation of contracts without court approval.
- It does **NOT** create retrospective criminal offences or retroactive capital punishment (protecting Article 20(1) rights).
- It does **NOT** dismiss civil servants without an inquiry and a fair opportunity to defend themselves (protecting Article 311 rights).
- It does **NOT** give any single political party power to appoint or control the Commission.

## 4. Power and Check Summary

| Power | Who Holds It | Who Checks It | Public Transparency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commission Appointments | Selection Committee (PM, Opposition Leader, CJI) | Parliament & Public Scrutiny | Selection criteria published |
| Asset & Contract Audits | Commission Valuation Panels | Code of Civil Procedure 1908 | Audits published within 14 days |
| Contract Quashing & Recovery | High Court Division Bench | Supreme Court of India | Open Court Decisions |
| Civil Service Reviews | Commission & UPSC | Central Administrative Tribunal | Statutory Hearing Record |

## 5. Multilingual and Multi-Format Accessibility Plan

1. **Languages**: Available in English, Hindi, and all 22 Scheduled Languages.
2. **Digital Accessibility**: W3C WCAG 2.1 AA compliant online portal, screen-reader compatible PDFs, and plain-text web versions.
3. **Low-Data / Offline Formats**: Printed summary leaflets distributed at District Collectorates and offline audio explainer summaries.
4. **Civic Education**: Standardized 5-minute video explainer detailing how public asset audits protect taxpayer funds while preserving constitutional due process.
