# THE GIG WORKER PROTECTION, MINIMUM LIVING WAGE, AND SOCIAL SECURITY BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to classify gig and platform workers as protected employees; to guarantee a minimum living wage, occupational safety, and health insurance; to establish a National Gig Worker Social Security Fund financed by a statutory platform transaction fee; to enforce algorithmic transparency and human-in-the-loop appeal rights against automated terminations; to mandate 100% mechanized alternative employment for sanitation workers; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Gig Worker Protection, Minimum Living Wage, and Social Security Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Algorithmic Deactivation" means the automated suspension, block, or termination of a gig worker's account by a digital platform algorithm;

(b) "Gig Worker" means a person who performs work or participates in a work arrangement and earns from such activities outside of a traditional employer-employee relationship through a digital platform;

(c) "Minimum Living Wage" means the hourly or task-based remuneration rate determined under section 3 to ensure a dignified standard of living;

(d) "Platform Entity" means any digital aggregator, marketplace, or enterprise coordinating gig work through digital applications; and

(e) "Social Security Fund" means the National Gig Worker Social Security Fund established under section 4.

### 3. Statutory Right to Minimum Living Wage and Fair Working Hours

(1) Every Gig Worker shall be entitled to receive a Minimum Living Wage for all active working hours, including waiting time mandated by a Platform Entity.

(2) No Platform Entity shall require or incentivize a Gig Worker to work in excess of twelve hours in any twenty-four-hour period.

### 4. National Gig Worker Social Security Fund

(1) There shall be established a non-lapsable fund to be called the National Gig Worker Social Security Fund.

(2) Every Platform Entity shall contribute two per cent of the gross transaction value of every customer booking or order to the Social Security Fund.

(3) The Fund shall provide health insurance, accident cover, disability benefits, maternity support, and pension entitlements to registered Gig Workers.

### 5. Algorithmic Transparency and Appeal Rights

(1) Every Platform Entity shall disclose to Gig Workers the criteria and parameters used by its algorithms for task allocation, rating evaluation, and earnings calculation.

(2) No Algorithmic Deactivation shall take effect without a prior human-in-the-loop review and a minimum seven-day written notice giving reasons to the Gig Worker.

(3) A Gig Worker aggrieved by an account deactivation shall have the statutory right of appeal to the Gig Worker Welfare Board established under section 6.

### 6. Mechanized Sanitation Work Guarantee

(1) Manual cleaning of sewers, septic tanks, or hazardous waste without mechanized equipment is strictly prohibited.

(2) Local authorities shall provide 100% mechanized sanitation equipment and alternative dignified employment for former manual sanitation workers.

### 7. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Millions of gig, platform, and informal workers in India work under precarious conditions without minimum wage guarantees, health insurance, or algorithmic fairness.

2. The Gig Worker Protection, Minimum Living Wage, and Social Security Bill, 2026 classifies gig workers as protected employees, establishes a statutory living wage, mandates a 2% platform transaction levy for social security, enforces algorithmic transparency, and guarantees mechanized sanitation.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 4 establishes the National Gig Worker Social Security Fund financed primarily by a 2% platform levy.

2. Central seed funding and administrative operational expenditure is estimated at rupees one thousand crores.

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION
