# THE POLICE WORKING CONDITIONS, WELFARE, AND SHIFT REFORM BILL, 2026

### A

### BILL

to guarantee an eight-hour shift system for all police personnel; to mandate one weekly rest day; to institute annual mental health screening and stress-management infrastructure; to guarantee statutory housing and comprehensive healthcare entitlements for police officers and their families; to establish the National Police Welfare Board; and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

### 1. Short title, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be called the Police Working Conditions, Welfare, and Shift Reform Act, 2026.

(2) It extends to the whole of India.

(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

(a) "Police Personnel" means all serving constables, head constables, sub-inspectors, inspectors, and officers of the police force;

(b) "Police Welfare Board" means the National Police Welfare Board established under section 5; and

(c) "Shift System" means the mandatory three-shift operational schedule established under section 3.

### 3. Mandatory Eight-Hour Shift System and Overtime Limits

(1) Every police station and operational unit shall operate on a mandatory three-shift schedule capping normal duty for Police Personnel at eight hours per day.

(2) Extension of duty beyond eight hours shall be permitted only during declared public emergencies or major law-and-order crises, subject to mandatory double overtime pay and compensatory time off.

### 4. Mandatory Weekly Rest Day and Mental Health Care

(1) Every member of the Police Personnel shall be entitled to one mandatory fully paid rest day per week.

(2) The State Government shall provide free, confidential annual mental health screening, psychological counseling, and stress-reduction programs for all Police Personnel.

### 5. National Police Welfare Board and Housing Entitlements

(1) There shall be established an autonomous body to be called the National Police Welfare Board.

(2) The Board shall administer police housing construction, comprehensive medical insurance schemes, and educational scholarships for children of Police Personnel.

(3) The State Government shall ensure that within five years of commencement of this Act, one hundred per cent of junior Police Personnel are provided with statutory rent-free government accommodation or adequate housing allowance.

### 6. Power to Make Rules

(1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every rule made under this section shall be laid before Parliament.

---

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

1. Extreme overwork (14–16 hour shifts), lack of weekly rest days, and inadequate housing cause severe physical and psychological burnout, depression, and high suicide rates among junior police personnel (*CERC* judgment).

2. The Police Working Conditions, Welfare, and Shift Reform Bill, 2026 mandates an 8-hour shift system, 1 weekly rest day, annual mental health care, statutory housing, and establishes the National Police Welfare Board.

---

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

1. Clause 3, Clause 4, and Clause 5 mandate additional recruitment for 3-shift rotation, mental healthcare, and housing.

2. Financial assistance from the Consolidated Fund of India is estimated at rupees five thousand crores over five years.

---

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

1. Clause 6 empowers administrative rule-making.
