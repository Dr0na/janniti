# Introduction-Ready Bill Template — Parliament of India

Use this template only after research confirms that the proposed measure is within Parliament's competence and is the appropriate legislative route. It is a drafting form, not proof of admissibility, financial recommendation, Secretariat clearance, or constitutional validity. Verify the current format and ancillary-document requirements with the official parliamentary/legislative authority for the proposed Bill before labelling an output introduction-ready.

The final `introduction-ready-*.md` must contain only completed text. Resolve or remove every instructional heading and bracketed item in this template; none may remain in the final instrument.

## Legal-list formatting

Keep every clause, subclause, paragraph, and list item on its own rendered line. Do not use Markdown `-` or `*` bullets in a clean Bill. For an enumerated series, put each legal designator—such as `(a)`, `(b)`, `(i)`, or `(ii)`—in a separate paragraph, with a blank line before the next item. Indent nested items by two spaces in source text where needed, but never by four spaces (which turns legislative text into a code block). Review the rendered Markdown before delivery: no two legal items may be merged into one displayed line.

### [Substantive clauses]

Use consecutively numbered clauses and subsections. State each duty, power, threshold, procedure, record, publication duty, independent check, challenge, remedy, limitation, and review mechanism in operative text.

Where a clause contains a list, use this completed-text shape:

```text
(1) The authority shall—

  (a) [first duty];

  (b) [second duty]; and

  (c) [final duty].
```

### [Rule-making provision, only if justified]

Limit the delegate, purpose, subject matter, procedural safeguards, publication, laying, review, and anti-subdelegation rules precisely. Do not delegate essential legislative policy.

### [Amendment, repeal, savings and transition]

Give exact Act, section, schedule, and replacement/omission language. State treatment of pending matters, existing appointments, records, proceedings, rights, liabilities, and subordinate legislation.

### [Review / sunset]

State the independent review, publication, legislative consideration, expiry, renewal, and remedy mechanisms.

### SCHEDULE[S]

Include each completed schedule with its statutory heading and cross-reference, if applicable.

## STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

Give a concise, accurate account of the existing-law problem, legislative objective, main means, and why the measure is needed. It must not claim that a proposal is current law or conceal material limits.

## FINANCIAL MEMORANDUM

State the estimated recurring and non-recurring expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of India and other public financial effects, with the basis and period of estimate. Include only when applicable under the current official requirements; otherwise omit it from the completed instrument and record the source-backed inapplicability determination in the analytical record.

## MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION

For each delegated power, identify the clause, delegate, subject matter, limits, laying/publication requirements, and why the delegation is necessary. Include only where delegated legislative power is conferred.

## [OTHER REQUIRED INTRODUCTION OR CIRCULATION MATERIAL]

Include a completed statement or certificate only when current official requirements make it necessary and the competent authorised person can properly make it. Never fabricate a signature, recommendation, certification, authentication, or procedural approval.
