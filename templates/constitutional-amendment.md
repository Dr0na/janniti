## Bill text

### Clause 1 — Short title and commencement

### Clause 2 — Amendment/insertion

Use exact replacement or insertion language. Define any new office, standard, time limit, publication duty, review route, appointment/removal process, funding rule, and remedy.

### Clause 2A — Anti-circumvention and review

Provide that no public authority may achieve indirectly, through delegation, outsourcing, emergency powers, secret instruction, funding, technology, or successive temporary measures, an outcome prohibited directly. Preserve standing, an effective remedy, and independent constitutional review.

### Clause 3 — Transitional and consequential provisions

Specify continuity, deadlines, protection of pending matters, rulemaking limit, review date, and severability.

## Clause-by-clause notes

For each clause: purpose; power holder; checker; public record; challenge; remedy; expiry/review; anti-evasion route; and abuse scenario prevented.
