# Legislative Lint Rules

Use this catalogue alongside legal judgment, not as a mechanical substitute for it. Every finding must identify the actual legal context, affected rights, likely abuse route, and smallest effective remedy.

| Rule | Flag | Core risk | Typical correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| L001 | Undefined or subjective threshold | Arbitrary exercise of power | Define terms, objective facts, evidence standard, and recorded reasons. |
| L002 | Open-ended discretion | Selective/unequal application | Specify purpose, factors, limits, published guidance, and review. |
| L003 | Immunity or “good faith” shield | Impunity for unlawful conduct | Limit to lawful good-faith acts; preserve remedies for bad faith, negligence, malice, and constitutional breach. |
| L004 | Ouster/finality/no-court clause | Unreviewable power | Preserve constitutional and judicial remedies, standing, and timely review. |
| L005 | Broad exception/exemption | Rule swallowed by exception | Narrow eligibility, necessity test, reasons, publication, duration, and appeal. |
| L006 | Unbounded delegated rulemaking | Executive legislates without scrutiny | State permitted subjects, procedure, publication, consultation, laying, and judicial review. |
| L007 | Secret/unpublished order | Secret law and lack of notice | Require publication, accessible notice, reasons, register, and limited confidentiality review. |
| L008 | Emergency/temporary power without expiry | Permanent exceptional power | Objective trigger, short expiry, legislative renewal, court review, and sunset. |
| L009 | Coercive action without process | Arbitrary deprivation | Notice, hearing, warrant/independent authorisation, reasons, appeal, and remedy. |
| L010 | Vague offence/penalty | Chilling effect/selective enforcement | Clear elements, mental state, defences, and proportionality. |
| L011 | Missing equality/access safeguards | Indirect discrimination/exclusion | Impact assessment, accessible procedure, language/disability support, data, and correction. |
| L012 | Missing conflict/audit/accountability | Capture/corruption | Disclosure, independent audit, complaint route, sanctions, and public report. |
| L013 | Anti-evasion gap | Indirect circumvention | Apply safeguards to delegates, contractors, algorithms, funds, data sharing, and successive temporary acts. |
| L014 | Missing implementation/funding/review | Paper right or unworkable duty | Resources, agency responsibility, timeline, metrics, public report, and statutory review. |
| L015 | Overbroad secrecy/data power | Surveillance/censorship and opacity | Necessity/proportionality, minimisation, retention, authorisation, log, notice, and independent audit. |
